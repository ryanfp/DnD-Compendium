## 🧠 Cipher: Combined Design Board

### 🧭 **Core Identity**

- Psychic assassin-warrior who conjures a **mind-forged blade** through sheer will
    
- Gains power from reading and manipulating the **minds and emotions of others**
    
- Uses **psionics**, not spells; integrates with **KibblesTasty’s psi point + disciplines system**
    
- Balanced between **Dexterity (agility)** and **Charisma (force of will)**
    
- Blends **Rogue/Cipher** stealth and precision with **Jedi/Samurai** spiritual discipline
    
- Subclasses iterate on **either the blade** or **mental manipulation**

---

## 🗡️ Core Flavor Pillars

- **Spectral Weapon Mastery** — The soulblade is summoned as an extension of identity
    
- **Predator of the Fractured Mind** — You punish, finish, and read enemies suffering mental instability
    
- **Discipline vs. Chaos** — Your psychic stillness contrasts with the minds you unravel
    
- **Code of the Blade** — A personal ideal or discipline informs your techniques and demeanor
    
- **Assassin of the Psyche** — You strike at intentions, break focus, and destabilize control
    
- **Ghostblade Presence** — You move with unnatural calm, like a psionic ghost

---

## 🔮 Psionic Weapon Features (Blade = Core Class)

- **Soulblade Manifestation**: Summon your weapon as a bonus action; counts as magical; finesse/light/versatile scaling options
    
- **Form Style Choices**: Blade can take different mechanical styles (dueling, reach, parrying)
    
- **Psi-Powered Strikes**: Channel psi points through the blade to inflict conditions or bonus damage (fear, silence, stagger)
    
- **Execution Triggers**: Gain benefits or bonus effects when attacking targets under mental conditions
    
- **Weapon Evolves Over Time**: Add new features/forms/upgrades at certain levels (change type, unlock ranged throw, psychic echoes)

---

## 🧠 Mental Interference Features (Whispers = Subclass Focus)

- **Tempting Whisper**: Psionic action to inflict mental conditions (Charmed, Frightened, etc.)
    
    - _Doubt_, _Desire_, _Guilt_ variants with different saves and rider effects
        
- **Echo of Doubt**: Force saves on movement or spellcasting to induce hesitation
    
- **Synaptic Severance**: Enemies with mental conditions suffer additional debuffs (reduced movement, no reactions, delayed actions)
    
- **Instinct Override**: Once mentally influenced, enemies may act unpredictably or even at your command
    
- **Surface Thought Extraction**: Read glimpses of thoughts or emotional states on hit (Insight bonuses, social manipulation hooks)
    
- **Mental Marking**: Apply a psionic “tag” — track creature, gain bonus vs saves, auto-detect lies, etc.

---

## 💥 Execution Triggers / Conditional Combat Features

- **Predator of the Fractured Mind**: Gain benefits when attacking or engaging mentally compromised targets:
    
    - Bonus psychic damage
        
    - Force movement / silence / mindlock (no reactions)
        
    - Get a free reaction when they fail a save
        
- **Execution Techniques**: Learn tiered reactions/bonus effects when exploiting a mind-debuffed target
    
- **Mindlock Strikes**: Your blade can _suppress reactions_, _impose disadvantage_, or _interrupt concentration_

---

## 🌀 Mobility / Flow Features

- **Disciplined Flow** (choose a stance or passive at rest):
    
    - **Flowing River** – Disengage on hit
        
    - **Still Mind** – +WIS or CHA to initiative, cannot be surprised
        
    - **Steel Veil** – Gain temp HP when enemies fail saves near you
        
- **Blink Step / Mind Step** – Short-range teleport to a target you’ve affected mentally
    
- **Momentum of Will** – Gain movement speed or defensive bonus after using psionics

---

## 🧬 Roleplay & Narrative Ribbons

- **Echo Reading** – After downing a mentally afflicted creature, read a surface memory or emotional fragment
    
- **Code of the Blade** – Each subclass includes a discipline or moral path (e.g., Tranquil, Wrathful, Reflective, Resisting)
    
- **Aura of Intention** – People can feel your mind's pressure, even when you’re silent

---

## 🌌 Capstone/High-Level Ideas

- **Avatar of the Willblade** – Transform your blade into its full psychic form for 1 minute:
    
    - Auto-crit vs mentally afflicted targets
        
    - Teleport on hit
        
    - Psychic backlash triggers on their saves/failures
        
- **Mental Chain Reaction** – Debuffs spread like echoes to nearby enemies
    
- **True Whisper** – Once per long rest, deliver a single phrase that causes confusion, fear, or compulsion in multiple creatures (no spell, just psionics)

## 🧠 Step 1: What's the Soulblade _Really_ About?

You’ve got two tightly linked themes:

### 🗡️ **Soulblade**

- Core combat loop
    
- Visual icon (soulbound weapon = lightsaber)
    
- Medium-range martial engine (Dexterity-based, finesse weapon, psionic powers through it)
    
- Offers customization and flow — versatile weapon, enhancement effects, psionic smite analogues

### 🗣️ **Mental Disruption / Tempting Whispers**

- Psionic powers that debuff, distract, or manipulate enemies
    
- Core to the class's advantage engine: you get benefits when enemies are _mentally destabilized_
    
- Key to creating _soft control_ and tactical execution opportunities

---

## 🧩 Option A: **Soulblade = Core Class / Mental Manipulation = Subclass**

### 🔹 Pros

- Keeps the blade as a _universal core fantasy_ — simple to grasp: “I summon and fight with my mind blade.”
    
- Subclasses can flavor the **"why/what"** behind the blade:
    
    - Blade of Serenity (pacifist duelist who disables with whispers)
        
    - Blade of Ruin (Jedi-gone-dark, uses wrath, fear)
        
    - Blade of Echoes (extracts memory, deals psychic DoT)

### 🔹 Subclass Identity

Each subclass modifies the **Mental Discipline** style:

- Unique Whisper powers
    
- Bonus Psionic Techniques focused on different mental states
    
- Blade interaction riders (e.g., fear amplifies crit chance)

> Think: Blade is your tool — subclass determines _what you do with it_.

---

## 🧩 Option B: **Mental Disruption = Core Class / Soulblade = Subclass**

### 🔹 Pros

- Shifts focus to the **mind-game** — illusions, manipulation, debuffs
    
- Subclasses define **how you project that force**:
    
    - One uses a blade (duelist style)
        
    - One uses telekinetic chains (controller)
        
    - One uses echoes of the self (clone/afterimage combat)

### 🔹 Drawback

- You risk marginalizing the blade — which you clearly _don’t_ want to do.
    
- Players might expect more _magic-y_ or ranged versions

---

## 🧩 Option C: **Both Are Class Features — Blade + Disruption**

(Eldritch Blast + Invocations model)

### 🔹 Blade = Always-on, Identity-focused Weapon

- Attacks scale with level
    
- Psi-channeling engine
    
- Thematic and aesthetic core

### 🔹 Whispers = Modular Power Suite

- Choose a “Psionic Technique” (like invocations) that inflict or exploit mental conditions
    
- Class provides baseline ones; subclasses expand or specialize them

### 🔹 Subclass = Emotional Philosophy

- Instead of weapon OR power, subclass defines _how you feel and channel psionics_
    
    - **Wrath** – Fear & pain
        
    - **Serenity** – Calm & clarity
        
    - **Guilt** – Control & justice
        
    - **Desire** – Charm & manipulation

These give:

- Aura-like passive
    
- Whisper enhancements
    
- Bonus reaction or blade strike mechanic

---

## ✅ Recommended: **Option C – Dual Pillar Core Class / Emotional Path Subclass**

Why this wins:

- **Blade** is the signature fantasy; every subclass should wield it
    
- **Disruption** defines your tactical loop (soft control, mind games)
    
- **Subclass** adds **emotional weight and specialty**, creating flavor + mechanical identity

|---|---|---|

|**Mental Disruption (Core)**|Tactical loop, advantage engine|You destabilize minds, then capitalize|

# 🧠 Class Mockup: **Soulblade**

_Psionic Striker / Mind Disruptor (DEX + CHA, Psionics Only)_

**Core Pillars (Class-Wide):**

- **Soulblade** – A psionically conjured, identity-bound weapon (always on)
    
- **Mental Disruption** – Whispers, interference, and mind destabilization
    
- **Execution Loop** – You are strongest against mentally compromised enemies
    
- **Psionic Discipline** – Integrates cleanly with KibblesTasty’s psi system

**Subclass Pillar:**

- **Emotional / Philosophical Path** – How you _apply_ pressure: fear, serenity, desire, guilt, etc.

## 🧠 Design Verdict (Big Picture)

- ✅ **Soulblade + Mental Disruption are both core**, like Eldritch Blast + Invocations
    
- ✅ Subclasses express _emotion, philosophy, and pressure style_
    
- ✅ Blade = consistent combat fantasy
    
- ✅ Whispers = tactical and narrative flexibility
    
- ✅ Strong room for modular psi techniques, disciplines, and feats

---

## 🔧 Design Suggestions to Reinforce Uniqueness

### 🔹 For the Soulblade

- **Avoid strong AOE debuffs** — keep it _surgical_, not suppressive
    
- Focus on **1v1 duelist pressure**, exploiting isolated or vulnerable minds
    
- Make **blade evolution + whisper interaction** your key identity (like Eldritch Blast + Invocations for Warlocks)
    
- Reinforce **emotional path subclasses** — Wrath, Serenity, Guilt — to deepen narrative distinction

### 🔹 For the Null

- Lean further into **"anti-energy martial"** as planned (like an inverse Monk/Spellbreaker)
    
- Null Fields should always feel **environmental** and **anti-flow** (vs Soulblade’s flow-focused gameplay)
    
- Unarmed strikes + zone control = perfect fit — keep weaponless identity strong

### 🔹 For the Aegis

- Focus remains clear: **Constructs, barriers, protection mechanics**
    
- Continue offering **party-level utility** where Soulblade and Null are more solo-operant
    
- Resolve system (e.g., _Shard, Surge, Step_) offers strong subclass modularity — keep that up
