# Shadar-kai
*Source: Dark Arts Player's Companion p. 6*  
![](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/races/img/shadar-kai.png#right)

- **Ability Scores**: Dexterity +2; Apply +1 to one of Intelligence or Charisma.
- **Size**: Medium
- **Speed**: 30 ft.

## Traits

### Age

The Shadar-kai reach physical maturity at the same rate as humans, but they generally live far longer. A shadar-kai may live up to 800 years.

### Alignment

Shadar-kai society is often seen as cruel and apathetic. The shadar-kai generally strive to create chaos and death, bringing death for both purposes of evil as well as good.

### Size

Shadar-kai are generally a tad below 6 feet in height and they weigh between 110 and 170 pounds. Your size is Medium.

### Speed

Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

### Superior Darkvision

Accustomed to the black skies and eternal shadow of the Shadowfell, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 120 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of grey.

### Cold of Heart

The chilling air of the shadowfell has made your body accustomed to the bitter cold. You are natural adapted to cold climates, as described in "chapter 5" of the Dungeon Master's Guide.

### Doomspeaker

You gain proficiency in the Intimidation skill.

### Fatespinner

When you fail a death saving throw, you can choose to reroll it and use the new roll. After you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Shadow Jaunt

On your turn, when you reduce a creature to 0 hit points, you can use your bonus action to teleport up to 15 feet.

### Languages

You can speak, read, and write Common and one other language of your choice.
