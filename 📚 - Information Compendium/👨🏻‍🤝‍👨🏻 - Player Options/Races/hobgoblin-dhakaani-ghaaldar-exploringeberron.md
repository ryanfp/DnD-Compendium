# Hobgoblin (Dhakaani Ghaal'dar)
*Source: Exploring Eberron p. 210*  


- **Ability Scores**: Constitution +2
- **Size**: Medium
- **Speed**: 30 ft.

## Traits

### Age

Ghaal'dar mature at the same rate as humans and have lifespans similar to theirs.

### Size

Ghaal'dar are between 5 and 6 feet tall and weigh between 150 and 200 pounds. Your size is Medium.

### Speed

Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

### Darkvision

You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray

### Discipline

You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed.

### Languages

You can speak, read, and write Common and Goblin.

## Description

Dhakaani hobgoblins are known as *ghaal'dar*, "mighty folk"--not to be confused with the Ghaal'dar tribes that dominate Darguun. The ghaal'dar are soldiers, scholars, and leaders, valued for their versatility and their vision.
