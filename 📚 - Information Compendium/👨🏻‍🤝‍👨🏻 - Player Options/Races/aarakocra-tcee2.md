# Aarakocra
*Source: Tasha's Crucible of Everything Else - Volume 2*  


- **Ability Scores**: Dexterity +2
- **Size**: Medium
- **Speed**: walk: 25 ft.

## Traits

### Age

Aarakocra reach maturity by age 3. Compared to humans, aarakocra don't usually live longer than 30 years.

### Size

Most mature Aarakocra are about 5 feet tall. They have thin, lightweight bodies that weigh between 80 and 100 pounds. Your size is Medium.

### Languages

You can speak, read, and write Common and Aarakocra.
