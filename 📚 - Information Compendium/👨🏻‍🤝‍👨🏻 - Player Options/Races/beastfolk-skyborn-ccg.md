# Beastfolk (Skyborn)
*Source: Crystalpunk Campaign Guide p. 27*  


- **Ability Scores**: Dexterity +2
- **Size**: Small or Medium
- **Speed**: walk: 30 ft.; fly: 30 ft.

## Traits

### Age

Beastfolk often reach maturity around age 15 and rarely live longer than 50 to 60 years.

### Size

Your size is Medium or Small (your choice upon creating a Beastfolk character).

### Bestial Talent

You gain one of the following options of your choice: (a) darkvision with a range of 60 feet or (b) a natural weapon on an appropriate part of your body, which you can use to make unarmed strikes. It deals damage equal to `d6` + your Strength modifier, and the damage type is bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing, as appropriate.

### Primal Battlecry

As a bonus action, you unleash a mystical howl, roar, or powerful cry which invigorates up to four allies of your choice you can see within 30 feet of you, which can include yourself. The next time those allies move on their turn, they can move up to 10 feet further. Also, the next time they deal damage with an attack or spell, it does extra damage equal to your level. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Armor Specialist

You have proficiency with both light and medium armor.

### Wounded Fury

After you take damage, you can use your reaction to regain hit points equal to `d6` + your proficiency bonus. You also gain an additional benefit that depends on your battleborn subrace. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Languages

You can speak, read, and write Common and Wild.

### Limited Flight

You have a flying speed of 30 feet. To use this speed, you can't be wearing heavy armor. Also, you must land at the end of your turn, or you fall.

### Glide

When you fall and aren't incapacitated, you can subtract up to 100 feet from the fall when calculating falling damage, and you can move up to 2 feet horizontally for every 1 foot you descend.

## Description

The skyborn are beastfolk originating from birds and flying insects.
