# Battleborn (Scout)
*Source: Crystalpunk Campaign Guide p. 25*  


- **Ability Scores**: Strength +1; Dexterity +1; Constitution +1
- **Size**: Medium
- **Speed**: walk: 35 ft.

## Traits

### Age

Battleborn were made to mature quickly, live in perfect health, and then rapidly degrade at the end of their lifespan. They reach adulthood at age 5 and rarely live to be more than 40 years old.

### Alchemy Adept

You have proficiency in [alchemist's supplies](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/items/alchemists-supplies-ccg.md).

### Martial Prodigy

You are proficient in three martial weapons of your choice.

### Armor Specialist

You have proficiency with both light and medium armor.

### Wounded Fury

After you take damage, you can use your reaction to regain hit points equal to `d6` + your proficiency bonus. You also gain an additional benefit that depends on your battleborn subrace. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Languages

You can speak, read, and write Common and Imperial.

### Scout's Swiftness

Your base walking speed increases to 35 feet.

### Darkvision

You have superior vision in dark conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

### Wounded Escape

When you use your Wounded Fury, you can move up to half your speed without provoking opportunity attacks, as a part of the same reaction.

## Description

Having a more slender form, scouts lack the destructive strength of the other battleborn, but they make up for it with their exceptional swiftness and specially enhanced vision.
