# Arsenal of Flame
*7th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You cause an array of flaming, spectral weaponry to spark into existence, and begin to assault your foes. Choose up to six creatures you can see within range. For each creature you target, you conjure a flaming weapon in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the target. Each weapon then makes a melee spell attack against its target using your spell attack modifier to hit, dealing `6d6` fire damage on a hit.

At the start of each of your turns, each weapon attempts to move up to 30 feet closer to its target if the target is outside of its 5-foot reach, is within 120 feet of you, and is seen by you. If the weapon reaches its target, it repeats its attack against it. Also, if you see a weapon's target leave the weapon's 5-foot reach, you can use your reaction to have the weapon make an opportunity attack against the target, following the normal rules for opportunity attacks.

If a weapon's target dies, that weapon dissipates into ash.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 8th level or higher, you can target two additional creatures for each slot level above 7th.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 40*
