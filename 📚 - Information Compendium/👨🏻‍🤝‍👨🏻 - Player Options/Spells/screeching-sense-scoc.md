# Screeching Sense
*1st-level, Divination*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You emit a high pitched screech that travels out 120 ft. At the beginning of your next turn, the sound you released returns in a black and white image of the local layout. This image does not show information behind walls, and is blurred around corners beyond 10ft. Your passive perception for this image is 10 + your spell casting modifier.
