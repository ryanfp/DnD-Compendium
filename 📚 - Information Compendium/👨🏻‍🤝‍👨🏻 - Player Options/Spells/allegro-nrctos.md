# Allegro
*2nd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (20-foot Radius)
- **Components:** V, S, M (a tail feather from a bird of prey)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Converted from:** *Spell Compendium*

You and up to eight allies within 20 feet of you have their walking speed doubled for the duration of this spell.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 68*
