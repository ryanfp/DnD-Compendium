# Elminster's Evoked Delivery
*3rd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Unlimited
- **Components:** S, M (a small bit of sealing wax, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You instantaneously shunt an object through the fabric of space to a willing creature with which you are familiar. The object can be no larger than 1 foot on a side and weigh no more than 10 pounds. When you cast the spell, the recipient is magically informed what the object is. If the creature is willing, the object appears in empty space within 5 feet of the creature (the creature's choice). If the creature is on another plane of existence, is incapacitated, is proofed against magical detection or location, or is unwilling to receive the object, the spell fails.

When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the maximum dimensions increase by both 1 foot on each side and 10 pounds for each slot level above 3rd.

*Source: Tasha's Crucible of Everything Else - Volume 1 p. 62*
