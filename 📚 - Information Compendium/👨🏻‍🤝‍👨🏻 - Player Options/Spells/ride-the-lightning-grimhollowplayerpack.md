# Ride the Lightning
*4th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (60-foot Line)
- **Components:** V, S, M (a length of copper wire)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You transform yourself into a bolt of lightning and teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. Each creature within 5 feet of the line created between your starting point and final destination takes `4d6` lightning damage unless they succeed on a Dexterity saving throw to halve the damage.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the damage increases by `d6` for each slot level above 4th. In addition, you can teleport yourself an additional 10 feet further for each slot level above 4th.

*Source: Grim Hollow: Player Pack*
