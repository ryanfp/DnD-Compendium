# Peppermint Plate
*1st-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (well-chewed peppermint chewing gum)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

A protective layer of hot, hard peppermint icing coats you For the duration, you gain a +1 bonus to AC and if a creature hits you with a melee attack, the creature takes `d4` fire damage. This damage can trigger no more than once per turn

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the fire damage increases by `d4` for each slot level above 1st.
