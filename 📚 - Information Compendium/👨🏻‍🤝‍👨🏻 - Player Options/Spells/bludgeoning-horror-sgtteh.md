# Bludgeoning Horror
*2nd-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 15 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (the body part of a Great One)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You summon a large number of tentacles and slugs from your palm to strike a creature within range. Make a melee spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes `3d6` bludgeoning damage. If you score a critical hit, the target is also stunned until the start of your next turn.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by `d6` and range increases by 5 feet for each slot level above 2nd.

*Source: Steinhardt's Guide to the Eldritch Hunt p. 191*
