# Dust of Death
*6th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of dust)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You turn a small pinch of dust into a deadly poison. If this poison is ingested (such as if you slip it into their food or drink), the victim must make a Constitution saving throw. The victim takes `10d8` poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If this reduces its hit points to 0, it dies.

This poison cannot be detected by non-magical means. Victims of this spell are left with a residue of dust around their mouths.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 43*
