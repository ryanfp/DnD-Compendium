# Wall of Mist
*2nd-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a vial of water)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You create a wall of dense fog. You can make the wall up to 60 feet long, 10 feet high, and 5 feet thick. The wall is opaque, and its area is heavily obscured. It lasts for the duration or until a wind of moderate or greater speed (at least 10 miles per hour) disperses it.

*Source: Tasha's Crucible of Everything Else - Volume 1 p. 65*
