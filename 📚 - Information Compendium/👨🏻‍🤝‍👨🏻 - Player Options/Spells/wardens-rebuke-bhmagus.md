# Warden's Rebuke
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 5 feet
- **Components:** V, M (a melee weapon)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

As part of the action used to cast this spell, you must make a melee attack with a weapon against one creature within the spell's range, otherwise the spell fails. On a hit, the target suffers the attack's normal effects, and the earth quakes threateningly beneath its feet until the start of your next turn. If the target willingly attacks a creature other than you before then, it immediately takes `d6` bludgeoning damage as the earth rises up to rebuke it, ending the spell.

This spell's damage increases when you reach higher levels. At 5th level, the melee attack deals an extra `d6` damage to the target, and the bludgeoning damage the target takes for attacking another creature increases to `2d6`. Both damage rolls increase by `d6` at 11th level (`2d6 / 3d6`) and 17th level (`3d6 / 4d6`).
