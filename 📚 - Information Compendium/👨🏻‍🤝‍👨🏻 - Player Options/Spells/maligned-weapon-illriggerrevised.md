# Maligned Weapon
*4th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You imbue a weapon you touch with an infernal blessing. Until the spell ends, the weapon extinguishes any mundane sources of light in a 30-foot radius. In addition, attacks made with the weapon deal an extra `2d6` necrotic damage on a hit. If the weapon isn't already a magic weapon, it becomes one for the duration.

As a bonus action on your turn while holding this weapon, you can end the spell early and cause the weapon to emit a burst of dark energy. Each creature of your choice who you can see within 30 feet of you must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes `4d6` necrotic damage and is [frightened](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Frightened) for 1 minute. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't [frightened](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Frightened). At the end of each of their turns, a [frightened](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Frightened) creature can make a Wisdom saving throw, ending the effect on themself on a success.

**Classes**: [Paladin](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-paladin.md); [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md)

*Source: The Illrigger Revised p. 33*
