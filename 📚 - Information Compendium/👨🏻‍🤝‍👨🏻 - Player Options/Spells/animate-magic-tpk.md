# Animate Magic
*9th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 8 hours

A magical effect is transformed into a being of living magic, an animate spell. Choose a spell effect of 6th level or higher within range. That spell ends, and an animate spell is formed in a space of your choice within range of the spell. The animate spell is of a school according to the effect you targeted. For example, if you targeted an evocation spell with this spell, you would create a [living evocation](/3-Content/Compendiums/bestiary/construct/living-evocation-tpk.md). You cannot command the animate spell, which is an independent creature beyond your control, though it is initially friendly to you and your allies.

**Classes**: [Wizard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-wizard.md); [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Sorcerer](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-sorcerer.md)
