# Death Armor
*2nd-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (paste made from ground bones)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

After casting this spell, a shroud of dark flames wreathes your body. For the spell's duration, any creature within 5 feet of you that touches or strikes you with an unarmed or melee weapon attack takes an amount of necrotic damage equal to `d4` + your spellcasting ability modifier.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the amount of necrotic damage increases by `d4` for each slot level above 1st.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 39*
