# Psychic Drain
*2nd-level, None*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You draw on the psychic energy of another creature you can see to sustain yourself. The target must make a Charisma saving throw. On failure it takes `3d8` psychic damage and you gain temporary hit points equal to half the amount of psychic damage dealt and regain one expended psi point.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the spell deals an extra `d8` psychic damage.

**Classes**: [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Psion](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-psion.md)
