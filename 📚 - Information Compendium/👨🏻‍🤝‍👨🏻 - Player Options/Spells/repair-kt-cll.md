# Repair
*4th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a construct or inanimate object, causing it to regain `10d6` hit points. Any parts or material that has broken off is reattached, restoring it to the condition it was in before losing those hit points.

If the target's damage is the result of aging, you can instead restore it to its condition as of 10d6 years ago. Its condition can only improve — if it was in worse condition previously, the spell has no effect.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the hit points restored or years of decay undone increase by `2d6` for each slot above 4th.
