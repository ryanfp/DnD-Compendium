# Phantasmal Theatre
*4th-level, Illusion*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Special

This spell creates a carefully scripted illusory performance. You target an area within range that fits within a 20-foot cube. As you cast the spell, you decide the content of the illusion. It can include any number of images, sounds, smells, and even non-harmful temperature changes that originate within the area and potentially move around within it. There are no limitations on what can appear, and there can be scene changes, speaking characters, sound effects, background music, and other such things. The performance starts as soon as the spell is cast.

The duration of this spell's performance is equal to the time you spend casting it, with a minimum duration of 6 seconds when you cast the spell using 1 action.

Physical interaction with the performance reveals it to be an illusion, because things can pass through it. A creature that uses its action to examine the performance can determine that it is an illusion with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC. If a creature discerns the illusion for what it is, the creature can see through the illusion, and any noise it makes sounds hollow to the creature.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the size of the cube increases by 10 feet for each slot level above 4th.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 53*
