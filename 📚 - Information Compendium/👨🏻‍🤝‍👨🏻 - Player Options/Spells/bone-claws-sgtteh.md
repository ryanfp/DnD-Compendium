# Bone claws
*1st-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a tiger's claw)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose a creature you can see within range. You infuse them with osteomantic energy, growing sharp claws from their carpal bones. For the duration, the target's unarmed attacks deal an extra `d4` slashing damage on a hit.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases to `d6` at 2nd level, `2d4` at 3rd level, `2d6` at 4th level, and `3d4` at 5th level.

*Source: Steinhardt's Guide to the Eldritch Hunt p. 188*
