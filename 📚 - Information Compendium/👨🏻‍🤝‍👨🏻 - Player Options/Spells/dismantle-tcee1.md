# Dismantle
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a miniature hammer)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

This spell breaks or tears a nonmagical object worth up to 10gp. The break or tear can be up to 1 foot in any dimension.

Make a melee spell attack roll to cast this spell on a construct. On a hit, this spell deals `2d6` force damage. This spell's damage increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level (`3d6`), 11th level (`4d6`), and 17th level (`5d6`).

*Source: Tasha's Crucible of Everything Else - Volume 1 p. 62*
