# Judgment
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round

Your hand momentarily flashes with light as you try to touch a creature. Make a melee spell attack against the target. You have advantage on the attack roll if the target attacked a creature other than you on its last turn. On a hit, the target takes `d6` radiant damage, and it begins to shimmer faintly until the start of your next turn. If the target attacks a creature other than you before then, it immediately takes `d6` radiant damage, its attack gains disadvantage, and the spell ends.

Both rolls of this spell's damage increase by `d6` when you reach 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`)

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 49*
