# Soul Bind
*3rd-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You strengthen yourself with powerful spiritual energy. The first time you deal force, radiant, or necrotic damage to a creature during this spell's duration, you bind the soul of one creature you damaged, affecting the target in one of the following ways of your choice:

- The target takes an extra `3d6` damage and must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the spell ends.  
- The target takes an extra `2d6` damage and must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be restrained until the spell ends.  
- The target takes an extra `d6` damage and must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be stunned until the spell ends.  

Regardless of the effect you choose, an affected target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the extra damage dealt increases by `d6` for each slot level above 3rd.
