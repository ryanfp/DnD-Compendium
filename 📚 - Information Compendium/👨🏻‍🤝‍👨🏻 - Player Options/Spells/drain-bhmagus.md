# Drain
*1st-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a red ribbon)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Stretching out your hand and pointing your finger at a creature within range, a flash of negative energy briefly connects the two of you. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take `3d4` necrotic damage. If the target fails its saving throw, you gain a number of temporary hit points equivalent to the damage dealt. You lose any temporary hit points granted by this spell after one minute.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, each level beyond the first adds `2d4` to the damage dealt by this spell.

**Classes**: [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Magus](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-magus.md)
