# Alpha's Shadowfire
*4th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a black opal worth at least 100 gp)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Converted from:** The Great Net Spellbook

A streak of black and green flame erupts from your hands and strikes a point you choose within range. Make a ranged spell attack to hit. On a success, your target takes `3d10` fire damage plus `3d10` necrotic damage.

**At Higher Levels.** For each spell slot of 5th level or higher, you inflict an additional `d10` fire or `d10` necrotic damage (your choice) per slot level above 4th.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 71*
