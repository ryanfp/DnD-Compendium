# Alpha's Spark Shower
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (15-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of fur, glass, and copper)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Converted from:** The Great Net Spellbook

You extend your arms and a sheet of sizzling purple sparks shoot forth from your hands. Each creature in a 15-foot cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes `d6` lightning damage on a failed saving throw, or half as much on a successful one. A creature wearing metal armor has disadvantage on this roll.

This spell does additional damage when you reach 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`).

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 71*
