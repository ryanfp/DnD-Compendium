# Spectral Barrage
*2nd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (60-foot Line)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A devastating blast of spiritual energy erupts from your soul in a direction of your choice, forming a line 60 feet long and 5 feet wide. Each creature in the line must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes `2d12` force, radiant, or necrotic damage (your choice) and is pushed 10 feet away from you. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't pushed.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by `d12` for each slot level above 2nd.
