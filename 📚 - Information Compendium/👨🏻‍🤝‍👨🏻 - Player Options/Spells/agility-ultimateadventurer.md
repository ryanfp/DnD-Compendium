# Agility
*8th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** 
- **Duration:** 1 hour

Until the spell ends, when you make a Dexterity check, you can replace the number you roll with a 15. Additionally, moving through any form of difficult terrain<sup><span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">'24</span></sup> costs you no extra movement.

*Source: The Ultimate Adventurer's Handbook p. 220*
