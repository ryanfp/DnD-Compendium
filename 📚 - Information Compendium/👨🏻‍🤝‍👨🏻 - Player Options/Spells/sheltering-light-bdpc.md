# Sheltering Light
*6th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 10 minutes

A warm light shines down on a location you specify within range. Until the spell ends, a 30-foot-radius, 40-foot-high cylinder of bright light glimmers there. This light is sunlight.

When a friendly creature within the area starts its turn with 0 hit points, this spell causes it to regain 15 hit points.

The spell ends once it has restored a total of 60 hit points. It ends early if you fall unconscious.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the total hit points that this spell can restore before it ends is increased by 15 for each slot level above 6th.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 54*
