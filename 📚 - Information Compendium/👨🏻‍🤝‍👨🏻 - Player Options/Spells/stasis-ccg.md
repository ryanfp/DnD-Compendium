# Stasis
*2nd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a small chunk of iron worth at least 50 gp, which the spell consumes if cast over 1 hour)
- **Duration:** 1 round, up to 1 year

This spell locks a target in stasis, preventing it from moving. There are two possible uses for this spell—granting either immediate or long-term benefits.

If you cast this spell using 1 action, choose a creature or object within range that is large or smaller. If you target a creature or an object held or worn by one, they must succeed on a Strength saving throw to resist the spell. On a failure, a target can't move until the end of your next turn, even if it is defying gravity. If the target is a creature, it is incapacitated until then. Any momentum the target had is maintained while in the spell, and while in stasis, all forces, except gravity, are applied to the target as momentum. When the spell ends, the target is flung in an appropriate direction based on the attacks that hit it and the forces applied to it during the spell. For each attack hit, a creature is flung 10 feet in a straight line. If you cast the spell this way, you can end the spell at any time (no action required from you).

If you cast this spell over 1 hour, choose an object within range that is large or smaller. The object becomes magically fixed in place for 1 year. It doesn't move, even if it is defying gravity. The object can hold up to 8,000 pounds of weight—if it holds more, the forces applying to it, such as gravity, begin to apply as normal.

When using either use of this spell, the stasis is relative to the center of the planet—applying the spell to a target within a moving object will cause it to stop in place for the duration, rather than stop above that moving object.
