# Freeze
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You instill a deadly chill into your hand. Make a melee spell attack against the target. On hit, the target takes `d8` cold damage, and the target's movement speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of their turn.

The spell's damage increases by `d8` when you reach 5th level (`2d8`), 11th level (`3d8`), and 17th level (`4d8`).

**Classes**: [Occultist](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md); [Spellblade](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-spellblade.md)

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 232*
