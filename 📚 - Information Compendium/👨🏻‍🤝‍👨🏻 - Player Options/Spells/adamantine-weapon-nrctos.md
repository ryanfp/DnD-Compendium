# Adamantine Weapon
*2nd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of diamond powder)
- **Duration:** 1 minute

**Converted from:** *Priest's Spell Compendium*

You transform your own cudgel, mace, staff, or other bludgeoning weapon into one made of pure elemental adamantine. It does an additional `d12` bludgeoning damage on a successful hit.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 65*
