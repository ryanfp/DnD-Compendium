# Kiss of Vampirism
*1st-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** 1 round

**Converted from:** The Great Net Spellbook

Your kiss drains the life out of your target. You target takes `d10` necrotic damage and you regain hit points equal to half the amount of necrotic damage dealt.

You must be able to kiss the target's skin (although you do not need to kiss its lips). If the creature is in combat, you must succeed at a melee spell attack to kiss it; if not in combat, the creature must be willing to be kissed, restrained, or unaware.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell with a 2nd-level or higher spell slot, your kiss causes an additional `d10` necrotic damage for each slot level above 1st.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 238*
