# Grip of the Dead
*1st-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You channel unholy strength into your hand as you attempt to grab a creature you can reach. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or become restrained by your deathly grasp. As an action on its turn, it can attempt to escape using a Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check against your Spell Save DC.

At the start of the creature's turn while you maintain the grip and the spell, it takes `1d8 necrotic` damage. You regain hit points equal to half the damage dealt.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by `d8` for each slot level above 1st.
