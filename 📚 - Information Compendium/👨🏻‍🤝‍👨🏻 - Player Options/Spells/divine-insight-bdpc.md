# Divine Insight
*7th-level, Divination (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 minute unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (incense, a drop of your blood, and a vial of holy or unholy water)
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You contact your deity or a divine proxy, and ask for a reminder, advice, or an omen. These three options are described below:

## Reminder

You are given information regarding the past. The divine being you are put in contact with reminds you of the most important piece of information that it thinks you may have forgotten or overlooked.

## Advice

You are given information regarding the present. The divine being you are put in contact with gives you advice on how to best complete your current task.

## Omen

You are given information regarding the future. The divine being you are put in contact with tells you about a significant event that is likely to occur in the near future.

Regardless of the option you choose, the answer will consist of at most a few sentences.

When you choose one of these options, the divine being you are put in contact with could ask you to choose a different option, as the being might not have any information to offer you of the type you sought.

If you cast the spell two or more times before finishing your next long rest, there is a cumulative 25 percent chance for each casting after the first that you get no answer.
