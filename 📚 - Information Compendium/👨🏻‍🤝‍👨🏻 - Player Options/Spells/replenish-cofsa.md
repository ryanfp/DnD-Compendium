# Replenish
*2nd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

> [!quote]  
> 
> With the turn of seasons, the cycle of life renews.

When you cast this spell, you touch an allied creature that you can see. The target surges with vital force, gaining `2d6` temporary hit points. These temporary hit points are lost whenever the target finishes a short or long rest. The target is also cured of the poisoned condition.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a slot of 3rd level or higher, the temporary hit points gained increases by `d6` per slot level above 2nd.

*Source: The Compendium of Forgotten Secrets: Awakening p. 173*
