# Alpha's Heat Lightning
*5th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a short glass rod, a bit of fur, and a bit or iron or lodestone)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Converted from:** The Great Net Spellbook

You call down a bolt of purplish-red lightning, which strikes a single creature you can see within range. Make a ranged spell attack to hit. On a success, that creature takes `4d10` fire damage and `4d10` lightning damage. If the creature is wearing nonmagical metallic armor or carrying a metallic weapon, the creature takes an additional `d10` lightning damage. The lightning will also set combustibles on fire.

**At Higher Levels.** If you cast this spell with a 6th-level or higher spell slot, you inflict an additional `d10` lightning damage for each slot level above 5th.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 69*
