# Fists of Fire
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You cause your fists to erupt in flames. For the duration, your fists become a set of simple natural weapons that deal `d6` fire damage. You are proficient in these weapons, and they have the Light property.

For the duration, any flammable object you attempt to hold catches fire. If you end your turn grappling another creature with your hands, it takes `d4` fire damage.

You can end the spell early (no action required).

The damage this spell does to grappled targets increases by `d4` at 5th level (`2d4`), 11th level (`3d4`), and 17th level (`4d4`)

**Classes**: [Occultist](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md)

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 229*
