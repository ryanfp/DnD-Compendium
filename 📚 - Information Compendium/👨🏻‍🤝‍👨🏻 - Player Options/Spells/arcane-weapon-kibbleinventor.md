# Arcane Weapon
*1st-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a weapon and imbue it with magic. For the duration the weapon counts as a magical weapon, any damage dealt by it is Force damage.

**Classes**: [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md)

*Source: KibbleTasty's Inventor p. 37*
