# Spell-Shattering Strike
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 5 feet
- **Components:** V, M (a melee weapon)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

As part of the action used to cast this spell, you must make a melee attack with a weapon against one creature within the spell's range, otherwise the spell fails. On a hit, the target suffers the attack's normal effects, and if the target is [concentrating](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Concentration) on a spell this attack deals an additional `d8` force damage.

This spell's damage increases when you reach higher levels. At 5th level, the melee attack deals an extra `d8` force damage to the target and the additional damage if the target is [concentrating](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Concentration) on a spell increases to `2d8`. Both damage rolls increase by `d8` at 11th (`2d8 / 3d8`) and 17th level (`3d8 / 4d8`).

**Classes**: [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Cleric (Eldritch Domain)](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-eldritch-domain-ghpg.md "subclass=GHPG")
