# Psionic Oppression
*6th-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You psionically overwhelm the mind of one creature you can see within range. The target must make an Intelligence saving throw. On a failed save, it takes `12d6` psychic damage and is [stunned](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Stunned) for 1 minute. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage and isn't [stunned](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Stunned).

The [stunned](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Stunned) target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

**Classes**: [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md)

*Source: The Psion p. 13*
