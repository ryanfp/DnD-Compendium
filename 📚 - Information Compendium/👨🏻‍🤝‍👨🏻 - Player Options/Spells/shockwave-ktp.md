# Shockwave
*1st-level, None*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Radius)
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a burst of telekinetic force in all directions. Each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you is knocked 5 feet back and must succeed on a Strength saving throw or take `8d6` damage bludgeoning damage and be knocked [prone](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Prone). A creature that succeeds on its saving throw takes half as much damage and isn't knocked [prone](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Prone).

**Classes**: [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Psion](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-psion.md)

*Source: KibblesTasty: Psion p. 33*
