# Lesser Restoration
*2nd-level, Abjuration*  
![](/3-Content/Compendiums/spells/img/lesser-restoration.webp#right)

- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature and end one condition on it: [Blinded](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Blinded), [Deafened](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Deafened), [Paralyzed](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Paralyzed), or [Poisoned](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned).
