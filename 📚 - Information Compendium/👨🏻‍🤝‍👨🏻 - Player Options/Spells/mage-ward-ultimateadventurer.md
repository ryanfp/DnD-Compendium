# Mage Ward
*cantrip, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round

You create a protective ward around yourself that strikes out at those who attack you. The first time a creature hits you with an attack before the start of your next turn, it takes `d10` force damage and the spell ends.

The spell's damage increases by `d10` when you reach 5th level (`2d10`), 11th level (`3d10`), and 17th level (`4d10`).

*Source: The Ultimate Adventurer's Handbook p. 250*
