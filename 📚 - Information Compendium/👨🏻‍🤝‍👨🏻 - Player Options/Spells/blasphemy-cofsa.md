# Blasphemy
*3rd-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (60-foot Radius)
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** 1 minute

> [!quote]  
> 
> Defy those who create life, for their life is equally false.

You curse, and will away the healing magics of nature and the gods. Each time a creature within a 60-foot radius of you would recover hit points from a spell, it must make a Charisma saving throw. If it fails, it does not recover hit points and instead takes `d6` psychic damage. You can end this spell as an action.

*Source: The Compendium of Forgotten Secrets: Awakening p. 162*
