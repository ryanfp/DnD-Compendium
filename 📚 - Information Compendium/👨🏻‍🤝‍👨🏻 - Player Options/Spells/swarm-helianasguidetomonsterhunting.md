# Swarm
*cantrip, Biomancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a spider sac)
- **Duration:** 1 round

Choose a target within range; a cloud of miniscule poisonous spiders floats on gossamer strands towards it The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw to avoid the spiders or become poisoned until the end of its next turn

This spell deals poison damage on a failed saving throw when you reach certain levels When you reach 5th level, a creature takes `d10` poison damage; at 11th level this increases to `2d10` and at 17th level this increases to `3d10`


*Source: Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting*
