# Spring
*2nd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a small forked stick, which is thrust into the ground)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

**Converted from:** *Priest's Spell Compendium*

You create a temporary freshwater spring that flows at a rate of `2d6` gallons per minute. In a desert or an area under drought conditions, it only produces `d4` gallons per minute.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 334*
