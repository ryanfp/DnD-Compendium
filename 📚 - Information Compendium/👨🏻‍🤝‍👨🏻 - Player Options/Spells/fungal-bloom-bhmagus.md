# Fungal Bloom
*cantrip, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, M (a ranged weapon)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You send an arrow towards a creature in range and, upon hitting its mark, the arrow explodes into a shower of fungal spores. As part of the action used to cast this spell, you must make a ranged attack with a weapon against one creature within the spell's range, otherwise the spell fails. On a hit, the target suffers the attack's normal effects, and a fungal bloom appears in three unoccupied spaces of your choice adjacent to the creature. These fungal blooms remain until the end of your next turn. When a creature moves through a fungal bloom, it is destroyed and the creature takes `d6` poison damage.

This spell's damage increases when you reach higher levels. At 5th level, the ranged attack deals an extra `d6` poison damage to the target, and the fungal bloom deals an additional `d6` poison damage when a creature moves through it. Both damage rolls increase by `d6` at 11th level (`2d6 / 3d6`) and 17th level (`3d6 / 4d6`).
