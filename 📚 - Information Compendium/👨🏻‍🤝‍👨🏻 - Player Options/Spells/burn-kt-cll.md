# Burn
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You ignite a brilliant flame around your hand that sears anything you touch. Make a melee spell attack against the target. On hit, the target takes `d12` fire damage.

The spell's damage increases by `d12` when you reach 5th level (`2d12`), 11th level (`3d12`), and 17th level (`4d12`).

**Classes**: [Occultist](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md)

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 217*
