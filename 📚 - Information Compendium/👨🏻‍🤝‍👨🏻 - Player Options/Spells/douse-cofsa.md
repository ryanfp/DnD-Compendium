# Douse
*cantrip, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

> [!quote]  
> 
> The ocean's spray conceals a cold and callous nature. 

You gesture, summoning a splash of saltwater to fall upon a creature or object within range. Flames are instantly quenched, and objects targeted by this spell cannot be ignited for at least one minute. If you target a creature, you can choose to have the saltwater be bone-chillingly cold. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. If they fail, they take `d8` cold damage.

This spell's damage increases by `d8` when you reach 5th level (`2d8`), 11th level (`3d8`), and 17th level (`4d8`).

*Source: The Compendium of Forgotten Secrets: Awakening p. 166*
