# Ward Against Spells
*2nd-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a handheld mirror)
- **Duration:** 1 minute

One willing creature you touch gains `4d6` temporary hit points<sup><span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">'24</span></sup>. While the creature has any temporary hit points granted by this spell, it has resistance to damage dealt by spells. The creature loses any temporary hit points it has remaining from this spell at the end of 1 minute.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the temporary hit points gained increases by `d6` for each slot level above 2nd.

*Source: The Ultimate Adventurer's Handbook p. 272*
