# Hellish Halo
*6th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (an obsidian talisman worth 100 gp)
- **Duration:** 1 minute

> [!quote]  
> 
> She shall arrive upon a chariot of steel bearing a talisman of stone, and the fire that once brought life to the world shall reap what it has sown. 

You summon a massive halo of flame that surrounds you, igniting the air around your body and infusing you with burning vitality. Whenever a creature enters a space within 10 feet of you for the first time during a turn, or starts their turn there, the target takes `5d10` fire damage. Whenever a creature takes damage from this effect, you gain temporary hit points equal to the damage dealt. You can choose to suppress or reignite this flaming halo as a bonus action.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the fire damage increases by `d10` per slot level above 6th.

*Source: The Compendium of Forgotten Secrets: Awakening p. 169*
