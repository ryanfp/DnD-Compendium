# Diamondblade
*7th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a diamond worth at least 100 gp)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Converted from:** The Great Net Spellbook

One bladed weapon you touch becomes incredibly sharp. You may then use the weapon or give it to someone else; you are considered to have proficiency with it.

The sword grants +3 to hit and damage. If you roll a 20, you inflict an additional `4d10` slashing damage and an additional `d10` damage on each of the target's next 3 turns due to excessive bleeding.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 151*
