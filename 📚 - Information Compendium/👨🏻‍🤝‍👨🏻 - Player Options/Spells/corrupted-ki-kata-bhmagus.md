# Corrupted Ki Kata
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 5 feet
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You make the hand sign of corruption and strike at a creature within range. As part of the action used to cast this spell, you must make an unarmed strike attack against one creature within the spell's range, otherwise the spell fails. On a hit, the target suffers the attack's normal effects and has its ki corrupted until the end of your next turn. If the creature has corrupted ki when you hit it with this cantrip, you deal an additional `d8` poison damage. While a creature has corrupted ki, it has disadvantage on saving throws against being [poisoned](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned).

This spell's damage increases when you reach higher levels. At 5th level, the unarmed strike deals an extra `d8` poison damage and the additional damage to creatures with corrupted ki increases to `2d8`. Both of these damages increases by `d8` again at 11th level (`2d8 / 3d8`) and 17th level (`3d8 / 4d8`).
