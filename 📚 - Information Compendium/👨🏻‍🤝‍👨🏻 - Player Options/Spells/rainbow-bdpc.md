# Rainbow
*5th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You imbue your bow or crossbow with magical energy, causing it to shimmer with scintillating colors. During the duration, when you attack with the weapon you can conjure magical ammunition for the attack, choosing among the seven colors listed below. You can conjure each of the individual colors only once per casting.

Once you have conjured all seven, the spell ends.

## Red

Hit or miss, this red-colored arrow/bolt explodes once it reaches its target. The target and each creature within 10 feet of it must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking `2d6` fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Orange

On a hit, this orange-colored arrow/bolt causes the target to take an extra `2d6` acid damage, and another `d6` acid damage at the end of its next turn.

## Yellow

On a hit, this orange-colored arrow/bolt causes the target to take an extra `2d6` radiant damage, and forces it to succeed on a Constitution saving throw or become blinded until the start of your next turn.

## Green

On a hit, this green-colored arrow/bolt causes the target to take an extra `2d8` poison damage, and forces it to succeed on a Constitution saving throw or become poisoned until the start of your next turn.

## Blue

On a hit, this blue-colored arrow/bolt causes the target to take an extra `2d4` cold damage, and forces it to make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, it is restrained by ice until the start of your next turn. On a successful save, it is not restrained but its speed is halved until the start of your next turn.

## Indigo

This indigo-colored arrow/bolt forces each creature in a 5-foot wide line between you and the target, including the target, to make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes `2d6` lightning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Violet

On a hit, this violet-colored arrow/bolt causes the target to take an extra `2d4` psychic damage, and forces it to succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become stunned until the start of your next turn.
