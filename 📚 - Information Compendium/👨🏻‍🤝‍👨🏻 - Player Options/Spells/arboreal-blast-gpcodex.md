# Arboreal Blast
*2nd-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Cone)
- **Components:** S, M (a twig and 1 buckshot, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A leaf-covered, wooden shotgun manifests itself in your hands. You may discharge the firearm by making a ranged spell attack against each target within range; firing the shot disintegrates the wooden weapon. On a hit, the targets take `4d6` piercing damage.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by `d6` for each slot level above 2nd.

*Source: The Gunpowder Codex p. 24*
