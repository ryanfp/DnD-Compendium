# Septic Shock
*5th-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a piece of a rotten liver)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a creature you can see within range that doesn't have all its hit points. You infect the target's wounds with putrefying bacteria. The target must make a Constitution saving throw.

If it has less than half its maximum hit points remaining, it makes the saving throw with disadvantage. On a failed save, the target takes `10d8` necrotic damage and gains 1 level of exhaustion. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and does not gain any exhaustion levels.
