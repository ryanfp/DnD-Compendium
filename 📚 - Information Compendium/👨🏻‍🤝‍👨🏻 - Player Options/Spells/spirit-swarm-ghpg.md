# Spirit Swarm
*5th-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You invite spirits to take their revenge upon a target. A creature that you can see within range must make a Charisma saving throw. A creature with a Charisma score of 2 or lower can't be affected by this spell. A target takes `8d8` psychic damage on a failed save and is [frightened](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Frightened) of you until the spell ends. On a successful save, a target takes half damage and is not [frightened](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Frightened).

**Classes**: [Cleric](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-cleric.md); [Wizard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-wizard.md); [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md)

*Source: Grim Hollow: The Player's Guide p. 126*
