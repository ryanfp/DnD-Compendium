# Death Shroud
*cantrip, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You make the hand sign of endings and focus your will on a creature within range. That creature gains a death shroud. When you deal damage to that creature with an attack, you can discard the death shroud you placed on the creature to deal an additional `2d4` damage.

At 5th level, the damage from your death shroud increases to `4d4`. It increases again at 11th level (`6d4`) and 17th level (`8d4`).
