# Arena of Trust
*2nd-level, Abjuration (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 minute unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of parchment and a drop of blood)
- **Duration:** 8 hours

As part of casting the spell, choose up to 6 willing creatures within a 60-foot-radius sphere centered on that point. For the duration of the spell, all creatures cannot be killed by each other while inside the arena. Instead, when a creature takes fatal damage from another affected creature, it is reduced to 0 hit points and is [unconscious](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Unconscious) for the next hour. After an hour has passed, the creature regains consciousness and all hit points lost while under the duration of the spell.
