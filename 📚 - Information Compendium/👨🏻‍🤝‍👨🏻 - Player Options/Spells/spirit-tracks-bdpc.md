# Spirit Tracks
*2nd-level, Divination*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (10-foot Radius)
- **Components:** V, S, M (true)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

Divination magic radiates from you in a 10-foot radius centered on you, moving with you. When you cast this spell, you can choose to search for specific creature types, or search for tracks made by any creature. Until the spell ends, the tracks of creatures that walked on the ground within the spell's area during the last 24 hours are highlighted. The highlighted tracks have the same appearance as the footprints of the creatures that walked there, and they are colored based on the age of the tracks. The most recent tracks are colored white, which slowly transitions to yellow and then red as the tracks get older. The coloring is distinct enough to tell the age of the tracks down to the hour.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 57*
