# Thunderburst Mine
*2nd-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 minute
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a tiny nonmagical item, which is destroyed when the spell ends)
- **Duration:** 8 hours

You set a magical trap, infusing thunderous magic into an object. You can set the item to detonate when someone comes within 5 feet of it, or at a verbal command by you. You can issue this command as an action or as a reaction when you see a creature move within 20 feet of a mine, detonating every mine keyed to the verbal command simultaneously.

When the magic trap detonates, each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on the item must make a Constitution saving throw. A creature takes `3d8` thunder damage on a fail, or half as much damage on a success. If a creature is in the area of effect of more than one thunderburst mine during a turn, it takes half damage from any mines beyond the first.

A magical mine must be set at least 5 feet away from every other mine, and cannot be moved once placed; any attempt to move it results in it detonating. The caster can permanently disarm a mine as an action.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 260*
