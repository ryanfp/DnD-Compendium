# Duskwalk
*1st-level, Divination*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 15 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

> [!quote]  
> 
> In the beginning, there was only darkness. It will return. 

Choose a location within range that is within 5 feet of a creature or in dim light or darkness. You teleport to this location. As a bonus action before or after teleporting, you can make a single melee spell attack against a target within your reach. If you hit, the target takes {dice `d6`} psychic damage.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the range increases by 5 feet and the psychic damage increases by `d6` per spell slot level above 1st. 

*Source: The Compendium of Forgotten Secrets: Awakening p. 166*
