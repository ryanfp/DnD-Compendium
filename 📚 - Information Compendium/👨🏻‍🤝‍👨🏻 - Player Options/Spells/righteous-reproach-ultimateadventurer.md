# Righteous Reproach
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 5 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a holy symbol)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You present your holy symbol, summoning divine power, and make a melee spell attack against a creature within 5 feet of you. If the target is an undead or fiend, the attack roll scores a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the `d20`. On a hit, the target takes `d8` radiant damage, and the creature has disadvantage on the first attack roll it makes against you before the start of your next turn.

The spell's damage increases by `d8` when you reach 5th level (`2d8`), 11th level (`3d8`), and 17th level (`4d8`). Once you reach 17th level, if the target is an undead or fiend, the attack roll scores a critical on a roll of 18-20 on the `d20`.

*Source: The Ultimate Adventurer's Handbook p. 260*
