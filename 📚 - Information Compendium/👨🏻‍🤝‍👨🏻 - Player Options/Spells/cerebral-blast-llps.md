# Cerebral Blast
*3rd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Your mind unleashes a mental thrust of overwhelming force in a 30-foot cone. Each creature within the area of this spell must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes `5d8` magical bludgeoning damage and is pushed 20 feet directly away from you, and falls [prone](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Prone). On a successful save, a creature takes half damage and remains in place.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th-level or higher, the magical bludgeoning damage increases by `d8` for each spell slot level above 3rd.

**Classes**: [Psion (Wilder)](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-wilder-llps.md "subclass=LLPS;class=LLPS"); [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md)
