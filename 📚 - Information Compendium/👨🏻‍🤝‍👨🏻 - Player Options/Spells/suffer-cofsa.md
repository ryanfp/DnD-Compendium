# Suffer
*2nd-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

> [!note]
> True misery is more than pain entering the mind; it is pain entering the soul.

You point at a creature within range and speak a terrible word, commanding them to suffer. The target must make a Charisma saving throw. If they fail, they take `2d8` necrotic damage and `2d8` psychic damage and they have disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity ability checks for 1 minute. If they succeed, they take half as much damage and do not have disadvantage.

**At Higher Levels.** At Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the necrotic damage or the psychic damage (your choice) increases by `d8` per slot level above 2nd.

*Source: The Compendium of Forgotten Secrets: Awakening p. 178*
