# Forest Thunder
*cantrip, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** S, M (1 cartridge, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A wooden, vine- and leaf-covered appendage grows suddenly on your arm, taking the form of a firearm. Its appearance and size are chosen by you, but its effects remain the same. Make a ranged spell attack against a target within range; firing the shot disintegrates the wooden weapon. On a hit, the target takes `d10` piercing damage.

This spell deals additional damage at higher levels: `2d10` at 5th level, `3d10` at 11th level, and `4d10` at 17th level.

*Source: The Gunpowder Codex p. 25*
