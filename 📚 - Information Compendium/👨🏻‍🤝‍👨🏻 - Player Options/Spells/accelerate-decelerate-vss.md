# Accelerate/Decelerate
*1st-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a drop of oil or a drop of molasses)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

This spell speeds up or slows down an attack the instant before it strikes, lessening or multiplying its force. 

**Accelerate.** Increase the damage the target takes by `d6` + your spellcasting modifier. 

**Decelerate.** Reduce the damage the target takes by `d6` + your spellcasting modifier (to a minimum of 0 damage). 

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the change in damage increases by `d6` for each slot level above 1st. 

*Source: Valda's Spire of Secrets p. 329*
