# Prismatic Weapon
*3rd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

A weapon you touch is infused with elemental power, becoming a magical weapon. Choose of the following damage types: acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison, or thunder. The weapon deals `d6` damage of the chosen weapon type, and if it does not already have a bonus to attack and damage, it gains a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell with a 4th- or 5th-level spell slot, the damage increases by `d6` (to `2d6`).

When you use a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the damage increases by `2d6` (to `3d6`).
