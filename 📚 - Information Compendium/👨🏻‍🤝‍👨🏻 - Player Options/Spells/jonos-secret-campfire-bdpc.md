# Jono's Secret Campfire
*1st-level, Illusion*  


- **Casting time:** 1 minute
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** S, M (a set of twigs)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You create a magical, silver-flamed campfire that fills a 5-foot cube extending from an area of ground that you can see within range. It lasts for the duration and sheds no smoke, but outside of that and its silver flames it is identical to a nonmagical fire.

For the duration, a 15-foot radius area around the fire is concealed by an illusion. When viewed from outside the area, this illusion causes the area to appear as it did before this spell was cast, thereby concealing new creatures and objects that enter the area. The area is also filled with bright light, which does not extend outside of it.

A creature that uses its action to examine the area can determine that it is concealed by an illusion with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC. Similarly, a creature that enters the area immediately discerns the illusion for what it is. If a creature discerns the illusion for what it is, the creature can see through it.

**At Higher Levels.** If you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the duration becomes 8 hours. If you use a slot of 5th level or higher, the duration becomes 10 days. If you use a slot of 7th level or higher, the spell lasts until it is dispelled.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 47*
