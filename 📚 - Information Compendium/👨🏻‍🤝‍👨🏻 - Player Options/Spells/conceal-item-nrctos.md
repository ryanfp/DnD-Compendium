# Conceal Item
*3rd-level, Illusion*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a handful of any kind of gemstone dust)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

**Converted from:** *Priest's Spell Compendium*

You render a single inanimate, nonliving object of Small-size or smaller completely undetectable when you hide it on your person. The object must be placed in a bag, hidden underneath your clothing, or otherwise concealed. No amount of searching, even a physical pat-down, will reveal the object, although true seeing will reveal its location.

You may take the object out, at which point it becomes visible, but until the spell's duration is over, it will return to being invisible once you return it to hiding.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 119*
