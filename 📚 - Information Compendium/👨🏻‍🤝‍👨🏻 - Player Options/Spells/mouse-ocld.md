# Mouse
*2nd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a little winding handle)
- **Duration:** 1 minute, Until dispelled

You animate an object with the speed and timidness of a mouse. Choose a Tiny object within range. The target grows four little furry legs and becomes an [animated mouse](/3-Content/Compendiums/bestiary/construct/animated-mouse-ocld.md) for the duration, immediately rolling initiative and attempting to escape to a hiding spot. It sees all other creatures as deadly threats, including you. 

The effects end if the animated mouse is reduced to 0 hit points (which destroys the object) or if a creature can see the animated mouse when the spell ends. Otherwise, it remains an animated mouse indefinitely.

*Source: Outclassed: The NPC Statblock Compendium p. 257*
