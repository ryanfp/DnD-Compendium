# Death Shroud
*cantrip, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You make a hand sign of endings and focus your will on a creature within range. That creature gains a death shroud. When you deal damage to that creature with an attack, you can discard the death shroud to deal an additional `2d4` damage.

The additional damage increases by `2d4` when you reach 5th level (`4d4`), 11th level (`6d4`), and 17th level (`8d4`).

*Source: The Ultimate Adventurer's Handbook p. 233*
