# Bloodletting Bite
*cantrip, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a weapon)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

As part of the action used to cast this spell, you must make a weapon attack against one creature within the spell's range, otherwise the spell fails. On a hit, if the creature's current hit points are more than half its maximum hit points, the target suffers the attack's normal effects. If the creature's current hit points are equal to or less than half of its maximum hit points, the target suffers the attack's normal effect and takes an additional `d12` necrotic damage.

This spell's damage increases when you reach higher levels. At 5th level, the additional damage dealt by this attack becomes `2d12`. This damage increases by `d12` again at 11th level (`3d12`) and 17th level (`4d12`).
