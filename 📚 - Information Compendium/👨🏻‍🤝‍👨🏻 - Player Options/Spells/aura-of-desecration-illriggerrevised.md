# Aura of Desecration
*4th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Emanation)
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Life-defiling energy radiates from you in an aura with a 30-foot radius. Until the spell ends, the aura moves with you, centered on you. Whenever creatures of your choice enter the area for the first time on a turn or start their turn there, they must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes `4d6` necrotic damage and can't regain hit points until the start of their next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and suffers no other effect.
