# Shooting Star
*9th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 1 miles
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You evoke a mighty beam of starlight that streaks along a 15-foot-wide line that extends from a point you can see within range. The length of the line is 100 feet at minimum and up to 1000 feet at maximum, and it can extend in any direction as long as the endpoint of the line is lower than the starting point.

Each creature in the line must make a Dexterity saving throw. The effect of this saving throw depends on the size of the target.

The beam pierces through creatures of size Huge or larger. On a failed save, such a creature takes `18d10 + 40` radiant damage, and is knocked prone. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and is not knocked prone.

The beam envelops and drags along creatures of size Large or smaller. On a failed save, such a creature takes `18d10` radiant damage, and is pushed along the line. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and is pushed to the nearest unoccupied space outside of the beam's area.

A creature that is pushed along the line can make a Dexterity saving throw after every 50 feet it is pushed. On a successful save, it escapes from the beam and is pushed to the nearest unoccupied space outside of the beam's area. When a creature leaves the beam's area or reaches its end, its momentum is conserved, causing it to be pushed an additional 50 feet even after it leaves the beam. It is also knocked prone.

If the beam would push one creature into another creature, the pushed creature moves around the other creature rather than colliding with it and/or pushing it along.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 55*
