# Glyph of Revealing
*5th-level, Divination*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (60-foot Radius)
- **Components:** V, S, M (ink made out of the juice of crushed eyebright flowers, white chalk, and powdered quartz)
- **Duration:** 10 minutes

**Converted from:** *Priest's Spell Compendium*

You inscribe a special rune on a flat, horizontal surface. All other magical runes, marks, glyphs, symbols, and other forms of written magic become visible, allowing them to be read or identified without being accidentally triggered.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 202*
