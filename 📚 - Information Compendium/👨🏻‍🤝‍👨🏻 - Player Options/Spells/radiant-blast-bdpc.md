# Radiant Blast
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (10-foot Line)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A line of radiant energy 10 feet long and 5 feet wide emanates from you in a direction you choose. Each creature within the line must succeed on a Strength saving throw or take `d6` radiant damage and be pushed up to 5 feet away from you.

The spell's damage increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`).

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 53*
