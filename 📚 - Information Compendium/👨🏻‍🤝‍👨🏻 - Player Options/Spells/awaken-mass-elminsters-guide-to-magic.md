# Awaken, Mass
*8th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 8 hours
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a canary diamond worth at least 5,000 gp, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

This spell works the same way as the awaken spell, except that you may awaken a number of targets equal to your spellcasting ability modifier.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 25*
