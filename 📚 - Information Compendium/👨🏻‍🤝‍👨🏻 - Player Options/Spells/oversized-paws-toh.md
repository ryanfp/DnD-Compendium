# Oversized Paws
*2nd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (claw or talon from a bear or other large animal)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Until this spell ends, the hands and feet of one willing creature within range become oversized and more powerful. For the duration, the creature adds `d4` to damage it deals with its unarmed strike.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each spell slot above 2nd.

*Source: Tome of Heroes p. 297*
