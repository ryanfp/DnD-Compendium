# Nullify Effect
*2nd-level, None*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** 1 round

You gain advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws, including the triggering save, until the start of your next turn. You also gain resistance to psychic and force damage until the start of your next turn.

**At Higher Levels.** If you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can reroll one of the dice after making an applicable saving throw at advantage. If you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, you gain immunity to psychic and force damage for the duration.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 274*
