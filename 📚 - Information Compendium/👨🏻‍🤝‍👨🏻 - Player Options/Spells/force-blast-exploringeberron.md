# Force Blast
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 180 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a bolt of mystical power at a creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes `d8` force damage.

This spell's damage increases by `d8` when you reach 5th level (`2d8`), 11th level (`3d8`), and 17th level (`4d8`).

*Source: Exploring Eberron p. 22*
