# Blizzard
*6th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You conjure a howling blizzard of swirling clouds of ice and snow which fills a 30-foot-radius, 40-foot-high cylinder centered at a point you can see within range. The area becomes heavily obscured and difficult terrain. Any creature that starts its turn within the area or enters it for the first time on its turn must make a Constitution saving throw. A creature takes `6d8` cold damage on a failed saving throw, or half as much on a successful one.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the damage increases by `d8` for each slot level above 6th.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 215*
