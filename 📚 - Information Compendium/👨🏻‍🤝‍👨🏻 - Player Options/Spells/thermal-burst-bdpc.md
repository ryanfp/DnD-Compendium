# Thermal Burst
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 5 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A burst of hot air expands around you. Each creature within range, other than you, must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 5 feet away from you and dealt 1 fire damage.

This spell's damage increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level (`1d6 + 1`), 11th level (`2d6 + 1`), and 17th level (`3d6 + 1`)

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 58*
