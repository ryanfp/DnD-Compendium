# Sanctuary, Mass
*5th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

This spell functions like sanctuary, except that it affects up to seven creatures within range (one of whom may be you).

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
