# Ten Thousand Masks
*cantrip, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You make the hand sign of deception and focus your will on your presence. You can make a disguise kit ability check without having access to a disguise kit. If you are not proficient with disguise kits, you may add half your proficiency modifier.
