# Sunmotes
*1st-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You conjure six motes of sunlight that orbit around you until the spell ends. You can use a bonus action to send up to two of the motes streaking towards creatures you can see within 60 feet of you. Each mote must be sent to a separate creature. Once a mote reaches its target, its magic is imbued into the target until the end of your next turn, or until the spell ends. Then, the mote is expended. The spell ends early if you expend the last mote.

If a mote is imbued into a friendly target, the next attack made against the target while the imbuement lasts is made with disadvantage. If a mote is imbued into a hostile target, the next attack made against the target while the imbuement lasts is made with advantage.

The motes shed dim light in a 5-foot radius around you and around any creatures imbued by a mote.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you conjure four additional motes for each slot level above 1st.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 57*
