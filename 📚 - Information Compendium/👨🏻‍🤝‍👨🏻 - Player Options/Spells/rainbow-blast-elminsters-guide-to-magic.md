# Rainbow Blast
*3rd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a small clear gem or crystal prism worth at least 50 gp)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A multihued ray of energy shoots from your hand at a target within range. Make a ranged spell attack. On hit, the ray deals `d8` acid damage, `d8` cold damage, `d8` fire damage, `d8` lightning damage, and `d8` thunder damage to the target.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the amount of damage of each type increases by `d8` for every three slot levels above 3rd.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
