# Bladethirst
*1st-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** 1 round

**Converted from:** *Spell Compendium*

Your sword or other slashing weapon bursts into unearthly steel-blue flame. Until the end of your turn, you have advantage on all rolls to hit with this weapon and inflict an additional `d6` cold damage. The weapon sheds bright light in 10-foot radius and dim light for a further 10 feet.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell with a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the amount of cold damage you inflict increases by `d6` for each slot level above 1st.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 95*
