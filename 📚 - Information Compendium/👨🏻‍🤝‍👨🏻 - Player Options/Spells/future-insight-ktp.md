# Future Insight
*1st-level, None*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** 10 minutes

Your roll `3d4` or `d12` (your choice) and record the results. During the duration, you can expend one of these dice to add or subtract them from any attack roll, saving throw, or ability check made by a creature within 60 feet of you until the dice are exhausted or the spell ends. You must expend the die after the roll is made, but before you know the outcome of the roll.

**Classes**: [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Psion](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-psion.md)

*Source: KibblesTasty: Psion p. 30*
