# Good Hope
*3rd-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round

**Converted from:** Pathfinder (via `d20`srd.org)

You fill up to 6 willing targets with cheer and powerful feelings of hope. Each creature gains advantage on all rolls until the end of its next turn. This spell counters and dispels [crushing despair](/3-Content/Compendiums/spells/crushing-despair-nrctos.md).

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 203*
