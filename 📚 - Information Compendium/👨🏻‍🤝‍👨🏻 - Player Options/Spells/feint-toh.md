# Feint
*1st-level, Illusion*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** 1 round

You create a momentary, flickering duplicate of yourself just as you make an attack against a creature. You have advantage on the next attack roll you make against the target before the end of your next turn as it's distracted by your illusory copy.

*Source: Tome of Heroes p. 282*
