# Water Whip
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a source of water that you can see within range that has a volume of at least 1 gallon Make a melee spell attack against a creature within 30 feet of the source of water On a hit, the target takes `d6` slashing damage and, if the creature is Large or smaller, you can force it to make a Strength saving throw, pulling it up to 10 feet towards the body of water on a failure

The spell's damage and distance it can pull a target increases when you reach 5th level `2d6` and 15 feet), 11th level `3d6` and 20 feet), and 17th level `4d6` and 25 feet)


*Source: Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting*
