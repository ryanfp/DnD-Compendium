# Thunderous Distortion
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (10-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You produce a distorted wave of noise in a 10-foot cone, which can be heard up to 100 feet away. Each creature in that area must succeed a Constitution saving throw, or take `d6` thunder damage.

An echo of this noise persists until the end of your next turn. If you cast this spell again before the end of your next turn, its damage becomes d8s, instead of d6s

This spell's damage increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`).

*Source: Valda's Spire of Secrets p. 355*
