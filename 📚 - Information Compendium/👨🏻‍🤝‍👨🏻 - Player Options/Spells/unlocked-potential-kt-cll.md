# Unlocked Potential
*1st-level, None*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You unlock the potential of a creature's mind, allowing it to fully reach its limits. For the duration, once per turn the creature can add `d4` to any attack roll, damage roll, or saving throw it makes. Each time it adds the extra `d4`, it takes 1 psychic damage as it pushes beyond its natural limitations.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 273*
