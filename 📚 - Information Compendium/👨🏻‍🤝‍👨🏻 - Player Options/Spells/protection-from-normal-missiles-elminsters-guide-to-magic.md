# Protection from Normal Missiles
*3rd-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a piece of turtle shell)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

A magical shield protects you from ranged attacks from nonmagical weapons, even larger projectiles like catapult and ballista. For the duration, you are immune to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from ranged weapon attacks.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
