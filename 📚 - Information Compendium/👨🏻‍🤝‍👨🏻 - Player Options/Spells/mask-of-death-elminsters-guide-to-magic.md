# Mask of Death
*4th-level, Necromancy (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a drop of doppelganger blood)
- **Duration:** 12 hours

You change the features of any dead humanoid to mimic someone else. You must either know the person you are mimicking or have an accurate picture or image of them. This spell can be cast on an undead creature that has a tangible body and an intact face (such as a zombie), but its statistics and behavior do not change. A body that is the target of this spell retains the facial features for the duration, even if it is then animated as undead.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
