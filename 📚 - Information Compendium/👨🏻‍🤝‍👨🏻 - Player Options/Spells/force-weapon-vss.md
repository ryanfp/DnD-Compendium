# Force Weapon
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 5 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a specially prepared gauntlet worth at least 5 gp)
- **Duration:** 1 round

You conjure a blade of magical force in the air, which lashes out at your foes. Make a melee spell attack against a creature within range. On a hit, the target takes `d10` force damage. The blade remains in existence for a short time; until the start of your next turn, you can make a single strike with your mystical blade as an opportunity attack.

You can make 1 additional attack on your turn at 5th level (2 attacks), at 11th level (3 attacks), and at 17th level (4 attacks).

*Source: Valda's Spire of Secrets p. 339*
