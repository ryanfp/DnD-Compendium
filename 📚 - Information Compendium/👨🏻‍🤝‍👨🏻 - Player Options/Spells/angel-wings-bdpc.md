# Angel Wings
*4th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You point your finger, and the creature that would've been reduced to 0 hit points instead drops to 1 hit point, and then regains additional hit points equal to `d8` + your spellcasting ability modifier (minimum 1). The creature is also bestowed with large, angelic wings that allow it to immediately use its reaction to fly up to 30 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. The wings disappear at the end of the turn, causing the creature to fall if it ended the turn in the air.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the healing increases by `d8` for each slot level above 5th.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 39*
