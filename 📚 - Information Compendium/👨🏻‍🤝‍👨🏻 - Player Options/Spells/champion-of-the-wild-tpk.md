# Champion of the Wild
*9th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 24 hours
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a star emerald worth 5,000 gp)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

After spending the casting time listening and conversing with nature itself, you touch a beast that is native to the surrounding environment. The target becomes a beast of legend, gaining the Beast of Legend Template. You choose which proficiencies the beast gains according to the Saving Throw Proficiencies and Skill Proficiencies feature and select the beast's special traits according to its [Expanded Traits](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-optfeaturetype-et.md) feature.

**Classes**: [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Druid](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-druid.md)

*Source: Total Party Kill Bestiary - Vol. 1 p. 50*
