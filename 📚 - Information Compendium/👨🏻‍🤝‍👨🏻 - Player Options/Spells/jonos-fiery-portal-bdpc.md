# Jono's Fiery Portal
*5th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 10 minutes
- **Range:** 10 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 10 days

As you cast this spell, you burn a series of runic marks onto a 10-foot square surface that you can see within range, creating a runic pattern that fills the square and lasts for the duration, unless they are dispelled or the surface they are on is destroyed.

If you have two or more of these patterns active at once, you can use your action to touch one of the patterns, causing the marks of that pattern as well as another active pattern of your choice on the same plane of existence to slowly burn away. After 1 minute, the patterns fully burn away, and both 10-foot square surfaces become connected portals surrounded by a ring of fire. These portals remain open for 1 minute. Any creature or object that enters a portal instantly appears within 5 feet of the opposite portal or in the nearest unoccupied space if that space is occupied, conserving its momentum as it passes through.

When the portals open, you can allow any number of creatures you select to safely move between the portals. If any other creature enters a portal, it takes `4d8` fire damage as it passes through it. The portals can also be seen through.

If one of the portals is dispelled, both portals close.

When the portals close, the spell ends for each portal.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the fire damage increases by `d8` and the spell's duration increases by 10 days for each slot level above 5th.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 47*
