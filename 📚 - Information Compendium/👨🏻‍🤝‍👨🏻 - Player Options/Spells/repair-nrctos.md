# Repair
*1st-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 10 minutes
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Converted from:** *Spell Compendium*

An inanimate object you touch regains a number of hit points equal to `d8` + your spellcasting ability modifier. This spell can be used to heal undead and constructs as well.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the healing increases by `d8` for each slot level above 1st.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 299*
