# Intensify Sensation
*2nd-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Converted from:** *Priest's Spell Compendium*

You touch a creature and cause all physical and emotional sensations its experiences to become unbelievable powerful. A creature under the influence of this spell is incapacitated for the duration. An unwilling target may make a Constitution saving throw to resist.

Spells designed to create an emotion or physical sensation (such as [crushing despair](/3-Content/Compendiums/spells/crushing-despair-nrctos.md) (q.v.) or many of the effects of symbol) have their duration doubled and the creature saves against them at disadvantage. This does not affect spells that merely inflict damage; the spell needs to create a sensation.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 227*
