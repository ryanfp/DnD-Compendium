# Card Trick
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a deck of playing cards)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

With a flash of your hands, you fling a playing or tarot card charged with energy at your opponents. Choose whether you make a spell attack roll or for the target to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a hit or a failed saving throw, the target takes `d6` force damage. 

This spell's damage increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`).

*Source: Valda's Spire of Secrets p. 332*
