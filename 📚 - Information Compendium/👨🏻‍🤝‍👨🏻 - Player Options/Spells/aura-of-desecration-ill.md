# Aura of Desecration
*5th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Radius)
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Life-defiling energy radiates from you in an aura with a 30-foot radius. Until the spell ends, the aura moves with you, centered on you. When a creature enters the area or starts its turn there, it takes `d6` necrotic damage and you regain hit points equal to the damage dealt.

*Source: MCDM - The Illrigger Class p. 19*
