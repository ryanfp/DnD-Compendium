# Cauterize
*2nd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round

You touch a creature's wound, causing the creature to regain a number of hit points equal to `2d8` + your spellcasting ability modifier, while inflicting an excruciating burning sensation. During its next turn, the creature's speed is halved and it is incapacitated by pain. This spell does not restore hit points to nor incapacitate constructs or undead. If you attempt to target an unwilling creature, that creature must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw to avoid the spell.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the healing increases by `d8` for each slot level above 2nd.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 41*
