# Misdirection
*1st-level, Illusion*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a brief flash of light, loud sound, or other distraction that interrupts a creature's attack. When a creature you can see within range makes an attack, you can impose disadvantage on its attack roll.

*Source: Tome of Heroes p. 296*
