# Instant Counter
*2nd-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a melee weapon worth at least 1 sp)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You vanish, teleporting to an empty space within 5 feet of the creature that damaged you, and immediately make a weapon attack against them with the weapon used in the spell's casting.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, if your attack hits, it deals `d8` bonus force damage for each slot level above 2nd.

**Classes**: [Spellblade](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-spellblade.md)

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 238*
