# Valiant Fury
*5th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose either yourself or a creature of your choice that you can see within range as the spell's target. The recipient gains advantage on Strength, Constitution, and Dexterity ability checks and saving throws for the duration. In addition, until the spell ends, the subject gains advantage on attack rolls that include its Dexterity or Strength modifier. When the subject takes the attack action on its turn, it can make an extra melee weapon attack as a bonus action.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
