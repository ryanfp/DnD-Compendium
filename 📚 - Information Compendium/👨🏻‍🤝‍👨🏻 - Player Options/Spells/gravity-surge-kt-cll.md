# Gravity Surge
*2nd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a crushing field of gravity in a 15 radius sphere. Each creature within the area must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes `2d6` bludgeoning damage and falls prone. On a success, it takes half as much damage and is not knocked prone. Creatures that are not on the ground make this save with disadvantage.
