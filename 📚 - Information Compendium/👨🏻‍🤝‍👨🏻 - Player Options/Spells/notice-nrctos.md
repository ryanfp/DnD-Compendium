# Notice
*cantrip, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 round
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** 1 round

**Converted from:** The Great Net Spellbook

You put yourself in a trance for a few seconds, and when you emerge, you are highly aware of your surroundings. Until the end of your next turn, you have advantage on your Perception check. While you are in this trance, you are deaf and blind as to your own surroundings.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 275*
