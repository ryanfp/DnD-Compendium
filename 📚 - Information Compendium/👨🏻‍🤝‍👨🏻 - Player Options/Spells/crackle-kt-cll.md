# Crackle
*2nd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You create three arcs of lightning striking targets in range. You can direct them at one target or several.

Make a ranged spell attack for each arc. On a hit, the target takes `d12` lightning damage. If three or more arcs hit a single target, it must make a Constitution saving throw or become [shocked](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Shocked), stunning it until the start of its next turn.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, you create one additional arc for each slot level above 2nd.

**Classes**: [Occultist](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md); [Spellblade](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-spellblade.md)

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 219*
