# Ahrvar's Forgery
*1st-level, Transmutation (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 round unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a sample of the handwriting to be copied)
- **Duration:** 10 minutes

**Converted from:** The Great Net Spellbook

When you cast this spell, you gain the ability to perfectly mimic one person's handwriting for the next ten minutes.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 67*
