# Inflict Pain
*1st-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature, inflicting them with negative energy and racking them with excruciating pain. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take `d10` necrotic damage and become incapacitated with pain until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Dark Arts Player's Companion p. 28*
