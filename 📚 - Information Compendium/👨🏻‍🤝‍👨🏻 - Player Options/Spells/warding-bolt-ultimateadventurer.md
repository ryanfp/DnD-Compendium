# Warding Bolt
*cantrip, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You launch a mote of divine light at a creature you can see within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target; the attack automatically hits the creature if it is willing. On a hit, you choose for the spell either to deal `d4` radiant damage to the target or to grant `d4` temporary hit points to it. The target loses any temporary hit points it has remaining from this spell at the start of your next turn.

The spell's damage and temporary hit points each increase by `d4` when you reach 5th level (`2d4`), 11th level (`3d4`), and 17th level (`4d4`)

*Source: The Ultimate Adventurer's Handbook p. 271*
