# Enervating Bolt
*4th-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A bolt of black lightning leaps from your target to you. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. The target takes `8d8` necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. In addition, its hit point maximum is reduced by the amount of damage it takes. After a long rest, the target's hit point maximum returns to normal.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a 5th level or higher spell slot, the damage increases by `d8` for each slot level above 4th.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 47*
