# Drain
*1st-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a red ribbon)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Stretching out your hand and pointing your finger at a creature within range, a flash of negative energy briefly connects you to the target. It must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take `3d4` necrotic damage, and you gain a number of temporary hit points<sup><span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">'24</span></sup> equal to the damage dealt. You lose any temporary hit points granted by this spell after 1 minute.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by `2d4` for each slot level above 1st.

**Classes**: [Accursed](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-accursed.md)

*Source: The Ultimate Adventurer's Handbook p. 236*
