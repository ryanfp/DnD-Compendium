# Sphere of Judgment
*9th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 500 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a black opal, worth at least 1,000 gp)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You conjure a 10-foot-radius black sphere at an unoccupied location that you can see within range. The sphere sheds bright light in a 250-foot radius and dim light for an additional 250 feet, and it lasts for the duration.

The sphere is an object that has AC 20, 500 hit points, and immunity to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from nonmagical attacks. If it drops to 0 hit points, the spell ends.

When you cast the spell, you decide which creatures will be affected by it. There are no limitations on this decision—it can be as specific or as broad as you'd like. You might have the spell affect creatures that are of a certain alignment or creature type, that follow a certain deity or leader, or that have done a specific crime or deed. These are just examples—there are many more options that you could choose.

An affected creature that starts its turn within the bright light or enters it for the first time on a turn must make a Constitution saving throw. A creature takes `8d8` radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

On each of your turns after you cast this spell, you can use your action to move the sphere up to 120 feet in a straight line in any direction. If you ram the sphere into a creature, that creature must make a Constitution saving throw, and the sphere stops moving this turn. The creature takes `5d8` bludgeoning damage plus `5d8` radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

Whenever an affected creature dies while within the area of bright light, its essence is absorbed into the sphere. While the spell lasts, the creature can be restored to life only through the casting of wish.

If you maintain your concentration on this spell for its whole duration, the absorbed creatures are relocated into the opal used as this spell's material component. Until the opal is destroyed or dispel magic is cast upon it at 9th level, the creatures can be restored to life only through the casting of wish.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 56*
