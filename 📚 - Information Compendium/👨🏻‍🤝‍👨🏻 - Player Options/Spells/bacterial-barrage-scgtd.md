# Bacterial Barrage
*cantrip, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You attack a creature in range with a quick spreading infection that takes advantage of existing conditions. Make a ranged spell attack against a target, on a hit the infection does `d8` necrotic damage to the target. If the target is suffering from the poisoned condition or a disease, it instead takes `d12` necrotic damage.

The spell's damage increases by one die when you reach 5th level (`2d8` or `2d12`), 11th level (`3d8` or `3d12`), and 17th level (`4d8` or `4d12`).

*Source: Sebastian Crowe's Guide to Drakkenheim p. 186*
