# Guidance
*cantrip, Divination*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch a willing creature and choose a skill. Until the spell ends, the creature adds `d4` to any ability check using the chosen skill.
