# Godly Protection
*4th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Converted from:** The Great Net Prayerbook

Divine light illuminates all of your allies within range, provided those allies are not engaging in any action other than joining you in prayer. After you have cast the spell, they may resume their normal activities.

For the spell's duration, your allies are immune to being frightened or charmed and will have advantage on any one type of roll of your choosing: either all attack rolls, saving throws, or ability checks.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 202*
