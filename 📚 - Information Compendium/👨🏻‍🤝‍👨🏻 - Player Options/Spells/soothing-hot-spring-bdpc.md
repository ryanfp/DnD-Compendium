# Soothing Hot Spring
*4th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 10 minutes
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (obsidian worth at least 30 gp, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** 24 hours

Choose a 15-foot square area on the ground within range. That area sinks by 3 feet, and then fills in with comfortably hot, 3-foot deep water that lasts for the duration.

A creature that takes a short rest while bathing within the hot spring can complete the short rest in just 30 minutes, and regains the maximum number of hit points possible from any hit dice it spends during the rest, rather than rolling.

A creature that takes a long rest while bathing within the hot spring is cured of any diseases or poisons afflicting it, has its exhaustion level reduced by 2, rather than 1, and gains 5 temporary hit points.

You can create a permanent hot spring by casting this spell in the same location every day for one year.

When the spell ends, the ground rises back to its previous position and the water dissipates.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the number of temporary hit points granted increases by 5 for each slot level above 4th.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 56*
