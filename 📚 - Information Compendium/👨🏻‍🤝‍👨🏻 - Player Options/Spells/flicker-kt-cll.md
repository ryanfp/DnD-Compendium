# Flicker
*1st-level, None*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** 1 round

You flicker between the material and ethereal planes. Until the start of your next turn, each time you would take damage, including the triggering attack, roll a `d4`. On a 2, you gain resistance to that instance of damage. On a 4, you don't take any damage.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 271*
