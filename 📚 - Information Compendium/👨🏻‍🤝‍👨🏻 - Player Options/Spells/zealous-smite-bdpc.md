# Zealous Smite
*4th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

The next time you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack during the duration, your weapon ignites with zealous fury, and the attack deals an additional `4d8` fire damage on a hit. Until the spell ends, you can't be charmed, frightened, or possessed by the target. Also, your melee weapon attacks against the target score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the damage increases by `d8` for each slot level above 4th.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 58*
