# Darklight's Planar Weapon
*6th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (true)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

**Converted from:** The Great Net Spellbook

You create a 3-foot-long blade of energy drawn from one of the Planes. You are considered proficient in it. The exact effects of the weapon depends on from which plane the energy came. It inflicts `9d6` damage on a hit; the exact type of damage inflicted by the sword depends on its type.

You may attempt to choose what sort of blade you produce by making a DC 15 ability check using your spellcasting ability. On a success, you get the blade you wish. Otherwise, the blade you receive is completely random.
