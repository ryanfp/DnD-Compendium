# Claws of the Umber Hulk
*6th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a umber hulk claw)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

Your fingers and nails transform into diamond-hard claws. The skin on your hands and forearms thickens and turns into a hardened shell. For the duration, you gain a burrowing speed of 30 feet, a +4 bonus to your AC, and advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws. You can also use an action on each of your turns to make two melee spell attacks with a reach of 10 feet, inflicting `1d8 + 4` magical slashing damage when one hits.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 35*
