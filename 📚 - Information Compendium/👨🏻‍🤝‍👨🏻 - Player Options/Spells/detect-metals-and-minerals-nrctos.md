# Detect Metals and Minerals
*1st-level, Divination (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** Self (60-foot Sphere)
- **Components:** V, S, M (a small magnet, a vial of weak acid, and a small sample of the desired material)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

**Converted from:** *Wizard's Spell Compendium*

For the duration, you sense the presence of a single type of ore, mineral deposit, or worked metal you specify within range. You will learn how far away it is, and roughly how much of it is there.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 150*
