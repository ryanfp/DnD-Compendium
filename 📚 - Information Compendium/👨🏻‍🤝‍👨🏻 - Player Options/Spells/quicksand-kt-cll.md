# Quicksand
*3rd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of sand)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You cause the ground in a 20-foot radius centered on a point you can see to turn to quicksand for the duration of the spell. A creature moving within or out of the area must spend 4 feet of movement for every foot it moves. A creature ending its turn in the area must succeed on a Strength saving throw or fall prone.
