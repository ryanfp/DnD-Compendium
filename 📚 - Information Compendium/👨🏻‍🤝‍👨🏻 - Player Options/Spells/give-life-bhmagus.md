# Give Life
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

By placing your hand on another creature, you transfer your life force to them. You spend and roll one of your hit die. The target regains an amount of hit points equivalent to the result of the roll.
