# Prophesied Strike
*7th-level, Divination*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch a creature and imbue it with the forces of destiny. The next time the creature makes a weapon or spell attack during this spell's duration, the attack automatically hits without the creature needing to make an attack roll, and the attack scores a critical hit.

**At Higher Levels.** When you use a spell slot of 8th level or higher, for each slot level higher than 7th, the creature rolls an additional `2d12` when determining the extra damage for the critical hit.

*Source: The Ultimate Adventurer's Handbook p. 258*
