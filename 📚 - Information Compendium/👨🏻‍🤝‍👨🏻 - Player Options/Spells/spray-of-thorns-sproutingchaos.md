# Spray of Thorns
*3rd-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (20-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V, S, M (a rose thorn)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You shoot forth a burst of thorns from your hands which pierce through your enemy's defenses, making them vulnerable to attacks. Each creature in a 20-foot cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes `6d6` piercing damage, and until the start of your next turn they take an additional `d6` piercing damage whenever they are hit by a weapon attack. On a successful save, a creature takes half damage and suffers no other effect.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the initial damage increases by `d6` for each slot level above 3rd.

*Source: Sprouting Chaos Player's Companion p. -1*
