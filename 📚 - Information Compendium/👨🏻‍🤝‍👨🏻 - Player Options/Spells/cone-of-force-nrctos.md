# Cone of Force
*5th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (60-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V, S, M (a small cone made of quartz worth at least 25 gp)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Converted from:** The Great Net Spellbook

A cone of green and golden magical force erupts from your hands. Each creature in a 60-foot cone must make a Constitution saving throw. A creature takes `8d10` force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature killed by this spell is reduced to dust.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the damage increases by `d10` for each slot level above 5th.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 120*
