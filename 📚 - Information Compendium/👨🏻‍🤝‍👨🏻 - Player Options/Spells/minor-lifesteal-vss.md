# Minor Lifesteal
*cantrip, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You drain life energy from a hostile creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take `d4` necrotic damage. You then gain temporary hit points equal to the amount of damage dealt, which lasts until you finish a long rest. This spell has no effect on undead or constructs.

This spell's damage increases by `d4` when you reach 5th level (`2d4`), 11th level (`3d4`), and 17th level (`4d4`).

*Source: Valda's Spire of Secrets p. 349*
