# Ignite Fire
*cantrip, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You ignite a magical fire that fills a 5-foot cube in a space you can see on the ground. A creature in the fire's space when you cast the spell must succeed a Dexterity saving throw or take `d8` fire damage. A creature that enters the fire's space for the first time or ends their turn there must repeat the saving throw against the effect. Flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried catch fire.
