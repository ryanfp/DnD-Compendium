# Bloodletting
*cantrip, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature and spend 1 hit point, opening a wound where you touch. Make a melee spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes `d12` necrotic damage. If this damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, you gain temporary hit points equal to half the damage dealt. These temporary hit points last for up to 1 minute.

The spell's damage increases by `d12` when you reach 5th level (`2d12`), 11th level (`3d12`), and 17th level (`4d12`).

*Source: Dark Arts Player's Companion p. 26*
