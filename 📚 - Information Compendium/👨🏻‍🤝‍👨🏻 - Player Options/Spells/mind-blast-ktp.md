# Mind Blast
*6th-level, None*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (60-foot Cone)
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You emit a blast of psychic energy. Each creature in a 60-foot cone must make an Intelligence saving throw. A creature takes `6d8` psychic damage and is [stunned](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Stunned) until the end of their next turn on a failed save. A creature takes half as much damage and is not [stunned](/3-Content/Rules/conditions.md#Stunned) on a successful save.

**Classes**: [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Psion](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-psion.md)

*Source: KibblesTasty: Psion p. 32*
