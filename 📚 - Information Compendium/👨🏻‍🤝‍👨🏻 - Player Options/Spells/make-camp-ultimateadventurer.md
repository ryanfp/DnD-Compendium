# Make Camp
*1st-level, Abjuration (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 minute unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a tent)
- **Duration:** 8 hours

When you cast this spell while setting up camp, the area within 20 feet of where you cast this spell gains the following benefits until the spell ends:

- Nonmagical weather and climate effects will not harm anyone within the area.  
- Beasts will not enter the area unless magically compelled to do so.  
- Creatures outside the area have disadvantage on ability checks to perceive or find the camp, unless you choose otherwise.  
- Creatures that complete a long rest<sup><span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">'24</span></sup> in the area gain `d6` temporary hit points<sup><span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">'24</span></sup> at the end of the long rest.  

*Source: The Ultimate Adventurer's Handbook p. 250*
