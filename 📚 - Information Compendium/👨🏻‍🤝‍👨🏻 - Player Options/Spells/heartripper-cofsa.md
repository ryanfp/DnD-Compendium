# Heartripper
*2nd-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

> [!quote]  
> 
> A plant fed with blood does not mourn, nor does it show mercy. 

When you cast this spell, barbed thorns sprout from your fingertips. Make a melee spell attack roll against a creature within range. If you hit, the target takes `d10` piercing damage and must make a Constitution saving throw. If they fail, you grasp a vital part of their anatomy and squeeze, inflicting an additional `3d10` piercing damage and stunning the target until the start of their next turn. If the spell attack roll resulted in a critical hit, the target has disadvantage on their saving throw.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a slot of 3rd level or higher, the piercing damage of the second phase of the spell increases by `d10` per slot level above 2nd.

*Source: The Compendium of Forgotten Secrets: Awakening p. 169*
