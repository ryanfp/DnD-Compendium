# Confuse Self
*1st-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

**Converted from:** The Great Net Spellbook

You become utterly and completely confused. You are incapacitated for the duration, but you are also immune to being charmed or frightened and to having your mind or emotions read or mind controlled. You are also immune to all mundane attempts to persuade or intimidate you.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 120*
