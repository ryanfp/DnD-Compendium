# Curse of Doom
*3rd-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (something from the target creature—such as blood, hair, or scales—which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You curse a creature within range, dooming it. The next attack roll that hits the target creature becomes a critical hit, after which the spell ends.

> [!note] Critical Hit vs 20
> 
> Note that while this makes the attack a critical hit, it does not make it a natural 20, meaning effects like a vorpal sword would not be triggered.
^critical-hit-vs-20
