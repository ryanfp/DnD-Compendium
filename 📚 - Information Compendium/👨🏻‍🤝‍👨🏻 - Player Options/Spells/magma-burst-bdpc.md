# Magma Burst
*5th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

A mass of magma erupts into existence at a point you can see within range. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes `5d10` fire damage, and is enveloped in lava for the duration. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage and is not enveloped.

An enveloped creature's speed is halved, and it takes `2d10` fire damage at the end of each of its turns. As an action, an enveloped creature can make a Strength check against your spell save DC. On a success, the target is no longer enveloped.

As a bonus action on your turn, you can choose one creature you can see within 60 feet of you that is enveloped by this lava, and cause that magma to harden. Beginning at the end of that creature's next turn, it stops taking fire damage from this spell and instead becomes restrained for as long as it remains enveloped by the hardened lava.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the initial damage increases by `d10` for each slot level above 5th.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 50*
