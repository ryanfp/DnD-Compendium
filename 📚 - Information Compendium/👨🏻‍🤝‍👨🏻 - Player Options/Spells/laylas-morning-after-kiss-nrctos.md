# Layla's Morning-After Kiss
*3rd-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Until dispelled

**Converted from:** The Great Net Spellbook

You kiss an unconscious or sleeping creature's skin (although you do not need to kiss its lips) and it makes a Wisdom saving throw. If it fails, it permanently forgets everything that happened up to 1 hour before it fell asleep.

The memory can be recovered by use of the [hypnosis](/3-Content/Compendiums/spells/hypnosis-nrctos.md) (q.v.) spell.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 241*
