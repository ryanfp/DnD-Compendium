# Bile Beam
*4th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (100-foot Line)
- **Components:** V, S, M (true)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a beam of acid in a line 5 feet wide and 100 feet long, which stops early if it impacts a creature, Large or larger object, or solid surface. If the beam hits a creature, the creature must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, it takes `12d4` acid damage and an additional `4d4` acid damage at the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half as much initial acid damage and no additional damage.

If a creature or object is killed or destroyed by the beam, the beam continues until it impacts another creature or object.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast the spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the initial damage increases by `d4` for each level above 4th.
