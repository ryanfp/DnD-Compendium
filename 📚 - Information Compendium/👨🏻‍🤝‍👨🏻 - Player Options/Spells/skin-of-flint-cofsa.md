# Skin of Flint
*4th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a shard of flint)
- **Duration:** 1 minute

> [!note]
> The spark that starts a fire of greed is often the wealth of another.

When you cast this spell, a thin layer of flint covers your skin and armor. Once per turn when you take damage from a weapon attack, sparks spring from the stone, inflicting `d4` fire damage to a creature of your choosing within 5 feet of you. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. If they fail, they are blinded until the start of their next turn.

**At Higher Levels.** At Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the fire damage increases by `d4` per slot level above 4th.

*Source: The Compendium of Forgotten Secrets: Awakening p. 176*
