# Gather Animals
*2nd-level, Enchantment (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 minute unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a bell or whistle)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You call upon the local creatures and command them to heed your call. Name one beast with a CR of 1/2 or less. Once the spell casting ends all beasts of that type within 1/2 mile travel to convene at your location. They act as they normally would, and may choose to leave after 2d4+2 rounds.
