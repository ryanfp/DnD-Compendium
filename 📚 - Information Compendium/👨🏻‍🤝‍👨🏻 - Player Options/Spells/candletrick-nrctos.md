# Candletrick
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** 1 hour

**Converted from:** *Wizard's Spell Compendium*

You light a candle or lantern—any source of light that has a wick (as opposed to torches). For the duration of the spell, this flame cannot be extinguished by nonmagical means. If the flame is snuffed out, it will automatically relight itself.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 109*
