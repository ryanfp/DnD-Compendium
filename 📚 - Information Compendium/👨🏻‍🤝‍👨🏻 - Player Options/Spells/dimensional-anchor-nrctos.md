# Dimensional Anchor
*4th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

**Converted from:** *Priest's Spell Compendium*

You fire a ray of emerald energy at one creature within range. You must make a ranged spell attack to hit. If you are successful, the target is bound by a magical net that blocks all forms of extradimensional travel. While trapped by the spell, the affected creature cannot use spells or magical effects such as astral projection, blink, dimension door, [Drawmij's instant exit](/3-Content/Compendiums/spells/drawmijs-instant-exit-nrctos.md) (q.v.), etherealness, gate, maze, misty step, plane shift, or teleport, or use gates and teleportation circles. It also prevents creatures from using native abilities, such as a blink dog's or balor's teleportation.

This spell is ineffective against creatures that are already ethereal or astral, nor does it prevent extradimensional perception or attack. It does not stop summoned creatures from vanishing when the spell that summoned them ends.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 152*
