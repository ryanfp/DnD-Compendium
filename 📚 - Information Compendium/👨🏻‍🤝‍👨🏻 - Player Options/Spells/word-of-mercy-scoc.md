# Word of Mercy
*1st-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

As a reaction when you see a friendly creature take damage, you shout a verbal command, causing purple mist to briefly surround the target of an attack within range that you can see. If the target's current hit points are more than the damage of the attack, the spell fails. Otherwise, the attack misses and the target takes no damage. At the start of your next turn, the purple mist surrounds you, dealing the damage of the initial attack
