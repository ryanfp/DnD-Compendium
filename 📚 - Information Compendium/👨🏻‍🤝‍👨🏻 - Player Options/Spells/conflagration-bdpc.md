# Conflagration
*6th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You cause one creature you can see within range to become enveloped in fire. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. It takes `3d12` fire damage on a failed saving throw, or `2d12` fire damage on a successful one. Either way, the target continues burning for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the saving throw. On a failed save, roll the same number of d12s (`d12`) as the last time you dealt damage with this spell. On a successful save, roll one less `d12`. When no d12s (`d12`) remain, the spell ends early. The target takes damage equal to the number rolled. For example, a creature that succeeds on its saving throw each turn would take `2d12` damage from the initial save, `d12` from the next save, and the spell would end after the third save is made.

If the target dies before the spell ends, its body is reduced to ash and the flames that enveloped it lash out to strike at nearby foes. Each creature of your choice within a 20-foot radius centered on the target must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking `6d12` fire damage on a failed saving throw or half as much damage on a successful one. Then, the spell ends.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the initial damage dealt and area damage dealt when the target dies each increase by `d12` for each slot level above 6th.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 41*
