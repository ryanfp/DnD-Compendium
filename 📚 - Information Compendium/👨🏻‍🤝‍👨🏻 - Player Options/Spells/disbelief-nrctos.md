# Disbelief
*8th-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Converted from:** *Priest's Spell Compendium*

You convince yourself or a creature you touch that as many as four objects or creatures of Huge size or smaller simply do not exist. Unwilling creatures may make an Intelligence saving throw to resist. The objects and creatures cannot harm or hinder you in any way, nor you can take any action against them. You can pass through a disbelieved creature or object as if it was difficult terrain and take `d10` force damage if you end your turn inside one.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 154*
