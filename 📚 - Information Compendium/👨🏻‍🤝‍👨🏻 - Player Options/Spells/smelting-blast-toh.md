# Smelting Blast
*2nd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You unleash a bolt of red-hot liquid metal at a creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes `2d8` fire damage and must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be coated in cooling, liquid metal for the duration. A creature coated in liquid metal takes `d8` fire damage at the start of each of its turns as the metal scorches while it cools. At the end of each of its turns, the creature can make another Constitution saving throw. On a success, the spell ends and the liquid metal disappears.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage (both initial and later) increases by `d8` for each slot level above 2nd.

*Source: Tome of Heroes p. 303*
