# Instant Replay
*1st-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

The next time you miss a creature with an attack before this spell ends, you can instantly reset yourself to the moment before the attack and repeat it against the same target.

**Classes**: [Vagabond (Mage Brand)](/3-Content/lists/list-spells-classes-mage-brand-mhp-vb.md "subclass=MHP:VB;class=MHP:VB")

*Source: Mage Hand Press: Vagabond p. 9*
