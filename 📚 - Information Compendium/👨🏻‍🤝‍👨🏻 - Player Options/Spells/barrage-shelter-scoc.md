# Barrage Shelter
*2nd-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a buckler, and a wooden arrow which is consumed upon casting)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

An area within a 10-foot-radius centered on that point that you choose becomes covered by a sudden barrier of force. For the duration every creature in a 10 foot radius benefits from half cover when targeted by magical ranged attacks, and cannot be targeted by nonmagical ranged attacks.
