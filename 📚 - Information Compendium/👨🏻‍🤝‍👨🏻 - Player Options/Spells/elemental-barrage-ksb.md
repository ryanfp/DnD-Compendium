# Elemental Barrage
*2nd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** 
- **Duration:** 1 round

You touch of piece of ammunition infusing it with chaotic elemental power. The next time that piece of ammunition is fired before the start of your next turn, it is destroyed and transformed into three elemental bolts one of which does fire damage, one of which does cold damage, and one of which does lightning damage.

Make a weapon attack roll for each elemental bolt. You can fire all three at the same target or different targets within the weapon attacks range. On a hit, each elemental bolt does elemental damage equal to the normal weapon damage of the attack.

**Classes**: [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Ranger](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-ranger.md); [Spellblade](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-spellblade.md)
