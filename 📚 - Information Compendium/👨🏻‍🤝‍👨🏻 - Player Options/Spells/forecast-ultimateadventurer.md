# Forecast
*6th-level, Divination (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 minute unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a glass thermometer and a copper weathervane)
- **Duration:** 24 hours

You predict that you're going to cast a spell within the next 24 hours and send your future self the power to cast it. Choose a spell of 5th level or lower that you know or have prepared and that has a casting time of 1 action, then expend a spell slot of the spell's level or higher to forecast it. Once before the spell ends, you can cast the forecasted spell without expending a spell slot, at which time you lose it as your forecasted spell.

You also lose the spell as your forecasted spell if you cast this spell again.

*Source: The Ultimate Adventurer's Handbook p. 241*
