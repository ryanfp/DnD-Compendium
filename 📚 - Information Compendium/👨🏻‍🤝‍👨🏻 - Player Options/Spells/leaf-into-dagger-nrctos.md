# Leaf into Dagger
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a normal leaf)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Converted from:** *Priest's Spell Compendium*

You transform any tree leaf into a magical dagger that does `d4` + your spellcasting ability modifier in piercing damage. You cannot be harmed by this blade and should you drop it, it instantly becomes a leaf again.

*Source: The (Not Really) Complete Tome of Spells p. 241*
