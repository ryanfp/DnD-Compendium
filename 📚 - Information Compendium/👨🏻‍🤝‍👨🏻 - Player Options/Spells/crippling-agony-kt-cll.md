# Crippling Agony
*1st-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a joint bone)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You can inflict crippling agony on one creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or become crippled with horrific pain. Whenever the target moves more than half of its speed or takes any action, it takes `d6` necrotic damage.

The target can repeat the saving throw, ending the spell on a success.

**Classes**: [Occultist](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md); [Spellblade](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-spellblade.md)

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 220*
