# Implosion
*9th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Your targets stand perfectly still for a moment, then fold into themselves with a disgusting SPLURCH! Choose four targets within range. Each target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a target's Constitution score is reduced by `4d6`. If a creature's Constitution is reduced to 0 in this way, it twists into a bloody sack of torn muscle and cracked bone, and dies. A creature that fails its saving throw, but survives the reduction of its Constitution, is poisoned until it finishes a long rest. Such a creature regains its normal Constitution score when it completes a long rest.

If a target succeeds on the Constitution saving throw, it suffers no ill effects.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
