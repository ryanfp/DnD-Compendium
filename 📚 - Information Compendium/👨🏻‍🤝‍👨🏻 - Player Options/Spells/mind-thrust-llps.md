# Mind Thrust
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You batter a creature within range with a torrent of psionic power. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On hit, the target takes `d12` magical bludgeoning damage.

This spell's damage increases by `d12` when you reach 5th level (`2d12`), 11th level (`3d12`), and 17th level (`4d12`).

**Classes**: [Bard](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Cleric (Eldritch Domain)](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-eldritch-domain-ghpg.md "subclass=GHPG")
