# Rotting Curse
*1st-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (something from the target creature—such as blood, hair, or scales—which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You inflicting a rotting decay on a creature, causing it to begin to rot. For the duration of the spell, every time the target takes damage, it takes an additional `d4` necrotic damage.

Additionally, the effect of all healing on the target creature is reduced by half and the target creature has disadvantage on any Charisma checks for social interaction during the effect of the spell.

**Classes**: [Occultist](/3-Content/Compendiums/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md)

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 20*
