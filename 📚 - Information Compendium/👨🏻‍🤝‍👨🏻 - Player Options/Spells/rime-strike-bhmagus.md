# Rime Strike
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, M (a ranged weapon)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

As part of the action used to cast this spell, you must make a ranged attack with a weapon against one creature within the spell's range, otherwise the spell fails. On a hit, the target suffers the attack's normal effects, and the air around it turns frigid, halving its movement speed until the end of its turn.

At 5th level the ranged attack deals an extra `d8` cold damage. This damage increases by `d8` again at 11th level (`2d8`) and 17th level (`3d8`).
