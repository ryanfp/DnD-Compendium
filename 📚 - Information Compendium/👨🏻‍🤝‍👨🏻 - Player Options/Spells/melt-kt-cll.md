# Melt
*6th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You point at a creature or Large or smaller object that is not being worn or carried within range, and attempt to make it melt into a puddle of slag. If the target is a creature, it makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, it takes `10d10` fire damage. On a success, it takes half as much damage.

If the target is an object, it takes `20d10` fire damage. If this reduces it to 0 hit points, it is melted into a puddle of slag.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the damage increases by `d10` for each slot level above 6th.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 243*
