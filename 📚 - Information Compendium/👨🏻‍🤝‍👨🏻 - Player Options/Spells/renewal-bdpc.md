# Renewal
*7th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 hour
- **Range:** 300 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a golden hourglass, worth at least 250 gp, filled with platinum dust worth at least 500 gp. The dust is consumed by the casting.)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a point in time no more than 30 days ago. You cause the terrain, structures, and any inanimate nonmagical objects within a 300-foot cube in range to return to the condition they were in at the specified point in time. The renewal occurs using only the resources contained within the area—if objects were removed from the area, they remain missing when the spell completes.

Similarly, objects that were added to the area remain in it.

For example, this spell can be used to rebuild a collapsed or burned down building, restore writing that was scrubbed away from a wall, and/or cause a fallen tree to stand up again. This spell cannot restore life to dead creatures, though it can restore life to non-sentient plant life (like nonmagical trees).

The renewal occurs slowly enough that it cannot trap or damage creatures within the area. Creatures that would be in the way of the renewal are instead safely pushed out of it.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell with an 8th-level spell slot, you can choose a point in time no more than a year ago.

With a 9th-level spell slot, you can choose a point in time no more than a century ago.

*Source: Blazing Dawn Player's Companion p. 54*
