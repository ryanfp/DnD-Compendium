# Earth Ripple
*2nd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You cause the earth to deform at a point you can see within range. Every creature within 10 feet must make a Dexterity saving throw. Choose one of the effects below, affecting each creature which fails its save.

- **Sink.** A creature is pulled into the earth, taking `d8` bludgeoning damage and reducing its movement speed to zero until it or another creature within 5 feet spends an action to dig it free.  
- **Slam.** A creature is pushed 5 feet in a direction of your choice by a wave of earth, taking `2d8` bludgeoning damage and falling prone.  
- **Impale.** A creature takes `4d8` piercing damage.
