## Class Features

### Maverick (Level 3)

> [!quote]  
> 
> I tried being reasonable—I didn't like it.

Rulebreakers, groundbreakers, and, often, heartbreakers, mavericks are those loose cannons who dance on a razor's edge and live life like there's no tomorrow. Armed with a quick wit and quicker trigger, they're out to make a name for themselves no matter the cost—and to the Hells with anyone, or anything, that stands in their way.

### Wild Card (Level 3)

Starting at 3rd level, Your inscrutable manner veils your intentions. You gain proficiency in the [Intimidation](/3-Content/Rules/skills.md#Intimidation) skill. If you already have this proficiency, you gain proficiency in the [Persuasion](/3-Content/Rules/skills.md#Persuasion) skill or one skill of your choice from the gunslinger skill list. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make against an intelligent creature that uses [Intimidation](/3-Content/Rules/skills.md#Intimidation) while you are carrying a weapon.

### Snapshot (Level 3)

Also at 3rd level, When you roll initiative, you can spend 1 grit point as a reaction to draw a firearm and make one ranged attack with it against a creature or object you can see.

### Rough And Tumble (Level 6)

Starting at 6th level, You can use a bonus action to [dash](/3-Content/Rules/actions.md#Dash), and throw an object you are holding against at a creature within 5 feet of you. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Dexterity modifier) or be blinded until the end of your turn.

### Notorious (Level 10)

Starting at 10th level, Dramatic tales of your virtue or villainy have traveled far enough to establish a well-known alias. Whenever you make a Charisma check against a creature that is aware of your alias, you can treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10.

### High Noon (Level 17)

Starting at 17th level, You can target up to six creatures or objects you can see with your Snapshot, making a separate attack roll for each.
