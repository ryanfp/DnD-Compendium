## Class Features

### The Sweet Science (Level 3)

Pugilists who practice the Sweet Science hit hard, fast, and often. This style is sometimes used by fighters who battle for the entertainment of the upper classes, but is just as often practiced in the back alleys and the bars by those whose next breath depends on a knockout.

### Bare Knuckle Boxer (Level 3)

Starting when you choose this fight club at 3rd level, your unarmed strikes score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.

### Cross Counter (Level 3)

Also starting at 3rd level, you can block the brunt of an attack and launch one of your own in the same fluid motion. When you take damage from a melee weapon attack, you can use your reaction and spend 2 [moxie points](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/pugilist-ultimateadventurer.md#Moxie%20(Level%202)) to reduce the damage by `d10` + your Strength modifier + your pugilist level.

If you reduce the damage to 0, you can make an unarmed strike or [pugilist weapon](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/pugilist-ultimateadventurer.md#Fisticuffs%20(Level%201)) attack against a creature within range as part of the same reaction.

### One, Two, Three, Floor (Level 6)

Starting at 6th level, when you use your bonus action to use [the Old One-Two](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/pugilist-ultimateadventurer.md#Moxie%20(Level%202)) and hit the same creature with both attacks, you can spend 1 [moxie point](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/pugilist-ultimateadventurer.md#Moxie%20(Level%202)) to make an additional unarmed strike against that creature as part of the same bonus action. If you hit, the creature is knocked prone in addition to taking damage as normal for your unarmed strike.

### Float Like a Butterfly, Sting Like a Bee (Level 11)

Starting at 11th level, when you reduce damage from an attack to 0 and successfully hit an enemy creature using your [Cross Counter](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/pugilist-the-sweet-science-ultimateadventurer.md#Cross%20Counter%20(Level%203)) feature, you regain 1 expended [moxie point](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/pugilist-ultimateadventurer.md#Moxie%20(Level%202)).

### Knock Out (Level 17)

Starting at 17th level, when you hit with an unarmed strike or [pugilist weapon](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/pugilist-ultimateadventurer.md#Fisticuffs%20(Level%201)), you can spend 1 or more [moxie points](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/pugilist-ultimateadventurer.md#Moxie%20(Level%202)) to try to knock out the opponent instead of dealing damage. Roll `3d12`, plus an additional `2d12` for every moxie point spent after the first, and add your pugilist level to the result. If the total is equal to or greater than the creature's remaining hit points, it is reduced to 0 hit points.
