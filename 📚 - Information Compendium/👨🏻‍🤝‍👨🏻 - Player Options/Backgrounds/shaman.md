# Shaman

*Source: Crystalpunk Campaign Guide p. 92*  

You are a shaman, a protector of the less fortunate in the slums of Prismatic Falls or similar places that are in need of aid. You are a guide for the lost and use magic or herbal remedies to ensure the safety of those who have none other to look out for them.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Two of your choice from Religion, Medicine, Nature, and [Navigation](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/📝%20-%20Rules%20&%20Mechanics/skills.md#Navigation).  
- **Tools.** [Alchemist's supplies](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/items/alchemists-supplies-ccg.md) or [chemist's tools](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/items/chemists-tools-ccg.md).  
- **Languages.** One of your choice  
- **Cantrip (in place of a skill).** spare the dying  
- **Spells (each in place of a tool or language).** protection from evil and good, augury, lesser restoration  
- **Gear Packs.** [Alchemist's supplies](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/items/alchemists-supplies-ccg.md) or [chemist's tools](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/items/chemists-tools-ccg.md), as well as one other pack of your choice.  

## Feature: Returning the Favor

You have provided aid to many in the slums of Prismatic Falls and beyond. Many of those who you have helped will often be willing to return the favor, in whatever small ways they can.
