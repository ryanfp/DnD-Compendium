# Mercenary
*Source: Crystalpunk Campaign Guide p. 91*  


You are a mercenary for hire, a skilled freelancer capable of completing a variety of difficult missions. You've spent a lot of time on such missions and are used to dangerous scenarios. You may specialize in a particular area, such as body guarding, infiltration, or hit jobs, but you'll often take whatever you can get.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Two of your choice among Acrobatics, Athletics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth, and [Streetwise](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/📝%20-%20Rules%20&%20Mechanics/skills.md#Streetwise).  
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Any one type of vehicle (land, water, or air) or [thieves' tools](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/items/thieves-tools-ccg.md).  
- **Languages.** One of your choice  
- **Gear Packs.** [Pathfinding gear](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/items/pathfinding-gear-ccg.md) or a set of [thieves' tools](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/items/thieves-tools-ccg.md), as well as one other pack of your choice.  


## Feature: Freelancer

Through your experience on missions, you've met many others in your line of work. You have connections to other mercenaries and fixers who may aid you with obscure information or recommended you for high paying missions.
