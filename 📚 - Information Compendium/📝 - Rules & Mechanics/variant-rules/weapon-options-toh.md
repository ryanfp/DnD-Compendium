# Weapon Options
*Source: Tome of Heroes p. 249* 

A weapon is more than the damage it deals. In combat, skilled warriors use their weapons to confuse, disorient, and disadvantage their enemies before moving in for the kill. This section offers unique options for using weapons in ways other than dealing damage, giving different weapons distinct impacts on the battlefield.

A character can use a weapon option only if the character is wielding and is proficient with a weapon that meets the weapon option's prerequisites. Some weapons can be used to perform certain weapon options even if the weapon doesn't ordinarily meet the option's prerequisites, as noted in the weapon option's entry. If an option requires a creature to make an ability check or saving throw, the DC equals 8 + the attacker's proficiency bonus + the attacker's Strength or Dexterity modifier (attacker's choice).

Unless specified otherwise, a weapon used to perform a weapon option has only the option's listed effect and doesn't deal normal weapon damage. If a weapon option instructs a character to make an attack with the weapon, and the character can make multiple attacks with the Attack action, the weapon option replaces only one of those attacks.

At the GM's discretion, some weapon options might not work against certain creatures. For example, the trip weapon option might not work against a creature without discernable legs, such as an ooze, or that is anchored or attached to the ground in some way, such as a tree with animated limbs.

## Unarmed Strikes

Unarmed strikes can be used to perform any weapon option that requires a melee weapon, provided the unarmed strike meets the option's other prerequisites. For example, a human monk can't perform the [hamstring](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/📝%20-%20Rules%20&%20Mechanics/actions.md#Hamstring) weapon option with an unarmed strike, but a [catfolk](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/races/catfolk-toh.md) with claws that deal slashing damage can.
