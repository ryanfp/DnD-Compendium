# Potent Focus
*Source: Crystalpunk Campaign Guide p. 101* 

When a spellcaster wields their spellcasting focus in two hands, your GM can rule that its grant them a 50% range increase on spells they cast through it, up to a maximum of a 30 feet increase. This should not apply to spells with a range of Self or Touch.
