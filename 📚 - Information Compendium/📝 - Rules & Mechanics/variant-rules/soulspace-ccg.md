# Soulspace
*Source: Crystalpunk Campaign Guide p. 171* 

Throughout the entirety of the gargantuan Prismatic Falls lies a parallel world hidden to the naked eye—a world with the same structures, but bathed in an ethereal gray, where the souls of all those in Prismatic Falls can be seen clearly. This is Soulspace—a name given to the section of the Ethereal Plane that lies over Prismatic Falls. It is a tangential dimension where richer citizens in Prismatic Falls go to live second lives, make secret deals, and entertain themselves. In Soulspace, the concepts of material and thought converge; allowing those inside to alter their appearances entirely and subtly affect the ethereal world around them using their imaginations.

## Entering Soulspace

In Prismatic Falls, Soulspace is accessed through the [ethereal jaunt](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/spells/ethereal-jaunt-ccg.md) spell. Regular citizens access this spell through Manateks or through Autospells that exist with locations called Ether Centres, which are stores that sell the casting of ethereal jaunt. The spell usually costs around 100 gp, and lasts for up to 4 hours.

The souls of those on the Material Plane exist within Soulspace in the same location as they are in the material. You normally can't interact with these souls within Soulspace, though some specialized agents have trained to do so (see the Lost Soul warlock patron).

## Second Lives

In Soulspace, one can move around the space freely—gravity does not exist in Soulspace, and those inside move around using their force of will. In addition to that, those in Soulspace can freely **alter their own appearance** (but not their abilities) using their imaginations, allowing anyone to be an anonymous member of this parallel community. Many take on alter egos and vibrant personas, creating a new person to live as; living out fantastical lives that they simply could not in the real world.

## Ethereal Structures

In addition to altering appearances, the very fabric of reality can be altered with one's imagination. While the effect is quite small for only one person, many people can, and have, come together to make grand structures purely out of their imaginations—which even includes color and moving objects, normally not present in the plane. This has transformed Soulspace into a vibrant community of extravagant artwork, interactive games, and grand structures; all created by the efforts of an anonymous community and existing on top of the already present locations and buildings projected up from Prismatic Falls. There are, however, threats to this community—groups of anonymous citizens who aim to tear down the ethereal structures in Soulspace, coming together to destroy what was made. These ethereal terrorists have varied motivations and are often hated by many who live much of their lives within Soulspace.

## Secret Deals

With Soulspace allowing complete anonymity, many secret deals are made within it, with each participant going to a set location marked on the Material Plane. The [inscribe](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/spells/inscribe-ccg.md) spell is also sometimes used in these deals, to mark some writing in a hidden location, where another will come to find it later. These messages can also be set to be viewed in the Material Plane.

## Action in Soulspace

While in Soulspace, one's features are mimicked within the plane—they are able to fight and cast spells as usual, and their hit points function as normal. (though they float around instead of walking). Some of their physical abilities, however, are bound by their mental fortitude. Whenever a creature in Soulspace makes an ability check or saving throw using Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution, they use their highest ability modifier out of Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma instead.

## 'Hacking' the Material

Affecting the material world while in the Ethereal Plane is normally impossible, but as the border to the Ethereal Plane is so weak in Prismatic Falls, it is possible, albeit difficult.

While in Soulspace, a creature can attempt to pierce the ethereal veil—making an Intelligence (Arcana) or Intelligence (Religion) check against a DC that is based on the intensity of magic where they are trying to pierce into, where more intense is easier. On a success, they pierce into the Material Plane for a brief period, able to interact with their surroundings for the next 5 minutes. On a failure, they take `2d6` psychic damage. In either case, they cannot attempt to pierce the veil again for the next hour.

During those 5 minutes, a creature can interact with the Material Plane as if they were on it, though they appear as a ghostly figure and any objects they take do not come back with them. This usually gives them enough time to cast a few spells, attempt to pick a lock, read some secret documents, or retrieve some secret information from objects that have had the [imbue knowledge](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/spells/imbue-knowledge-ccg.md) spell cast on them. Certain adept agents—called Runners—are specially trained to be ethereal scouts in Soulspace using this method, gathering information and aiding allies in the real world wherever they can.

There are many defenses used against this method. Mercenaries called Seekers and Phantoms are used as well as spells such as Private Sanctum, which stops creatures from piercing the veil in that area. The most secure locations in Prismatic Falls have such ethereal defenses, and they are tough to break through with this method.

## Threats in Soulspace

There are a few threats in Soulspace. One is ghosts, though they tend to not stray near any of the created structures. Some vengeful ones are still a threat to those within the plane. The second are Ethereal Agents, which is the name given to trained mercenaries that defend material locations against Soulspace and those within it. Those that stay in the Material Plane and see into the ethereal are called Seekers, and those that venture into Soulspace are called Phantoms. For Seekers, use the Augmented stat block (choosing the Otherworldly Vision upgrade for them), and for Phantoms, use the Street Warrior stat block.

## Living in Soulspace

Some spend so much time in Soulspace that one could say that they live there. The effect on one's body from doing so is brutal—a lack of activity can cause it to deteriorate at a rapid rate, making common extended visits to Soulspace a large health risk. Many take this risk however, as they enjoy themselves much more in Soulspace than on the material. There are even some who purposely let their material bodies die while in the ethereal jaunt spell, allowing them to stay in Soulspace forever.

## Missions in Soulspace

Missions in Soulspace usually involve intrigue or are a part of a larger endeavor, often a heist, which is taking place at the same time within the Material Plane. Here is a mission example:

**Ethereal Revenge.** A [nephilim](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/races/nephilim-ccg.md) named Indigo who is an Ethereal Artist has angered a Kingmaker gang led by a half-elf named Switchblade in the material world. Indigo had made artwork depicting the Kingmaker as brutal barbarians, and the gang retaliated by destroying all of Indigo's artwork in Soulspace. Indigo hires the players to deal with this gang in whatever method they see fit—though the gang's base of operation is a mystery. Indigo knows what Switchblade's avatar looks like in Soulspace, and asks the players to hunt them down in Soulspace to find out where they are hiding.
