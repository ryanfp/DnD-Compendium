# Optional Class Features for Crystalpunk
*Source: Crystalpunk Campaign Guide p. 44* 

The following optional class features are intended for use with characters in the Crystalpunk world.

## Shotgun Slayer

*Optional 1st-level barbarian feature*

You can use Strength rather than Dexterity for the attack and damage rolls of ranged weapons you are proficient in, provided you make the attack against a target within 20 feet of you. If you make a ranged attack using Strength this way, it receives the benefits of your Rage and Brutal Critical features as if it was a melee weapon. It is also eligible for the advantage granted by your Reckless Attack, provided that the attack is made against a target within 5 feet of you.

If you take this optional feature, your barbarian hit die is reduced to a d10, which reduces your hit points at 1st level and at higher levels to that of the fighter class.

## Holy Sniper

*Optional 1st-level paladin feature*

At 2nd level when you choose your Fighting Style, Archery is added to your available options.

Also, you can now only use your Divine Smite feature with ranged weapon attacks, rather than with melee weapon attacks. The extra radiant damage dealt when you use the feature is also adjusted—it is now `2d6` for a 1st-level spell slot, plus `d6` for each spell level higher than 1st, to a maximum of `5d6`. The damage increases by `d6` if the target is an undead or a fiend, to a maximum of `6d6`.

Additionally, when you reach 11th level, your Improved Divine Smite feature now only deals its extra radiant damage on ranged weapon attacks, rather than on melee weapon attacks, and the extra damage radiant damage dealt is adjusted to `d6`.

## Mystic Sight

*Optional 3rd-level ranger feature, which replaces the Primeval Awareness feature*

At 3rd level, you learn an additional spell of your choice from any class. The spell you choose must be a divination spell that is of a level you can cast. When you reach 5th, 9th, 13th, and 17th level, you can choose one additional spell for this feature, which must abide by the above prerequisites. These spells count as ranger spells for you, but don't count against the number of ranger spells you know.

You can cast each of the spells you learn with this feature once without expending a spell slot. Once you cast a spell in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.

## Urban Explorer

*Optional 1st-level ranger feature*

When choosing a favored terrain for your Natural Explorer feature, High-Rises, Streets, and Slums are added to your available options. These new options represent the various altitudes of the city of Prismatic Falls—with the High-Rises at the top, and the Slums at the bottom.
