# Species
*Source: Valda's Spire of Secrets: Player Pack* 

Fantasy settings are defined perhaps most clearly by their peoples: not merely humans with the diversity of our world, but fantastic species of nonhumans, the likes of which solely exist in the realms of imagination. These vary from the remarkably humanlike, such as elves and halflings, to the colorful and imaginative, such as dragonborn. However, fantastical settings often include more eclectic species, drawn from the well of monstrous foes that bear intelligence and guile.

This section presents two new species: the toylike Geppettin and the Mandrake plant-people.
