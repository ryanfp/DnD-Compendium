# Feats
*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies* 

## Active Martial Feats

Active Martial Feats are a group of feats that have the unique aspect of granting a character a new way to act (generally in combat). These abilities, which usually recharge on a short rest, allow characters to perform extraordinary feats, akin to a sort of 'martial spell.' They scale with your proficiency bonus, remaining relevant throughout the whole game, and usually don't require a full action. Instead, they integrate either as part of the Attack action, a bonus action, reaction, or even in some cases not requiring an action at all.

These are good options for characters that want to have more active and tactical options in combat. They're particularly well suited for martial characters, especially when paired with the Variant Martial Progression rules that grant more feats to those classes.

> [!note] Active Martial Feat Save DC
> 
> Any time an active martial feat requires a saving throw, the **DC is 8 + your Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice) + your proficiency bonus**.

## Weapon Feats

Weapon feats are way for characters to gain special powers from the type of weapon they wield, gaining always effective benefits while wielding that weapon. They represent more consistent and passive bonuses than Active Martial Feats for player characters that prefer consistent mastery over bursts of strength.

They are good options for characters that want to make a weapon part of their identity or have more flexible ways to attack on their turn consistently.

These are meant compliment and work in conjunction with core 5e feats of a similar vein, and are balanced around a similar consideration to the strength of feats that specialize in polearms, heavy weapons, and other weapons.

## Combat Style Feats

Combat Style feats are similar to the weapon feats, but represent broader categories of fighting rather than specific weapons. They exist to supplement existing feats that empower certain weapon properties, rather than types.

## Mythic Path Feats

Mystic path feats are feats that grant you a special power in a particular affinity. They are similar to active martial feats, but while those are potentially superhuman, these feats are supernatural, no longer representing feats of strength or speed, but of unique power and affinity.

> [!note] Mystic Path Modifier
> 
> You can choose your Mystic Path modifier when you have one or more Mystic Path feats. Your Mystic Path Modifier can be any ability score modifier besides Constitution. Your **Mystic Path DC is calculated as 8 + your Mystic Path Modifier + your proficiency bonus**. Any spells you gain through a Mystic Path feat use your Mystic Path modifier as its spellcasting modifier
