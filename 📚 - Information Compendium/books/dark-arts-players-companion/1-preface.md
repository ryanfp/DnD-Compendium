# Preface
*Source: Dark Arts Player's Companion, p. 2* 

In many adventures and campaigns, necromancy and similar magic often plays a key role in the story. This supplement aims to give both players and DMs more options in these types of campaigns by giving them more races, subclasses, spells, and creatures for them to work with. If your dungeon master allows, these options can be used in any of your campaigns.

## SPENDING HIT POINTS

Some of the features and spells in this companion allow you to spend hit points to augment their effect. In 5E terms, spending hit points is equivalent to taking necrotic damage that ignores resistance and immunity, with the necrotic damage you take being equal to the amount you choose to spend.

## ON THE COVER

Performing an ancient blood ritual, the Circle of Twilight druid channels energy from the source of fresh blood to cast a blood magic ritual, in this scene illustrated by Felipe Escobar Bravo.
