# Chapter 1: Races
*Source: Dark Arts Player's Companion, p. 3* 

This chapter presents three new Races to supplement those in the Player's Handbook: [Mummies](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/races/mummy-dapc.md), [Wights](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/races/wight-dapc.md), and [Shadar-kai](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/races/shadar-kai-dapc.md). Mummies and Wights are converted from creatures in the Monster Manual, and should be treated in the same way as monstrous races from Volo's Guide to Monsters. The Shadar-kai are a race native to the toxic darkness of the shadowfell. These new options are available when you make a character, provided that your DM allows them in your campaign.
