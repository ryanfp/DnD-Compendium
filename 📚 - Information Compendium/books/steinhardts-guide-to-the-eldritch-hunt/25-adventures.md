## A Note on the Adventures

Below are five unique adventures designed to expand upon the lore established in the main text. Each is constructed to utilize the new rules and mechanics introduced in this guide, providing example explorations into various aspects of Luyarnha's mythos. In the lore of Steinhardt's Guide to the Eldritch Hunt, the [Eldritch Beckoning counter](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/tables/eldritch-beckoning-counter-sgtteh.md) [...] ^[The following text has been removed with this homebrew: (Page 283)] never rose above 399. This counter is a central component to the world's dynamics, significantly influencing how the environment reacts and evolves. It's crucial to understand that the shift in the counter can drastically transform the world, affecting the way the characters perceive and interact with their surroundings.

Each adventure takes place in a designated point in the city's history and has a corresponding [Eldritch Beckoning counter](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/tables/eldritch-beckoning-counter-sgtteh.md) state [...] ^[The following text has been removed with this homebrew: (Page 283)]. These adventures are standalone scenarios and are not intrinsically linked to each other. However, GM can adapt and integrate these adventures into a broader campaign narrative if they see fit to do so.

Here's a brief outline of those for each adventure:

## Streets of Terror:

This adventure serves as an potential introduction to the world, unfolding immediately after the events documented in the book's lore. The [Eldritch Beckoning counter](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/tables/eldritch-beckoning-counter-sgtteh.md) is set between 300 and 399, depicting a city just beginning to succumb to corruption.

> [!note]
> See the Streets of Terror adventure.

## The Remedy:

Set approximately five years after the canonical events, "The Remedy" portrays a city deep in the throes of chaos. The [Eldritch Beckoning counter](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/tables/eldritch-beckoning-counter-sgtteh.md) for this adventure ranges from 700 to 799, reflecting a city spiraling further into madness.

> [!note]
> See the The Remedy adventure.

## The Vile Village:

This adventure's timeline is flexible, occurring anywhere from a few days to several years after the canonical events. Its [Eldritch Beckoning counter](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/tables/eldritch-beckoning-counter-sgtteh.md) ranges from 300 to 399, Luyarnha's baseline state.

> [!note]
> See the The Vile Village adventure.

## Echoes of Madness:

This adventure is situated any time after the 33rd Hunt. The state of the [Eldritch Beckoning counter](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/tables/eldritch-beckoning-counter-sgtteh.md) is not integral to this scenario and can be adjusted according to the GM's discretion.

> [!note]
> See the Echoes of Madness adventure.

## Deicide:

Unfolding in the distant future, "Deicide" portrays a Luyarnha teetering on the brink of collapse, decades into the Scourge's reign. The [Eldritch Beckoning counter](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/tables/eldritch-beckoning-counter-sgtteh.md) is set between 900 and 999, but with the Glacial Moon alone dominating the skies.

> [!note]
> See the Deicide adventure.
