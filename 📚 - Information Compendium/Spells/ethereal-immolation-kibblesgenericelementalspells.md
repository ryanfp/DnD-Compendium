# Ethereal Immolation
*2nd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You shroud a target in visible ethereal power that reacts violently to the presence of magic. For the duration of the spell, if the target casts a spell, it takes `d12` force damage per level of spell slot spent (`d12` for cantrips). If the target ends their turn while concentrating on a spell, they take `d4` force damage.

*Source: Kibbles' Generic Elemental Spells p. 14*
