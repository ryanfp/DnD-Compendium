# Spatial Swap
*4th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You switch locations with a creature you can see. If there is insufficient space for either creature at the other's location, the spell fails. If the target is unwilling, it can make a Charisma saving throw. On a success, the spell fails.

**Classes**: [Occultist](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md)
