# Shield of Faith, Mass
*4th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a small piece of parchment with some holy text written on it)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

This spell functions like shield of faith, except that it affects you and up to six of your allies within range.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
