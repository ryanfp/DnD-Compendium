# Acid Bubble
*1st-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a bubble of acid that floats to the target point before bursting a shower of acid. All creatures and objects within a 5-foot-radius sphere must make a Dexterity saving throw. On failure they take `3d4` acid damage, or half as much on a successful save.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by `2d4` for each slot level above 1st.

*Source: Kibbles' Generic Elemental Spells p. 5*
