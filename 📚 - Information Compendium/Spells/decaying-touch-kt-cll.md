# Decaying Touch
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (powdered remains of a dead animal)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You wreath your hand in necrotic decay, causing anything you touch to wither and die. Make a melee spell attack against a creature or object within range. On a hit, the target takes `d6` necrotic damage and starts to flake and decay. The first time it takes damage from another source before the start of your next turn, it takes an additional `d6` necrotic damage. Targets immune to diseases are immune to this effect.

Both the initial and secondary damage of the spell increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`).

**Classes**: [Spellblade](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-spellblade.md); [Occultist](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md)
