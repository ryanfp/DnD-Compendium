# Spirit Echo
*2nd-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You summon a ghostly echo of yourself. It shares your space and acts automatically, mirroring your actions. The first time on your turn you make a weapon attack, it attacks the same target. It makes a separate attack roll using your attack modifier. On a hit, roll your attack's normal damage; the target takes force damage equal to half the result.
