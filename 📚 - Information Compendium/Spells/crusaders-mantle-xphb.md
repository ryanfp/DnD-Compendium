# Crusader's Mantle
*3rd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Emanation)
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You radiate a magical aura in a 30-foot Emanation. While in the aura, you and your allies each deal an extra `d4` Radiant damage when hitting with a weapon or an [Unarmed Strike](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/📝%20-%20Rules%20&%20Mechanics/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md).
