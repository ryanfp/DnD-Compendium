# Blade Ward
*cantrip, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round

You extend your hand and trace a sigil of warding in the air. Until the end of your next turn, you have resistance against bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage dealt by weapon attacks.

**At 5th Level.** You can cast this spell on a creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet of you. 

**At 11th Level.** You can ignore the verbal component of the spell.

**At 17th Level.** When you cast this spell on another creature, you also gain its benefits.

*Source: EvolvingCantrips p. 218*
