# Pincer
*6th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You force your enemies into a tight spot. Choose two points you can see within range. The points must be at least 30 feet apart from each other. Each point then emits a thunderous boom in a 30-foot cone in the direction of the other point. The boom is audible out to 300 feet.

Each creature within a cone must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes `5d10` thunder damage and is pushed up to 10 feet away from the point that emitted the cone. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't pushed. Objects that aren't being worn or carried within each cone are automatically pushed.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the damage increases by `d10` for each slot level above 6th.

*Source: Tome of Heroes p. 298*
