# Guiding Snarl
*5th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You conjure a trickster spirit who distracts an enemy, allowing your ally to strike true. The ally can reroll the triggering attack roll with advantage. If the attack hits, it deals an extra `2d10` psychic damage.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the psychic damage increases by `d10` for each slot level above 5th.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
