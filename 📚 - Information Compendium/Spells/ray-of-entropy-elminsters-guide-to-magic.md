# Ray of Entropy
*6th-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You emit a ray of negative energy from your outstretched hand at a target within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. If the attack hits, the creature has disadvantage on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws and ability checks for the duration of the spell.

An affected creature can attempt a Charisma saving throw at the end of each of its turns, and on a success, the spell ends.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
