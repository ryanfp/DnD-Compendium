# Waterspout
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 10 feet
- **Components:** S, M (a waterskin filled with water, worth at least 1sp)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A blast of water leaps from its vessel to strike a foe and pull them off their feet. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or else take `d6` bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.

This spell's damage increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`).

*Source: Tasha's Crucible of Everything Else - Volume 1 p. 65*
