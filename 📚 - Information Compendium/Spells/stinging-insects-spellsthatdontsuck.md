# Stinging Insects
*cantrip, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (six sugar crystals)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Replaces:** Infestation

You cause a cloud of biting, stinging insects to appear near one creature you can see within range. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On failure, it takes `d4` piercing damage and `d4` poison damage and moves 5 feet in a direction of your choice. The creature doesn't move into obviously dangerous ground, such as a fire or a pit.

The spell's damage increases by `d4` when you reach 5th level (`2d4+2d4`), 11th level (`3d4+3d4`), and 17th level (`4d4+4d4`).

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 41*
