# Phantasmal Horror
*4th-level, Illusion*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Replaces:** Phantasmal Killer

You select a creature you can see within range and create an illusion of its worst nightmares, which only it can see. At the start of each of the target's turns, it must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it takes `4d10` psychic damage and is frightened. On a success, the spell ends.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the damage increases by `d10` for each slot level above 4th.

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 33*
