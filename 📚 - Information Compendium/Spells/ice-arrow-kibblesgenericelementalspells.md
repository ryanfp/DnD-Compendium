# Ice Arrow
*1st-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You conjure a shard of ice before hurling it at a target you can see within range. Make a ranged spell attack. On hit, the target takes `2d8` + your spellcasting ability modifier cold damage.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by `d8` for each level above 1st.

*Source: Kibbles' Generic Elemental Spells p. 6*
