# Karmic Reflection
*1st-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a broken shard of a mirror)
- **Duration:** 1 round

You place a magical binding on a creature you can see within range. Until the start of your next turn, anytime that creature deals damage with its action, bonus action, or reaction, it takes radiant damage equal to the damage dealt, up to a maximum of 10 damage. The target is aware of the spell's effects. After reflecting 10 damage, the spell ends.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the maximum damage increases by 10 for each spell slot level above 2nd.
