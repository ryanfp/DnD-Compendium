# Flight of the Dragon
*4th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, M (a dragon's wing claw)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

When you cast this spell, small dragon wings grow from your back and you gain a flying speed of 60 feet for the duration. If your game uses the optional encumbrance rules, you cannot fly with this spell while encumbered.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 49*
