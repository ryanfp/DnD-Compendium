# Returning Weapon
*1st-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 24 hours

You touch a weapon and grant it the thrown (20/60) property. If it already has the thrown property, its range increases by 20/60. For the duration, it reappears in the thrower's hand after it's thrown as part of an attack.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 249*
