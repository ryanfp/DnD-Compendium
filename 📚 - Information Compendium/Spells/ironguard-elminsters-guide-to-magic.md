# Ironguard
*7th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a tiny shield of wood, glass, or crystal)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

This spell functions like lesser ironguard, except the person touched is immune to both nonmagical and magically imbued metals.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
