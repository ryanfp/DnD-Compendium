# Enthrall
*2nd-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You weave a distracting string of words, causing creatures of your choice that you can see within range to make a Wisdom saving throw. Any creature you or your companions are fighting automatically succeeds on this save. On a failed save, a target has a -10 penalty to Wisdom ([Perception](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/📝%20-%20Rules%20&%20Mechanics/skills.md#Perception)) checks and Passive [Perception](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/📝%20-%20Rules%20&%20Mechanics/skills.md#Perception) until the spell ends.

**Classes**: [Bard](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Warlock](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-warlock.md)
