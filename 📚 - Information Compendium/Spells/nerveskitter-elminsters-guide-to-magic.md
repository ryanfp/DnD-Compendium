# Nerveskitter
*1st-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You energize one ally with a jittery burst of power, speeding up its senses and reactions to the point of near precognition. The subject makes its initiative roll with advantage.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
