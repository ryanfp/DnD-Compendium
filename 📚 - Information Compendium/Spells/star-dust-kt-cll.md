# Star Dust
*2nd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You evoke a burst of brilliant particles of force energy sweeping out in a 30-cone originating from you. Each creature in the radius takes `3d4` force damage and the next attack roll made against it before the start of your next turn has advantage.
