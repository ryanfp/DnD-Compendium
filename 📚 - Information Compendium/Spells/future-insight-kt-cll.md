# Future Insight
*1st-level, None*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** 10 minutes

Roll `3d4` or `d12` (your choice) and record the results. For the duration, you can expend one of these dice to add or subtract it from any attack roll, saving throw, or ability check made by a creature within 60 feet of you. You must expend the die after the roll is made, but before you know the outcome of the roll. Once all the dice have been expended, the spell ends.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 271*
