# Hellish Rebuke
*1st-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

The creature that damaged you is momentarily surrounded by green flames. It makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking `2d10` Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by `d10` for each spell slot level above 1.

**Classes**: [Bard](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Warlock](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-warlock.md)
