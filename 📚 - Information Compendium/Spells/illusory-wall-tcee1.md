# Illusory Wall
*2nd-level, Illusion*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a mirror)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You create an illusion of a vertical surface that is up to 60 feet long, 10 feet high, and 1 foot thick. The wall can include gaps and openings such as windows, peepholes, and doorways, but they are considered to be holes in the illusion as well. You can use a bonus action to move some or all of the gaps and openings, create new ones, or close the existing ones, but remember that the illusion can only be 1 foot across at its thickest point.

Physical interaction with the wall reveals it to be an illusion, and a creature that uses its action to examine the wall can determine that it is an illusion with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC. If a creature discerns the illusion for what it is, the creature can see through the wall. Otherwise, the opaque sections block line of sight.

*Source: Tasha's Crucible of Everything Else - Volume 1 p. 63*
