# Form of the Elements
*9th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

For the duration of the spell, you gain the benefits of the [form of fire](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/spells/form-of-fire-kt-cll.md), [form of ice](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/spells/form-of-ice-kt-cll.md), [form of stone](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/spells/form-of-stone-kt-cll.md), [form of water](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/spells/form-of-water-kt-cll.md), and [form of wind](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/spells/form-of-wind-kt-cll.md) spells.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 231*
