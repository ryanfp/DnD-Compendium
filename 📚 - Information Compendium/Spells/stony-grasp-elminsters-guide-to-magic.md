# Stony Grasp
*3rd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a miniature hand sculpted from stone)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

This spell functions like Maximillian's earthen grasp except the hand is made of stone, and it deals `3d6` bludgeoning damage on both the initial grab and the crushing damage.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the amount of bludgeoning damage increases by `d6` for every two slot levels above 3rd.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
