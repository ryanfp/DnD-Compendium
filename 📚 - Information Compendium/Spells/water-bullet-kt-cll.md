# Water Bullet
*cantrip, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a compressed bead of water and fire it at a creature you can see within range. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes `d6` + your spellcasting ability modifier piercing damage.

This spell's damage increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`).

**Classes**: [Spellblade](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-spellblade.md); [Occultist](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md)

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 267*
