# Gale Bolt
*1st-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A blast of concentrated wind streaks toward a creature of your choice within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes `2d8` bludgeoning damage and if it is Large or smaller is knocked 10 feet away from you.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell lot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by `d8` for each level above 1st.

**Classes**: [Spellblade](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-spellblade.md); [Occultist](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md)
