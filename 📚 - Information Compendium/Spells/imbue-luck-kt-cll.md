# Imbue Luck
*2nd-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a four leaf clover)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a weapon or worn item and imbue luck into it for the duration. If you target a weapon, its wielder can roll an additional `d20` when making an attack (deciding after the roll, but before the outcome is determined). The creature chooses which of the `d20`s to use.

If you target a worn item, the wearer can roll a `d20` when attacked, choosing whether the attack uses the original d20 result or this one.

After the extra die is rolled, the spell ends.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 237*
