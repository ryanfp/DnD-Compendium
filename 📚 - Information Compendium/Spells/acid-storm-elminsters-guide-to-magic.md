# Acid Storm
*6th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a flask of acid)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A cloud of swirling acid rain engulfs the area in a 20foot radius by 20-foot high cylinder. Each creature within the cylinder must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes `10d8` acid damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 20*
