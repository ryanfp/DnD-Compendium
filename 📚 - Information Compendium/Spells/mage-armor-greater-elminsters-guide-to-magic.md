# Mage Armor, Greater
*3rd-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 8 hours

This spell functions exactly like mage armor, except that it does not require a material component, you can only cast it on yourself, and your base Armor Class becomes 15 + your Dexterity modifier for the duration.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
