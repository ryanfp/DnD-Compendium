# Lesser Restoration
*2nd-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature and can end either one disease or one condition afflicting it. The condition can be [blinded](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/📝%20-%20Rules%20&%20Mechanics/conditions.md#Blinded), [deafened](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/📝%20-%20Rules%20&%20Mechanics/conditions.md#Deafened), [paralyzed](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/📝%20-%20Rules%20&%20Mechanics/conditions.md#Paralyzed), or [poisoned](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/📝%20-%20Rules%20&%20Mechanics/conditions.md#Poisoned).
