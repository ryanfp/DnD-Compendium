# Fireball
*3rd-level, Evocation*  
![](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/spells/img/fireball.webp#right)

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a ball of bat guano and sulfur)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A bright streak flashes from you to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into a fiery explosion. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on that point makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking `8d6` Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried start [burning](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/traps-hazards/burning-xphb.md).

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by `d6` for each spell slot level above 3.
