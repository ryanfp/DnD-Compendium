# Minor Drain
*1st-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Replaces:** Ray of Sickness (with [Touch of Filth](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/spells/touch-of-filth-spellsthatdontsuck.md))

You send a pulse of necrotic energy towards a target, sucking its life essence away. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes `3d6` necrotic damage, and you gain half the damage dealt as temporary hit points. These temporary hit points fade after 1 minute.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by `d6` for each slot level above 1st.

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 30*
