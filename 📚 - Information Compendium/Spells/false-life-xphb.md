# False Life
*1st-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a drop of alcohol)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You gain `2d4 + 4` [Temporary Hit Points](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/📝%20-%20Rules%20&%20Mechanics/variant-rules/temporary-hit-points-xphb.md).
