# Indomitability
*5th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 12 hours

The target you touch is protected from the next attack that would take it to 0 hit points. An attack must deal at least 1 hit point of damage to trigger this spell's effect. The recipient is reduced to 1 hit point instead, but is otherwise unharmed. If the attack incapacitates the victim another way, then the spell has no effect.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
