# Aether Lance
*3rd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Line)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You gather raw aether in your hand and expel it in a lance of power forming a line 30 foot long and 5 foot wide. Each creature in a line takes `8d4` force damage.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by `d4` for each slot level above 3rd.

*Source: Kibbles' Generic Elemental Spells p. 14*
