# Cone of Flame
*1st-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (15-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Replaces:** Burning Hands

Flames shoot forth from your fingertips. Each creature in a 15-foot cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes `3d6` fire damage on a failure, or half as much damage on a success.

The flames ignite any flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by `d6` and the range of the cone increases by 5 feet for each slot level above 1st.

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 11*
