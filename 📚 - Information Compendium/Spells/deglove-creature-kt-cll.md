# Deglove Creature
*5th-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a finger bone)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You point at a creature within range, and attempt to make its skeleton rip free of its body. The creature must make a Constitution saving throw. The target takes `7d10 + 7` necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

If this damage kills the target, its flesh sloughs off its skeleton, collapsing into a pile. Its skeleton emerges and becomes a Skeleton, hostile to all living creatures, and attacks the closest target.

**Classes**: [Spellblade](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-spellblade.md); [Occultist](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md)
