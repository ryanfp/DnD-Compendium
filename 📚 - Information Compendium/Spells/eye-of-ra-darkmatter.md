# Eye of Ra
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** M (a holy symbol)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A beam of furious radiance erupts from your right eye, directed at a creature you can see within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes `d8` radiant damage and can't take the Hide action until the end of your next turn.

This spell creates an additional beam and deals more damage at higher levels. At 5th level, you fire a second beam originating from your left eye. Your first beam's damage increases by `d8` (`2d8`) when you reach 11th level, and your second beam's damage increases by `d8` (`2d8`) when you reach 17th level.

*Source: Dark Matter p. 269*
