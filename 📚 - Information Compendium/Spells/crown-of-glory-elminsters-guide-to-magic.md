# Crown of Glory
*8th-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (10-foot Radius)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

When you cast this spell, the magnificence of your deity emanates from you in an aura that makes you appear larger than life and bolsters nearby allies. For the duration, you and allies that start their turns within a 10-foot radius sphere centered on you are immune to being frightened and have advantage on all attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws while within the aura created by this spell.

If a creature leaves the area of the spell, it immediately loses all these benefits until it starts its turn within the aura once again. In addition, when you cast this spell, each ally within 10 feet of you gains 10 temporary hit points.

The aura is centered on the caster, who can only move up to half speed while the aura is active.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 38*
