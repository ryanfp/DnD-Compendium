# Resistance, Superior
*6th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a miniature cloak)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

This spell functions like resistance, except the target gains a bonus of `d8` on a number of saving throws it makes during the duration. After it has added this bonus a number of times equal to your spellcasting ability modifier, the spell ends.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
