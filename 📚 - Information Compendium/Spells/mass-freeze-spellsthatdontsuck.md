# Mass Freeze
*5th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (60-foot Sphere)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Replaces:** Freezing Sphere (in part)

You choose a point within range and cause all the warmth to vanish from its vicinity. Each creature in a 20-foot radius sphere centered on that point must make a Constitution saving throw. A target takes `4d8` cold damage on a failure, or half as much damage on a success.

If a creature fails the saving throw, it is frozen in place, locked down by sheets of ice. It takes `d8` cold damage at the start of each of its turns, and is restrained. It may reattempt the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 30*
