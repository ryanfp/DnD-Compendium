# Resilient Friendship
*cantrip, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 5 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You magically assist a creature within range, granting it the benefits of the Help action. If the target you helped successfully accomplishes the task by the start of your next turn, you gain `d4` temporary hit points, which last for 1 hour.

The number of temporary hit points you gain increases when you reach 5th level (`d6`), 11th level (`d8`), and 17th level (`d10`).

*Source: Obojima: Tales from the Tall Grass p. 188*
