# Orb of Cold
*4th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You create an orb of cold roughly 3 inches wide that shoots from the palm of your hand toward a target within range. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the orb explodes, and the target takes `8d6` cold damage.

In addition, a creature struck by the orb must make a Constitution saving throw, or its speed is reduced by half until the end of its next turn.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the amount of cold damage increases by `d6` for each slot level above 4th.

"There are other versions of this spell that conjure orbs of fire, thunder, lightning, or acid. They all hurt."

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
