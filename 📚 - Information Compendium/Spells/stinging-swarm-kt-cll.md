# Stinging Swarm
*4th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You conjure a magical swarm of flying insects that fill a 5-foot cube within range. The swarm cannot be damaged or destroyed. For the duration, you can use a bonus action to command it to move up to 30 feet. If the swarm enters another creature's space, it stops and stings the creature repeatedly, and cannot be moved until the start of your next turn. The creature takes `2d4` piercing damage and must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take `6d4` poison damage.
