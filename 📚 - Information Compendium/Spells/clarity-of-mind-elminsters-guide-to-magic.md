# Clarity of Mind
*3rd-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You touch a target and grant it extraordinary focus for the duration. The target has advantage on Wisdom and Intelligence saving throws against spells. 

In addition, the target does not have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks in areas that are lightly obscured, or on attack rolls against creatures that are heavily obscured.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 35*
