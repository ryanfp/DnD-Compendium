# Gift
*1st-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a creature you can see within range. You take `d20` necrotic damage, and the target regains hit points equal to the amount of damage you took.

*Source: Obojima: Tales from the Tall Grass p. 185*
