# Finger Guns
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You extend your forefinger and thumb, a dangerous gesture mimicking a gun. For the duration, you can use your action to make a spell attack roll against one creature you can see within 60 feet, dealing `d8` force damage on a hit.

Your finger gun is considered to be a blaster (but not magical technology) for spells and effects which influence blasters.

The spell's damage increases by `d8` when you reach 5th level (`2d8`), 11th level (`3d8`), and 17th level (`4d8`).

*Source: Dark Matter p. 269*
