# Shifting Paths
*8th-level, Illusion (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 10 minutes unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 8 hours

This spell hides a path or road that you choose, while also creating an illusory path that starts at a point you choose within range. The illusory path continues in a direction you choose for up to a number of miles equal to 2 x your spellcasting ability modifier. This illusory path avoids obstacles, and does not provide bridges, stairs, ladders, ramps, or other methods to travel through impassable terrain. The path will not cross cliffs or rivers more than four feet deep.

A creature following the original path must make a Wisdom saving throw when it comes to the start of the illusory path. On a failed save, the creature follows the illusory path as if it were the real one. If the path moves through difficult terrain, the creature believes the path slopes and twists to justify its reduction in speed.

On a successful save, the creature sees both paths, but the illusory path appears shadowy.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
