# Thunder Note
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You emit a crashing bang at the location of a creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take `d8` thunder damage and become deafened until the start of its next turn. If this damage forces it to make a Constitution saving throw to maintain concentration on a spell, it makes that save at disadvantage.

This spell's damage increases by `d8` when you reach 5th level (`2d8`), 11th level (`3d8`), and 17th level (`4d8`).

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 260*
