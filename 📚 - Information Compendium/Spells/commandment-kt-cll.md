# Commandment
*4th-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You issue a divine order to another creature you can see within range that can hear and understand you, invoking divine authority to punish transgressions against the order. If the target creature violates the issued order, it takes `4d6` radiant damage. Creatures only take this damage if they intentionally violate the stated rule. Once a creature takes damage this way, they cannot take damage again from it until the start of your next turn.

Some examples of orders you can issue would include ordering the target to not take attacks, to not move, or to not breath. The order must be possible to attempt to obey, or the spell ends.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 4th.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 219*
