# Mind Ray
*1st-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You launch a ray of mental energy from your mind at the mind of another creature within range. This ray ignores cover and passes through physical barriers.

The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it takes `2d8` psychic damage and has disadvantage on its next attack roll or ability check before the start of your next turn. On a success, it takes half as much damage and suffers no additional effects.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by `d8` for each level above 1st.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 244*
