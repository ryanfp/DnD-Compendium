# Void Walk
*6th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 8 hours

You touch a willing creature. For the duration, it gains a flying speed equal to its walking speed and can hover.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 6th.

*Source: Steinhardt's Guide to the Eldritch Hunt p. 200*
