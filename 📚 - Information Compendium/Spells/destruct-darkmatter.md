# Destruct
*9th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 1000 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Bringing all your magical powers to bear, you conjure a wave of destructive energy and unleash it at a ship. Make a spell attack roll against a ship you can see within range. On a hit, the ship takes `10d20 + 40` mega force damage.

*Source: Dark Matter p. 268*
