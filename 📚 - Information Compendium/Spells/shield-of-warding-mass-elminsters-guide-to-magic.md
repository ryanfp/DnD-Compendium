# Shield of Warding, Mass
*6th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

This spell functions like shield of warding, except that it affects you and up to six of your allies within range.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
