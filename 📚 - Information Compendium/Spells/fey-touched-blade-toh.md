# Fey-Touched Blade
*2nd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You touch a nonmagical weapon and imbue it with the magic of the fey. Choose one of the following damage types: force, psychic, necrotic, or radiant. Until the spell ends, the weapon becomes a magic weapon and deals an extra `d4` damage of the chosen type to any target it hits.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, you can imbue one additional weapon for each slot level above 2nd.p

*Source: Tome of Heroes p. 282*
