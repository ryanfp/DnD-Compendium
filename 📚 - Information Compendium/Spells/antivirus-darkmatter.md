# Antivirus
*1st-level, Abjuration (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 24 hours

You ward a device that you touch from digital interlopers. For the duration, this device can't be disabled by magical effects, such as the [technical difficulties ](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/spells/technical-difficulties-darkmatter.md) or [logic bomb ](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/spells/logic-bomb-darkmatter.md) spells, and it can't be infected by the N-Virus. Additionally, creatures have disadvantage on Intelligence (Data) checks made to hack this device.

*Source: Dark Matter p. 265*
