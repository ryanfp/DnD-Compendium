# Subliminal Aversion
*1st-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You ward a creature within range against attacks by making the choice to hit them painful. When the warded creature is hit with a melee attack from within 5 feet of it, the attacker takes `d4` psychic damage.

*Source: Tome of Heroes p. 306*
