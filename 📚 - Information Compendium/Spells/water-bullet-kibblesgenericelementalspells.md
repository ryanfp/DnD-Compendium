# Water Bullet
*cantrip, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a compressed bead of water and fire it at a creature you can see within range. Make a ranged spell attack. On hit, the target takes `d6` + your spellcasting ability modifier piercing damage.

This spell's damage increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`).

*Source: Kibbles' Generic Elemental Spells p. 19*
