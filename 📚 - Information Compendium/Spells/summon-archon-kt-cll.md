# Summon Archon
*2nd-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (an ornate relic worth at least 200 gp)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You call forth a minor celestial archon, which manifests in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. It uses the [Archon spirit](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/bestiary/celestial/archon-spirit-kt-cll.md) stat block, and you select either the Hound, Lantern, or Trumpet option when you cast the spell. The creature disappears when it drops to 0 hit points or when the spell ends.

The creature is an ally to you and your companions. In combat, it shares your initiative count, and its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). if you don't issue any command, it takes the Dodge action and uses its moves only to avoid hazards.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, use the higher level whenever the spell's level appears in the stat block.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 256*
