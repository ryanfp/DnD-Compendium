# Summon Vehicle
*1st-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 5 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a small piece of rubber)
- **Duration:** 10 minutes

You summon a bicycle or moving machine in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you. The vehicle can be no larger than a 5-foot cube, and it can only carry one creature that is Medium or smaller. The vehicle has a walking speed of 60 feet, which you can use while riding it, provided you have at least one free hand to operate the vehicle.

You can end the spell early by using an action to dismiss it. When the spell ends, the vehicle vanishes.

*Source: Obojima: Tales from the Tall Grass p. 191*
