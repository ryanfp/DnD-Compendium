# Updraft
*1st-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a miniature propeller or windmill)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you cast this spell, a small vortex of swirling air forms beneath your feet, lifting you upward. You can rise a number of feet equal to 20 x your spellcasting ability modifier. You can also move a number of feet horizontally during any part of the movement equal to 5 x your spellcasting ability modifier. At the end of the turn in which you cast this spell, you float gently to the ground if you are still aloft.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can rise an additional 20 feet, and move horizontally by another 5 feet, for each slot level above 1st.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
