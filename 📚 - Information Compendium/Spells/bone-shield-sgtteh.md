# Bone Shield
*1st-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You generate a barrier of elongated femurs that protects you. You reduce the incoming damage by `d12`, and if the attacking enemy is within 10 feet of you, the osseous barrier explodes, dealing slashing damage to them equal to the amount reduced.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage reduction increases by `d12` for each slot level above 1st.

*Source: Steinhardt's Guide to the Eldritch Hunt p. 188*
