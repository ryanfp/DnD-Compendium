# Life Support
*5th-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** S, M (a splash of your blood worth at least 5 hit points, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

When a creature is reduced to 0 hit points, you can forge a link to them, bolstering their life force with your own. For the duration of the spell, they do not fall unconscious from being at 0 hit points and can act normally, and any damage they take is instead transferred to you.

If a creature regains hit points and is no longer at 0 hit points, the spell ends.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 58*
