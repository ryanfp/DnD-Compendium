# Easy Trail
*1st-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (40-foot Radius)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 8 hours

When you cast this spell, heavy undergrowth parts as you approach, then moves back after you pass. 

For the duration, your speed and that of creatures within 40 feet of you isn't reduced by difficult terrain caused by any kind of plant or vegetable matter, and any creature attempting to track you has disadvantage on its survival checks to find or follow your trail.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 44*
