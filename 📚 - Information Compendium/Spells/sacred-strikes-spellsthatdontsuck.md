# Sacred Strikes
*1st-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

**Replaces:** Divine Favor

You ask for a divine blessing on your weapon, empowering it against your foes. For the duration, your weapon attacks deal an extra `d6` radiant damage.

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 37*
