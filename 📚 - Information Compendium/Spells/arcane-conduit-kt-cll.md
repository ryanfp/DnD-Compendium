# Arcane Conduit
*2nd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You tap directly into the arcane weave. For the duration, you have resistance to all damage from magical sources besides force damage.

At the start of each of your turns while tapped into this power, you and all creatures with 5 feet of you take `d6` force damage. The damage increases by `d6` each turn to a maximum of `4d6` damage.
