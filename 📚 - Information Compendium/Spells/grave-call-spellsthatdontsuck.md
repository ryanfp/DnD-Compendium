# Grave Call
*cantrip, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a coffin nail)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Replaces:** Toll the Dead

You gesture at one creature within range and chant a brief dirge, hastening its doom. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or take `d8` necrotic damage. If it is below half its maximum hit points, the damage increases to `d12`.

This spell's damage increases by one die when you reach 5th level (`2d8` or `2d12`), 11th level (`3d8` or `3d12`), and 17th level (`4d8` or `4d12`).

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 23*
