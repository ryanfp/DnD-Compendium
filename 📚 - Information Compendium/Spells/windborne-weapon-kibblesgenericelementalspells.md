# Windborne Weapon
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a piece of ammunition or weapon with the thrown property worth at least 1 cp)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Make an attack using this spell's material component as part of the action used to cast this spell, turning it into a ranged spell attack. This attack ignores cover. On hit, it deals `d8` damage of the weapon's damage type.

**At Higher Levels.** This spell's damage increases by `d8` when you reach 5th level (`2d8`), 11th level (`3d8`), and 17th level (`4d8`).

*Source: Kibbles' Generic Elemental Spells p. 21*
