# Eldritch Armor
*1st-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a vial of scorpion venom)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

**Replaces:** Armor of Agathys

You protect yourself with an acidic coating that sprays outward when struck. You gain `1d4 + 4` temporary hit points for the duration. If a creature hits you with a melee attack while you have these temporary hit points, the creature takes `2d4` acid damage.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, both the temporary hit points and the acid damage increase by `2d4` for each slot, up to a maximum of `9d4 + 4` temporary hit points and `10d4` damage at 5th level.

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 17*
