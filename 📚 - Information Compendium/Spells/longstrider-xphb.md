# Longstrider
*1st-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of dirt)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a creature. The target's [Speed](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/📝%20-%20Rules%20&%20Mechanics/variant-rules/speed-xphb.md) increases by 10 feet until the spell ends.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
