# Fling
*2nd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You manipulate gravity around one Large or smaller creature. The target must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, you can fling it 40 feet straight up or 20 feet in any other direction.

If you fling the target upward, it immediately falls, taking `4d6` falling damage and landing prone. If you fling it in any other direction, it takes `2d6` damage and lands prone. If this movement would be stopped early by a creature or object, both the target and whatever it collides with takes `3d6` bludgeoning damage.
