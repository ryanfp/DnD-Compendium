# Thunder Burst
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (5-foot Radius)
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Replaces:** Thunderclap

You create a concussive pulse, producing a boom audible out to 300 feet. All other creatures within 5 feet of you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take `d8` thunder damage and be deafened until the end of its next turn.

The spell's damage increases by `d8` when you reach 5th level (`2d8`), 11th level (`3d8`), and 17th level (`4d8`).

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 46*
