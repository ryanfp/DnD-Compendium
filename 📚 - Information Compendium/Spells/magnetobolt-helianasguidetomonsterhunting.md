# Magnetobolt
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A near-invisible pulse of arcanomagnetic energy shoots towards a creature within range Make a ranged spell attack against the target On a hit, the target takes `d6` force damage and must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be knocked prone A creature made of ferrous metal or wearing ferrous armour has disadvantage on this saving throw

This spell's damage increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level `2d6` 11th level `3d6` and 17th level `4d6`
