# Shillelagh
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (mistletoe, a shamrock leaf, and a club or quarterstaff)
- **Duration:** 1 minute

The wood of a club or quarterstaff you are holding is imbued with nature's power. For the duration, you can use your spellcasting ability instead of Strength for the attack and damage rolls of melee attacks using that weapon, and the weapon's damage die becomes a `d8`. The weapon also becomes magical, if it isn't already. The spell ends if you cast it again or if you let go of the weapon.

**At 5th Level.** You can cast this spell on any melee weapon made of wood. Additionally, as an action, you can make a melee spell attack against a target within reach to release the weapon's imbued magic into it. On a hit, the target takes `2d8` bludgeoning damage. The spell then ends. 

**At 11th Level.** The damage of the special attack increases by `d8` (`3d8`). 

**At 17th Level.** The damage of the special attack increases by `d8` (`4d8`).

*Source: EvolvingCantrips p. 275*
