# Shattering Shield
*2nd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You cause a shield of ice to erupt blocking the attack. The damage of the attack is reduced by `d8` + your spellcasting modifier. If the attack damage is not completely blocked, the shield shatters, dealing the amount of blocked damage as cold damage to the attacking creature. If the shield is not broken, it melts away after blocking the damage.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the amount of damage blocked increases by `d8` for each slot level above 2nd.

*Source: Kibbles' Generic Elemental Spells p. 6*
