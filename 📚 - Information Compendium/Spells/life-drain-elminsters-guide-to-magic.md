# Life Drain
*3rd-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You heal your wounds by draining vital energies from a foe you touch. Make a melee spell attack against a creature within your reach. On a hit, the target takes `d8` necrotic damage and it is paralyzed until the end of your next turn, and you or an ally within 5 feet regain hit points equal to half the amount of necrotic damage dealt. Until the spell ends, you can make the attack again on each of your turns as an action, but only against the same target.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
