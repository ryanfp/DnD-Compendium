# Elemental Barrage
*2nd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, M (a piece of ammunition)
- **Duration:** 1 round

You touch a piece of ammunition and infuse it with chaotic elemental power. If that piece of ammunition is fired before the start of your next turn, it transforms into three elemental bolts, one each dealing fire, cold, and lightning damage.

Make a weapon attack roll for each elemental bolt. You can fire all three at the same target or at different targets within the weapon's range. On a hit, each bolt deals elemental damage equal to the attack's normal damage, but of its elemental damage type.
