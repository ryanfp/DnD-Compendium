# Embalm
*2nd-level, Necromancy (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (unguents worth 100 gp)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You preserve one corpse against the ravages of time. Any decomposition stops but is not reversed. If cast soon enough after death, the body will look like it's simply resting (but it won't breathe, have a heartbeat, or show any other signs of life).

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 46*
