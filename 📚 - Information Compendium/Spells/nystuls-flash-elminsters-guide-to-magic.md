# Nystul's Flash
*1st-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (15-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

All creatures caught in a 15-foot cone must make a successful Constitution saving throw or be blinded for `d4` rounds. The affected target must make a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a successful save, it is no longer blinded.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
