# Melting Glob
*1st-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You hurl a glob of acid at a creature within range, making a ranged spell attack. On hit, the creature takes `4d4` acid damage, and starts to be melted by acid. For 1 minute, at the end of each of its turns, it takes `d4` acid damage. A creature can end the effect early by using its action to clear off the acid.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the initial damage increases by `2d4` for each slot level above 1st.

*Source: Kibbles' Generic Elemental Spells p. 5*
