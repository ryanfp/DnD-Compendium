# Spore Cloud
*cantrip, Biomancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (5-foot Sphere)
- **Components:** V, S, M (a toadstool)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You produce a cloud of virulent spores Each creature within 5 feet of you, other than you, must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take `d4` poison damage A creature that fails the saving throw by 5 or more is poisoned until the end of its next turn

The spell's damage increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level `2d4` 11th level `3d4` and 17th level `4d4`


*Source: Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting*
