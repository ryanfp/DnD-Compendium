# Entomb
*1st-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You attempt to encase a Medium or smaller creature you can see within ice. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or become restrained in ice for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, it takes `d8` cold damage and can make another Strength saving throw. On success, the spell ends.

If the creature suffers an instance of at least 5 fire or bludgeoning damage while restrained, the ice breaks and the target is freed, ending the spell.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by `d8` for each slot level above 1st.
