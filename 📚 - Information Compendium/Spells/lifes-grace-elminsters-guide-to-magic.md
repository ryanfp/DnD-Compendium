# Life's Grace
*5th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch a living creature and ward it against death magic for a time. For the spell's duration, the subject gains resistance to necrotic damage and advantage on saving throws against effects that would reduce its hit point maximum (such as the bite of a vampire) or kill it instantaneously without dealing damage (such as the wail of a banshee). In addition, when the subject would fail a saving throw an undead creature forces it to make, it can choose to succeed instead, and the spell ends.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
