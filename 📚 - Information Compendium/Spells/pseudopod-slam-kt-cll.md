# Pseudopod Slam
*2nd-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 10 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You conjure a pseudopod of acidic ooze that slams a creature or object within range. Make a melee spell attack. On hit, the target takes `8d4` acid damage and is moved 5 feet in a direction of your choice.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by `2d4` for each slot level above 2nd.

**Classes**: [Spellblade](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-spellblade.md); [Occultist](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md)
