# Spell Immunity
*4th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (the same components as the spell being countered, if any)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You choose one spell of 4th-level or lower that can't affect you. The next time the chosen spell would affect you before you finish a long rest, you are unaffected by that casting of the spell, as if it had never been cast, and this spell ends.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
