# Blade Burst
*cantrip, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (5-foot Radius)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Replaces:** Sword Burst

You conjure a ring of blades to slash or stab at your foes. All other creatures within 5 feet of you must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take `d6` slashing or piercing damage (your choice).

This spell's damage increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`).

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 6*
