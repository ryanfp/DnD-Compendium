# Blood Bolt
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** S, M (a drop of your blood worth at least 1 hit point, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You transmute a drop of blood into a crimson dart and launch it at creatures within range, making a ranged spell attack. On hit, the target takes `d6` piercing damage and `d6` necrotic damage.

The piercing and necrotic damage increase by `d6` at 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`). The blood consumed also increases when the damage increases at 5th (2), 11th (3), and 17th (4).

**Classes**: [Occultist](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md)
