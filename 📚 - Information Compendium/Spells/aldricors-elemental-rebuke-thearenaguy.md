# Aldricor's Elemental Rebuke
*208th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You manipulate some of the incoming elemental energy, harnessing its power in your hand. The damage you take from the triggering attack or effect is cut in half. You can then hurl the energy back at the creature that damaged you if it is within 60 feet of you. The creature must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking `d8` damage of the triggering type on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
