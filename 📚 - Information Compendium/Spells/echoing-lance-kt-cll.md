# Echoing Lance
*4th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You emit a targeted burst of intense sonic energy at a creature within range. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, it takes `3d8` thunder damage and is stunned for the duration. On a success, it takes half as much damage and isn't stunned.

At the end of each of its turns, the target can make another Constitution saving throw. On a success, the spell ends. On a failure, it takes an additional `d8` thunder from the ongoing echoes of the sound.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the initial damage increases by `d8` for each slot level above 4th.
