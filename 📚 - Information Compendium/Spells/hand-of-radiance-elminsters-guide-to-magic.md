# Hand of Radiance
*cantrip, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a holy symbol)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Sparks of holy light streak from your fingertips to strike your enemies. Choose up to three creatures within range, none of whom can be more than 10 feet apart. Each target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take `d4` radiant damage.

**At Higher Levels.** This spell's damage increases by `d4` when you reach 5th level (`2d4`), 11th level (`3d4`), and 17th level (`4d4`).

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
