# Hellfire
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You create an eruption of smoldering hellfire around a creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or take `d4` fire damage plus `d4` necrotic damage.

**At Higher Levels.** Both of the spell's damage types increase by `d4` when you reach 5th level (`2d4` each), 11th level (`3d4` each), and 17th level (`4d4` each).

*Source: The Illrigger Revised p. 33*
