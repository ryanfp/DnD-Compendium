# Symbol of Resilience
*3rd-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Replaces:** Beacon of Hope

You create a tiny symbol above you, which radiates hope in a 30-foot radius until the spell ends. The symbol can take whatever form you choose, such as that of your deity. As a bonus action on your turn, you can move the symbol up to 30 feet.

Each non-hostile, living creature in the symbol's radius (including you) has advantage on Wisdom saving throws, adds your spellcasting ability modifier to its death saving throws (treating rolls equal to or above 20 as a natural 20), and regains the maximum number of hit points possible from any healing.

The first time a non-hostile, living creature starts its turn in the symbol's radius, it can use a bonus action to spend one Hit Die to regain hit points as if it had taken a short rest. If the creature had fewer hit points than half its hit point maximum, it also gains an equivalent number of temporary hit points until the spell ends.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, a creature can spend one additional Hit Die as a bonus action for each slot level above 3rd.

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 45*
