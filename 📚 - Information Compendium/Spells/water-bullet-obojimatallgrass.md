# Water Bullet
*1st-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a sphere of swirling water that spins rapidly in your hand or mouth before you hurl it at a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes bludgeoning damage based on how far it is away from you.

- 1-10 feet: `5d6`  
- 11-60 feet: `4d6`  
- 61-90 feet: `3d6`  

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by `d6` for each slot level above 1st.

*Source: Obojima: Tales from the Tall Grass p. 192*
