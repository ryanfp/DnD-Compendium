# Karmic Curse
*3rd-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (something from the target creature—such as blood, hair, or scales—which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You hex a target creature, placing a karmic binding on it. For the duration of the spell, the first time the target damages another creature on a turn, it takes `4d6` psychic damage.

**Classes**: [Occultist](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md)

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 20*
