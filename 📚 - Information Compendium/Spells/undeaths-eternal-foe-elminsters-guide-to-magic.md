# Undeath's Eternal Foe
*9th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Radius)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Select up to seven friendly creatures within range (one of which may be you). Each subject is surrounded by a spectral ghost-like aura that grants it a +4 bonus to its Armor Class against undead creatures.

In addition, affected creatures gain the benefits of the death ward spell, and automatically succeed on saving throws they are forced to make against the abilities of undead creatures, except when such saving throws are caused by spells.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
