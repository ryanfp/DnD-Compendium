# Poison Spray
*cantrip, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 10 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You extend your hand toward a creature you can see within range and project a puff of noxious gas from your palm. The creature must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take `d12` poison damage.

This spell's damage increases by `d12` when you reach 5th level (`2d12`), 11th level (`3d12`), and 17th level (`4d12`).
