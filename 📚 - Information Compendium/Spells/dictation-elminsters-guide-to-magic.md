# Dictation
*1st-level, Evocation (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, M (a quill)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

Choose a book, scroll, or paper that is on your person when you cast this spell. For the duration, any words spoken out loud within 20 feet of the chosen object automatically appears written on its pages. Foreign languages are not translated but do appear spelled correctly using that language's alphabet. Verbal components of magical spells are not recorded.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 41*
