# Perforating Shot
*1st-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

As your attack hits or misses the target, the weapon or ammunition transforms into a 5-foot-wide <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Line</span> of magical energy that extends out to the weapon's normal range. The <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Line</span> includes the attack's original target. Each creature within the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Line</span> makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking Force damage equal to the weapon's normal damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The weapon's damage increases by `d8` for each slot level above 1.

*Source: The Gunslinger Class: Valda's Spire of Secrets*
