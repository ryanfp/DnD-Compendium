# Virtuous Strike
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 5 feet
- **Components:** V, M (a weapon)
- **Duration:** 1 round

As part of the action used to cast this spell, make a melee weapon attack against a creature within range. On a hit, the target suffers the attack's normal effects, and divine light invested in your weapon fills you with resolve. If the target forces you to make a saving throw before the start of your next turn, the target takes an additional `d6` psychic damage, you gain a bonus on the saving throw equal to the psychic damage inflicted, and the spell ends.

The spell's damage increases when you reach higher levels. At 5th level, the melee attack deals an extra `d6` radiant damage to the target, and the psychic damage the target takes for forcing you to make a saving throw increases to `2d6`. Both damage rolls increase by `d6` at 11th level and 17th level.

*Source: Elminsters Guide to Magic v3 p. 0*
