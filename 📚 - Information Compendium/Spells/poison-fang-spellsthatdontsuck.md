# Poison Fang
*cantrip, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Replaces:** Poison Spray

You conjure a spectral snake that bites down on one creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take `d12` poison damage and be poisoned until the start of its next turn.

This spell's damage increases by `d12` when you reach 5th level (`2d12`), 11th level (`3d12`), and 17th level (`4d12`).

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 33*
