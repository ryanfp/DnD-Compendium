# Mandy's Feral Follower
*4th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a small servant's bell)
- **Duration:** Special

You touch a Medium or smaller Beast, which shape-shifts into a Humanoid with an appearance of your choice. The Beast's statistics are replaced by the stat block of a Commoner but retains its <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point Dice</span>. You choose the Commoner's skill proficiency. The commoner wears Fine Clothes but has no other equipment, and has a basic understanding of all unskilled tasks.

The target gains 10 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>. The spell ends early on the target if it has no <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> left. When the spell ends, the Fine Clothes dissipate into smoke.

## Combat

The commoner is an ally to you and your allies. It rolls its own <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> and acts on its own turn. It behaves as though it is dutifully employed by you.

## Duration

This spell's duration varies depending upon when and where it was cast. If cast in a location where time passes normally, the spell lasts until the twelfth stroke of the next midnight, however long that may be. Elsewhere, the spell lasts for 24 hours.

## Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot

You can shape-shift one additional Beast for each spell slot level above 4.
