# Soul Rend
*1st-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You empower yourself with spiritual energy. The first time you deal force, radiant, or necrotic damage to a creature during this spell's duration, you tear at the soul of one creature you damaged, dealing an extra `d6` damage to the target. Also, you affect the target in one of the following ways of your choice:

- The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of you until the end of your next turn.  
- The target must succeed a Strength saving throw or you move them up to 15 feet in any direction of your choice, except upward. Once they reach their destination, you can also knock them prone.  
- The extra damage dealt increases by `d6`.  

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can choose an additional effect for each slot level above 1st. You cannot choose the last effect more than once.
