# Windborne Weapon
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You wrap a weapon or piece of ammunition in swirling wind. Make a ranged spell attack against a target within range, using the spell's material component. This attack ignores cover. On a hit, it deals `d10` damage of the weapon's damage type.

**At Higher Levels.** This spell's damage increases by `d8` when you reach 5th level (`2d10`), 11th level (`3d10`), and 17th level (`4d10`).

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 268*
