# Blood Seal
*2nd-level, Necromancy (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (an ornate dagger worth at least 25gp)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You inflict a wound upon yourself to create a protective seal on another creature within range. As you cast the spell, you spend a number of hit points and the target gains `d10` + the number of hit points spent as temporary hit points for the duration.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the target gains `d10` additional temporary hit points for each slot level above 2nd.

*Source: Dark Arts Player's Companion p. 26*
