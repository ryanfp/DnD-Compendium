# See Invisibility
*2nd-level, Divination*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of talc)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

For the duration, you see creatures and objects that have the [Invisible](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/📝%20-%20Rules%20&%20Mechanics/conditions.md#Invisible) condition as if they were visible, and you can see into the Ethereal Plane. Creatures and objects there appear ghostly.
