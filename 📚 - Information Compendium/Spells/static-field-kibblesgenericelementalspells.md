# Static Field
*3rd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

A visible field of static energy crackles to life in a 20 foot radius around a point you can see within range. Each time a creature moves more than 5 feet or takes an action while within the area of effect, it takes `d12` lightning damage.

*Source: Kibbles' Generic Elemental Spells p. 16*
