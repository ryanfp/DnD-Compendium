# Curse of Misfortune
*1st-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (something from the target creature—such as blood, hair, or scales—which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You curse a creature within range, bringing it bad luck. When you place this curse, pick a number from 2 to 19. Whenever the target rolls the chosen number on an attack roll, saving throw, or ability check, that number is treated as 1.

Additionally, once during the effect you can use your reaction when the target rolls an attack roll, saving throw, or ability check to force to reroll the d20 and use the lower roll. You regain the ability to do this each time the target rolls your chosen number.

**Classes**: [Occultist](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md)

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 19*
