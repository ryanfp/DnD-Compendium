# Draining Bolt
*cantrip, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Replaces:** Sapping Sting

You sap the vitality of one creature you can see in range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes `d6` necrotic damage and has disadvantage on the next weapon attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn. 

This spell's damage increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`).

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 15*
