# Riptide
*3rd-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Replaces:** Tidal Wave

You conjure a sudden wave in a 20-foot square on the ground or in a body of water which you can see within range. Each creature in the area must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, it takes `4d8` bludgeoning damage and a Huge or smaller creature is knocked prone. On a success, it takes half as much damage and is not knocked prone. All creatures failing the save are moved 15 feet in a direction of your choice.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell with a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by `d8` and the distance moved on a failed save increases by 5 feet for each slot level above 3rd.

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 36*
