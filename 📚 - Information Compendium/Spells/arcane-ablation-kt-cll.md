# Arcane Ablation
*1st-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a piece of worn armor or clothing and imbue it with magic. For the duration, the wearer gains 4 temporary hit points. If these temporary hit points are exhausted, the creature gains 1 fewer temporary hit points at the start of its next turn. When the temporary hit points would reach 0, the spell ends. Temporary hit points gained from this spell are lost when the spell ends.

**At Higher Levels.** The initial temporary hit points increases by 1 for each slot level above 1st.
