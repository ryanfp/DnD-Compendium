# Shockwave
*1st-level, None*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Radius)
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a burst of telekinetic force in all directions. Each creature of your choice within 30 feet is knocked back 5 feet and must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, it takes `8d6` bludgeoning damage and is knocked prone. On a success, it takes half as much damage and isn't knocked prone.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 273*
