# Nauseating Poison
*1st-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round

You shroud your hand, a weapon you are holding, or a natural weapon in dark and ichorous miasma. The next time you hit a creature with a melee attack (including a melee spell attack) before the start of your next turn, the attack deals an extra `d12` poison damage and the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of your next turn.

The spell ends after dealing damage, or at the start of your next turn, whichever occurs first.

**Classes**: [Occultist](/📚%20-%20Information%20Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-occultist.md)
