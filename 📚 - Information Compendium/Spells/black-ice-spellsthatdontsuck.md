# Black Ice
*cantrip, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Replaces:** Frostbite

You cause a patch of nearly transparent ice to form on ground that you can see within range. Until the spell ends, the magic ice fills a 5-foot square. Any creature on the ice's space when you cast the spell must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take `d6` cold damage. If the creature is Medium or smaller, it also falls prone on a failed save.

A creature must also make the saving throw when it moves onto the ice's space for the first time on a turn or ends its turn atop it.

This spell's damage increases by `d6` when you reach 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`).

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 8*
