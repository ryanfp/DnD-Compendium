# Bloodlust
*2nd-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You imbue a willing creature that you can see within range with vitality and fury. The target gains `d6` temporary hit points, has advantage on Strength checks, and deals an extra `d6` damage when it hits with a weapon attack. When the spell ends, the target suffers one level of exhaustion for 1 minute.

*Source: Tome of Heroes p. 275*
