# Supernova
*9th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Sight
- **Components:** V, S, M (a lump of starforged iron)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You form a fist-sized star at a point you can see within range, which then explodes into a cataclysmic supernova. All creatures within 60 feet of the point must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes `30d8` radiant damage and is blinded for 1 minute. On a success, it takes half as much damage and is not blinded. A blinded creature can repeat this saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

*Source: Kibbles' Compendium of Legends and Legacies p. 260*
