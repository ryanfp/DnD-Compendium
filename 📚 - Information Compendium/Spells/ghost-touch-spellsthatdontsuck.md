# Ghost Touch
*cantrip, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** 1 round

**Replaces:** Chill Touch

You create an intangible, cadaverous hand that latches onto a creature within range, assailing its life force. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes `d8` necrotic damage, and it can't regain hit points until the start of your next turn.

If you hit an undead target, it has disadvantage on attack rolls against you until the end of your next turn.

This spell's damage increases by `d8` when you reach 5th level (`2d8`), 11th level (`3d8`), and 17th level (`4d8`).

*Source: Spells That Don't Suck p. 22*
