# Homebrew Errata Guide

## Unorganized List of Changes

- reworked resting system, long rests now take 3 days in a haven, you can only take your PB # of Short Rests for every Long Rest you take
- Exhaustion uses the 5.5e rules, while also affecting Spell Save DCs. It also reduces movement speed by 5ft per level and reduces max health by 5% per level
- You gain a level of exhaustion when you go down in combat twice (applied after combat/initiative ends)

- Two skills have been added:
	- Engineering (Intelligence): For mechanical and structural knowledge, technical know-how, and crafting/tinkering
	- Streetwise (Charisma): A metric of the ability to fit in with a local culture and gather resources like information, leads, rumors. Maybe it gives you an edge into local customs or faux pa’s, social hierarchies, etc.
