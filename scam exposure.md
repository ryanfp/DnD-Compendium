# Behance Comission Art Scam

So, I would say I’m extremely new to art commissions and this whole scene in general, but after this experience I feel the need to bring it to light in case other people have interacted with them. I’ve also reported them on Behance, Paypal, and Twitter (if applicable). 

Basically, I saw a reddit post advertising their portfolio in one of the art commission subreddits, and followed the link and liked what I saw. The stuff looked good, non-AI generated, and semi-consistent. I messaged the creator, we got to talking and eventually we agreed upon 50% of the payment through PayPal G&S. 

![[image-93.png]]

Throughout the conversation, I got the vibe they were using AI to respond to my messages, it felt very similar to what you’d expect. I even asked at one point, and they seemed to take back over and write some responses themselves again I guess. Anyway, it really threw me off once we got to PayPal. They sent me a link that was a different @name, different display name, and different profile picture. 

![[image-94.png]]

At this point, my alarm bells *should* have been going off, but it wasn’t until after the payment that I started digging. Here’s a quick rundown of my findings:

The name “DevArtistry” brings up [this Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/behance/comments/1dttcig/profile_with_stolen_works_behance_not_acting/) where they talk about a scammer using stolen artwork/AI art to scam people on Behance. The profile picture matches exactly to the one from the PayPal profile. 

Searching “@DigiWorldwide” returns nothing. 

Searching for the Behance profile name, the name of the profile on Discord, or the names of the “Appreciations” folders on Behance bring up nothing either.
