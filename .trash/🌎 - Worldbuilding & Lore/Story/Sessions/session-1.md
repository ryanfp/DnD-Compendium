# Session 1

Funeral in Wroat. Lightning Rail murder mystery. Arrival in Sharn.

Session 1 covers the funeral of the session 0 NPC in Wroat, boarding the Lightning Rail to Sharn, a murder mystery investigation, the reveal that the murder was cover for a heist, a rooftop action sequence, and the introduction of Dusknoir upon arrival in Sharn.

## Phase 0 — The Funeral (Wroat)

Late afternoon. Rain. Small rundown cemetery in Wroat's lower wards. Gray stone, iron fences, puddles on cracked paths.

Few attendees — the party and a handful of strangers. Each player created the NPC in session 0, and the party realizes they are all here for the same person. The NPC was a different thing to each of them.

**Post-burial:** Smoky jazz club and bar the NPC owned. Rain on windows. Singer on a small stage. Drinks on the house.

A bartender or lawyer delivers a posthumous message: references a colleague on tomorrow's Lightning Rail to Sharn, says the colleague is carrying something important and may be in danger, asks the party to meet them and ensure delivery. Does not mention the expedition, Anchors, Prophecy, or anything cosmic.

## Phase 1 — Establishment (Lightning Rail, first 1–2 hours)

Wroat–Sharn Lightning Rail. Blue energy crackling beneath carriages. Rain.

The party finds compartments and meets NPCs naturally:
- **Blood of Vol Priest:** Nearby compartment, friendly, offers tea, heading to Sharn for an aid center.
- **Agent 1 and Agent 2:** Dining car, young couple, quiet. Small dog at their feet.
- **Karrnathi Diplomat:** Passes in the corridor with a bodyguard. Curt nod.
- **Research Assistant:** Visibly anxious, sitting alone, mentions a disagreement with a professor.
- **Conductor:** Checks tickets, professional, harried.

The party seeks out the victim (the researcher). Alive, preoccupied, working in their compartment. If the party mentions the session 0 NPC's name: *"Thank the Sovereigns. I was told someone would come. We'll talk properly at dinner."*

Allow 30–60 minutes of real-time roleplay before anything goes wrong.

## Phase 2 — The Murder

The party goes to meet the researcher for dinner. Compartment door closed, no answer. Door unlocked. Researcher dead inside.

**Scene:** Body slumped at desk. Defensive wounds. Ink stains. Sweet chemical smell (sedative). Door lock: no damage but faint magical residue (Arcana DC 12, Knock spell). Window unlatched from inside. Dark hair on sleeve. Greenish powder on doorframe (Investigation DC 15). Journal open, mid-sentence.

The conductor is summoned. No authorities until Sharn. Car-to-car movement locked down.

## Phase 3 — The Investigation

The longest phase (50–60% of session). The party interviews NPCs and examines evidence.

- **Murder scene:** 6 physical clues (P1–P6) with DCs.
- **Cargo hold:** 4 physical clues (P7–P10) — case missing, second set of tool marks from Grovyle's earlier failed attempt, canvas scrap, torn pages from research satchel.
- Each NPC has specific cracks and contradictions available through interview.

## Phase 4 — The Pivot

Two clues converge: the case is missing from cargo, and Agent 1's testimony contradicts the diplomat's bodyguard. The murder is reframed as cover for a heist. The party can identify Agent 1 (and possibly Agent 2) as suspects.

## Phase 5 — The Break

The party confronts Agent 1, or she realizes they are about to.

Flash bomb. Agent 2 draws a weapon. The dog's Seeming drops — skeletal hound, green-fire eyes.

Interior fight: 1–2 rounds. Tight quarters in the corridor and dining car. The hound blocks the corridor.

The operatives flee to the car connector and climb to the roof.

## Phase 6 — The Rooftop

Rain, wind, blue conductor-stone energy below. Treacherous footing (Dex saves / Athletics checks required).

The agents fight defensively, moving toward the rear of the train. A Lyrandar wind skiff pulls alongside and drops a rope ladder.

Grovyle enters from the front of the train over the roof. He fights whoever has the case. He is visibly better than everyone else. Possible line: *"You don't know what this is. Walk away."* If he gets the case, he jumps. If he cannot secure it in 1–2 rounds, he jumps anyway.

**Multiple valid outcomes:** The operatives escape, the party recovers the case, Grovyle takes it, or the party holds everything. In every version, Grovyle escapes, and the hound reverts to its true undead form when destroyed.

## Phase 7 — Sharn Arrival (Dusknoir)

The Lightning Rail pulls into Sharn. Rain. Impossible towers.

Dusknoir is waiting at the platform. He already knows about the incident — too quickly. He is warm and charming. He asks the right questions, solves one lingering thread, and redirects suspicion away from the Emerald Claw.

- If the party mentions Grovyle: *"Half-orc, Tharashk marks, wanted in three nations. A thief with a manifesto."* (Note: Grovyle is Shadar-kai. Dusknoir may be deliberately using an outdated cover identity to misdirect the party, or this line may need updating.)
- If the party mentions the Blood of Vol or the Emerald Claw: he deflects toward "organized crime."

The research assistant approaches quietly and offers the party their copied notes if the case was lost.

If the party shares the notes with Dusknoir, he thanks them warmly. He may destroy or exploit them.

## Session End State

The party ends session 1 in Sharn with:
- A dead contact
- A dead (true form) undead dog
- The operatives captured, dead, or escaped
- The dragonshard case recovered or lost
- The research assistant's copied notes
- A famous paladin ally (Dusknoir)
- A mysterious figure who told them to walk away (Grovyle)
- The session 0 NPC's name in the victim's journal

The party has no understanding yet of Anchors, the Prophecy, or the Convergence.
