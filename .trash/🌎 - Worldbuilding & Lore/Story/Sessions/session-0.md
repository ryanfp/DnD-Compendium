# Session 0

Character creation. Each player creates one facet of the session 0 NPC.

## Overview

Session 0 is the character creation session. Each player creates one facet of the same NPC — a person who is already dead before session 1 begins. One player may have known the NPC as a mentor, another as an employer, another as an old friend. The players do not know they are all describing the same person until session 1.

## The NPC's Role

The session 0 NPC was each PC's handler in the Mosaic (or an adjacent organization). The NPC recruited each PC independently. Each PC knew only the NPC — they did not know each other.

## The Expedition

The party members were independent operatives in separate cells. They worked with no connection to each other except through cryptic messages or dreams from their handlers. They were recruited individually and told to meet at a specific location for an expedition. They arrived as strangers with a shared mission, got stained, received the Irian binding, lost their expedition memories, and returned to separate lives.

They do not recognize each other at the funeral because they never knew each other outside the expedition window — and that window is erased.

## The Irian Binding Plant

If a PC has a distinctive physical feature in their backstory, privately tell the player it appeared roughly three years ago.
