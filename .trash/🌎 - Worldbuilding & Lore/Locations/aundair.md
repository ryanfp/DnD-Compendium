# Aundair

Nation. Home of the Arcane Congress and the Mosaic Committee (Layer 3).

## The Mosaic Committee

The original Mosaic Committee is an academic sub-committee within the Arcane Congress, located in Aundair. It has 5–12 members and still exists. It is compromised by both the Chamber and the Lords of Dust.

The Committee believes it is the real Mosaic. It is unaware of Layer 1 (the Dhakaani duur'kala) or Layer 2 (the broader mortal network).
