# Karrnath

Nation. Undead military tradition. Emerald Claw origin.

## Overview

Karrnath has a strong undead military tradition. The Emerald Claw originated here.

## Session 1

The Emerald Claw operatives on the Lightning Rail use a Karrnathi undead beast — a skeletal hound kept under a permanent Seeming spell — as combat support.
