# The Chamber

Secret dragon organization that monitors and manipulates the Draconic Prophecy.

## Overview

The Chamber is almost completely unknown. A few intelligence agencies have fringe files about covert dragon operatives. The Gatekeepers know of them in a general ancient-tradition sense.

Party learning about the Chamber at all is a Tier 2–3 revelation.

## Relationship to the Mosaic

The Chamber knows the Mosaic exists. It considers the Mosaic useful, irritating, and tolerable. A Chamber dragon might protect a Mosaic cell one year and obliterate a different cell the next, depending on Prophecy needs. The Mosaic experiences this as inexplicable luck and inexplicable disaster.

The dynamic is MI6/CIA — not allies, not enemies, occasionally serving each other's purposes by accident.
