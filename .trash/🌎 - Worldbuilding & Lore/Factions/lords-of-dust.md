# The Lords of Dust

Rakshasa cabal working to free imprisoned Overlords.

## Overview

The Lords of Dust are a rakshasa cabal whose goal is to free the imprisoned Overlords. Dragons and fiends spend millennia engineering or preventing the conditions required by the Draconic Prophecy.

## Tactics Against the Mosaic

The Lords of Dust use turned mortals — not shapechangers — to compromise Mosaic cells. This is slower and less reliable than direct infiltration. The Mosaic's Prophecy Resonance defense works against monsters and fiends, not against people who simply choose wrong.

## Mordakhesh

Mordakhesh is the prakhutu (speaker) of Rak Tulkhesh. He tells the truth because truth serves war.
