# The Order of the Emerald Claw

Militant arm serving Erandis Vol's agenda.

## Overview

The Order of the Emerald Claw is the militant arm of the Blood of Vol, operating in service of Erandis Vol's agenda. They are distinct from the Blood of Vol faith itself.

## Session 1 — Lightning Rail Operation

Two agents (Agent 1 and Agent 2) operate on the Lightning Rail under cover as a young couple. They murder a Morgrave researcher and steal a dragonshard case from cargo. Their undead dog — a Karrnathi undead beast kept under a permanent Seeming spell — provides combat support.

**Extraction method:** A Lyrandar wind skiff pulls alongside the train during the rooftop sequence. A rope ladder drops for the operatives to escape.

## Signature Compound

A greenish alchemical powder is their signature compound. It is unidentifiable at level 1 but becomes recognizable later in the campaign.

## Dusknoir's Deflection

Dusknoir redirects suspicion away from the Emerald Claw: *"The Claw are terrorists, yes, but they're disorganized. This feels more like organized crime."*

## Campaign Progression

- **Session 0:** Emerald Claw killed the session 0 NPC (Dusknoir possibly involved).
- **Tier 3:** Dusknoir operates openly as Emerald Claw commander.
