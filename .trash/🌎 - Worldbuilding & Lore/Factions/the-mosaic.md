# The Mosaic

Homebrew decentralized mortal network studying the Draconic Prophecy. Three-layer structure.

## Overview

The Mosaic is a decentralized, continent-spanning network of individual agents who study the Draconic Prophecy independently. Named "the Mosaic" — each member is a single tile in a larger picture none of them can see.

## Structure

- Every member is a single-person cell. No groups.
- Each member knows at most 2–3 contact points (dead drops, coded publications, Sivis message routes). Not people.
- Communication is one-to-one. No gatherings, no councils.
- Recruitment is organic — someone asks the right questions, a nearby agent finds them.
- No unified doctrine. Cells disagree on what the Prophecy means. This is a feature; it makes infiltration harder.
- If a member goes dark, nobody investigates. The network accepts losses.

## Verification — Prophecy Resonance

The Mosaic verifies members through their metaphysical relationship to the Draconic Prophecy, not through passwords or trust.

- Prophecy-significant mortals produce a faint, detectable resonance when exposed to Prophecy fragments (dragonshards, manifest zone boundaries, carved inscriptions).
- A rakshasa or dragon cannot fake mortal Prophecy resonance. Fiends and dragons have their own resonance signatures that read differently.
- Does not detect turned, bribed, or dominated mortals. Human treachery still works. This made magical infiltration expensive, not impossible.
- Developed through centuries of trial and error. Early cells were infiltrated. The ones that survived developed the test. Natural selection.

## Three-Layer Structure

| Layer | What It Is | Age | Awareness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Layer 1 — The Root (Kech Volaar / Dhakaani duur'kala) | Hobgoblin dirge singers who have tracked Prophecy patterns through oral bardic memory for millennia. 5–10 alive at any time. None know each other's locations. The song is distributed, not centralized. | Thousands of years | Don't call themselves "the Mosaic." Predate the concept. |
| Layer 2 — The Network (The Mosaic proper) | Mortal single-person cells across Khorvaire. Founded ~200–300 years ago when academic Prophecy researchers made contact with a duur'kala. Race-agnostic. | Centuries | Think of themselves as the whole network. Don't know Layer 1 exists. |
| Layer 3 — The Mosaic Committee (Aundair) | The original academic sub-committee within the Arcane Congress. 5–12 members. Still exists. Compromised by Chamber and Lords of Dust. | Canon (Dragons of Eberron) | Think they're the real thing. Don't know about Layers 1 or 2. |

## Thematic Function

- Goblins — dismissed, conquered, scattered — quietly hold the longest Prophecy record on the continent. Their insignificance is their armor.
- The duur'kala don't need immortality. The song is immortal. Each singer lives a normal lifespan but carries millennia of perfect memory passed via bardic ritual. Mirror to Aerenal's Undying Court: elves preserve individual souls; Dhakaani preserve the knowledge itself.
- Direct inversion of Illmarrow: she's an immortal individual hoarding secrets. The Mosaic is a mortal chain where no individual matters but the knowledge never dies.

## Relationships

- **The Chamber:** MI6/CIA dynamic — not allies, not enemies, occasionally serving each other's purposes by accident. See [[the-chamber]].
- **The Lords of Dust:** Use turned mortals to compromise cells. Human weakness, not magical infiltration.

## Campaign Integration

- The party's pre-expedition team was adjacent to a Mosaic cell.
- The session 0 NPC may have been a cell leader whose funeral note points the party toward the Lightning Rail.
- Mosaic agents in Tiers 2–3 provide pieces of the Anchor puzzle. No single agent has the whole picture.
- Some cells are compromised through human weakness. The party can't trust any single cell completely.
