# The Silver Flame

Divine force that binds the Overlords. Also a church.

## Overview

The Silver Flame is the divine force that actively holds the Overlords bound. It is also an organized church across Khorvaire.

## The Couatl Sacrifice

Most couatl voluntarily dissolved their individual existence into the Silver Flame in order to bind the Overlords. This is the canon precedent for mortals choosing to merge into a cosmic force and losing selfhood.

## Overlord Bindings

The bindings are active, not passive. The Silver Flame holds the Overlords. The Lords of Dust constantly work to weaken the conditions. Binding requires ongoing sacrifice.

## Campaign Role — Theme 7 (Faith Without Proof)

The Silver Flame dims after Bahamut is healed with only approximately 10 of 13 Anchors restored (end of Tier 2). Clerics lose power. The question becomes: do you keep going when the source of your conviction is diminished?
