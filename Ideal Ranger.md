## Ideal Ranger Details

### Objectives

We want to try and buff up the ranger a little towards the end of tier 2 play, but especially so in tiers 3 and 4. Maybe moving the extra attack and extra reaction around would be good? I also think increasing the damage of the “hunter’s mark-type feature” (as the class should have one version between it). I want to keep most of the flavor of the base ranger intact as a kind of deft fighter-rouge-druid combo that excels in the wilderness or environment with light spells etc. But I also want to lean in on the monster tracker/hunting aspect of it through the abilities from the GH Monster Hunter class and the base Hunter’s mark or Quarry feature

Most likely will remove the method of favored enemy and replace it with the monster grimoire feature. I will put the monster types into five groups and reduce the amount of times they can choose a group to be proficient in, probably to a max of ⅗ at level 19 or something super high level.

Make the “Hunter’s mark-like feature” ability scale better, to maybe make it equal with other classes DPR?
