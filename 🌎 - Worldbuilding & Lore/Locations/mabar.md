# Mabar

The Endless Night. Plane of darkness and entropy. Tier 2 exile destination.

## Properties

- Devours energy, light, and hope.
- Undead are empowered here.
- Manifest zones on Eberron create death and darkness effects.
- Feeds on positive energy specifically.
- Time is accelerated — months pass inside Mabar while the party is within. They return to a changed world.

## Distinction from Dolurrh

Mabar does not necessarily destroy consciousness instantly. Things persist in diminished forms — shadows, echoes, fragments that remember what they were. Dolurrh dissolves memories into nothing; Mabar allows diminished persistence.

## Campaign Role

- **Tier 2 exile:** The party and Grovyle are exiled to Mabar by Dusknoir's betrayal.
- The party's Irian binding is under siege here. Mabar is specifically eating the thing keeping them alive.
- Memory recovery happens because Mabar eats the Irian layer, exposing original memories underneath. Recovery is damage, not a gift.
- Celebi (Grovyle's spouse) was pulled into Mabar. May persist in diminished form.
- Grovyle is Shadar-kai — native to Mabar. His home plane is consuming the person he is trying to find.
- Erandis Vol operates out of Mabar. She controls 3 Anchors here.
- The Mabar stay is longer than 1–2 sessions.
