# Dolurrh

The Realm of the Dead. Souls fade here — memories first, then identity.

## Overview

In Dolurrh, souls fade — memories first, then identity, then nothing. It is not punishment. It is entropy.

## Resurrection

Resurrection works but returns less the longer someone has been in Dolurrh.

## Distinction from Mabar

Dolurrh dissolves memories into nothing. Mabar allows diminished persistence — shadows, echoes, fragments that remember what they were.

## Campaign Role

The stain marks souls for Dolurrh.
