# Sharn

City of Towers. Breland. Destination of the Lightning Rail in session 1.

## Atmosphere

The city rises in impossible towers. Rain.

## Session 1

Dusknoir is waiting at the platform when the Lightning Rail arrives — already knowing about the incident, and too quickly so. He is warm and charming.

The Blood of Vol priest is heading here to open a community aid center.

## Factions Present

- The Boromar Clan is the dominant crime syndicate in Sharn.
