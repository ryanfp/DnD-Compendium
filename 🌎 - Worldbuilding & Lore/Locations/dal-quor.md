# Dal Quor

The Region of Dreams. Severed from planar orbit by giants.

## The Severance

The giants permanently removed Dal Quor from its planar orbit. This establishes the precedent that mortals can permanently alter planar structure at extreme cost.

## The Convergence

If Dal Quor becomes coterminous during the Convergence, the current quori go extinct. This is why the Dreaming Dark opposes the Convergence.

## Dreaming Dark's Solution

The Dreaming Dark's preferred solution is permanent severance of ALL planes — not just Dal Quor — to prevent the Convergence. This would be catastrophic for everyone else.
