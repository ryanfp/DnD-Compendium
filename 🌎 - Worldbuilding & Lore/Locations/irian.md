# Irian

The Eternal Day. Plane of positive energy and creation. Source of the Irian binding.

## Properties

- Raw creation energy from Irian doesn't restore from a template; it grows new material.
- The thinnest boundary between Irian and Eberron occurs at dawn. Sunrise is literally Irian touching the world.
- Irian is the paired opposite of Mabar.

## Campaign Role

When Anchors are restored and planar boundaries stabilize, Irian returns to its natural orbital cycle — sometimes close, sometimes remote. This is why the stain eventually wins.
