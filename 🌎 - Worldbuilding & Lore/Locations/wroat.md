# Wroat

Capital of Breland. Starting city for session 1.

## Atmosphere

Noir aesthetic: rain, gray, bureaucratic, post-war malaise.

## Session 1

The session 0 NPC was based in Wroat. The funeral takes place here — late afternoon, rain, a small rundown cemetery in Wroat's lower wards. Gray stone, iron fences, puddles on cracked paths.

The post-funeral gathering point is a smoky jazz club and bar that the NPC owned.
