# House Lyrandar

Mark of Storm. Airship and sea travel.

## Session 1 — The Extraction

A Lyrandar wind skiff — a small, fast, open-decked airship — serves as the Emerald Claw extraction vehicle during the rooftop sequence on the Lightning Rail. It pulls alongside the moving train and drops a rope ladder for the operatives to escape.
