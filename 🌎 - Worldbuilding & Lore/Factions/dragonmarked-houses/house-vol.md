# House Vol (Destroyed)

Bore the Mark of Death. Destroyed by dragon-elf genocide.

## History

House Vol bore the Mark of Death. The Mark of Death appearing on the Vol bloodline was a Draconic Prophecy event alarming enough to trigger a joint dragon-elf genocide that destroyed the house entirely.

Dragonmarks are expressions of Prophecy conditions being met — not predictions.

## Erandis Vol

Erandis Vol is the sole survivor of House Vol. She is a lich bearing the aberrant Mark of Death.
