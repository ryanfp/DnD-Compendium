# House Tharashk

Mark of Finding. Bounty hunters and prospectors.

## Open Questions

Grovyle was originally conceived as "Vex Tharashk" (half-orc), which would have connected him to House Tharashk. His race has since been changed to Shadar-kai, so the House Tharashk connection no longer applies. "Grovyle" is his current name; his in-world cover identity (which replaced "Vex Tharashk") is an open question.
