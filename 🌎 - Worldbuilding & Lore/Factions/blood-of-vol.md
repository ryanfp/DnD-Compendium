# The Blood of Vol (Organization)

Religious organization built around the Blood of Vol faith.

## Overview

The Blood of Vol organization is built around the Blood of Vol faith. It is distinct from the Emerald Claw, which is the militant arm, and from Erandis Vol's personal agenda. The faith and the militant organization are not the same thing.
