# The Gatekeepers

Ancient druidic order that maintains seals against extraplanar threats.

## Overview

The Gatekeepers are an ancient druidic order responsible for maintaining seals against extraplanar threats. The order is nearly extinct.

## Seals

Gatekeeper seals are epic druidic workings that require life force. They are metaphysical, not merely physical. The original druids gave their lives to create them. The seals degrade as their fuel runs out.

## Knowledge

The Gatekeepers know about the Chamber in a general, ancient-tradition sense.

## Campaign Connection

The research assistant's copied notes from session 1 reference Gatekeeper sites.
