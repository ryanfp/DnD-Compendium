# The Dreaming Dark

Quori organization operating through the Inspired in Riedra.

## Overview

The Dreaming Dark is a quori organization that operates through the Inspired in Riedra. They are unknown to the average person. The Kalashtar know of them. The Inspired are known publicly as foreign diplomats, but no one connects them to dream-parasites.

## Goal

The Dreaming Dark wants to prevent the Convergence — the event in which Dal Quor becomes coterminous with Eberron, which would cause the extinction of the current quori.

**Preferred solution:** Permanent severance of ALL planes, not just Dal Quor. This is catastrophic for everyone else: the Silver Flame loses connections, druidic magic weakens, and the Kalashtar are doomed.

## Relationship to Erandis Vol

The Dreaming Dark cannot ally with or be exploited by Erandis directly — she is undead, does not dream, and the two factions are mutually opaque to each other's tools.

However, Erandis can manipulate them indirectly by making the Convergence look inevitable, forcing desperate visible actions that destabilize Khorvaire further. Their panicked self-preservation serves her chaos even though they are working against her.
