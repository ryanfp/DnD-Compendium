# The Blood of Vol (Faith)

Religion teaching that divinity resides in mortal blood.

## Overview

The Blood of Vol faith teaches that divinity resides in mortal blood. Core teaching: *"We help the living, not worship the dead."*

The faith is distinct from the Emerald Claw organization and from Erandis Vol's personal agenda. The faith itself is sympathetic. The militant arm and the lich exploiting it are the corruption.

## Session 1 — Priest Quote

*"We teach that divinity lives in mortal blood. We help the living."*
