>[!statblock]+ Mareanie
> I've had this idea for a Poison/Ice regional form for Mareanie and its new evolution.
>                
>**Dex Entry:** "*Like their Alolan counterparts, this Pokemon will feed on Corsola if given the opportunity. However, Mareanie in this region seem to prefer the taste of land-dwelling Pokemon who wander too close to bodies of water and drown. Once the unlucky prey falls in, Mareanie freezes the water so that its hapless meal cannot escape. It takes its time munching on the still conscious prey's frozen body parts.*"
>
>The new regional form of Mareanie evolves into the also **Poison/Ice-type** **Pinnacold** (pinnacle + cold, like how Toxapex is toxic + apex), the Vicious Star Pokemon.
>
>Dex Entry: "*This bloodthirsty Pokemon's hunger for land-dwelling Pokemon has brought it ashore, actively pursuing anything from smaller Pokemon to whatever it can freeze solid or subdue with its potent venom. Attacks on humans, including small children, are rare, but have been reported.*"
>
>In addition to the Water-type and Poison-type moves of its Alolan counterparts, the new regional form of Mareanie and its evolved form Pinnacold learn Ice-type moves like Icicle Spear, Ice Shard, Freeze-Dry, Frost Breath, and Ice Beam.
>
>Pinnacold shares the move Baneful Bunker with Toxapex, but also gains a new signature move: Cold Shoulder - the Ice-type counterpart to Baneful Bunker, inflicting the frostbite status on attackers who make contact (assuming the frostbite status is carried into the mainline games, which I hope it does).
>
>Having adapted to hunting prey on land, Pinnacold's stats differ from those of Toxapex's. Pinnacold has the same base HP as Toxapex, but has significantly higher base Attack and Special Attack, slightly lower base Defense and Special Defense, and significantly higher base Speed. This is reflected in its drastically different appearance. Pinnacold has thinner, more lightweight arms than Toxapex, allowing it to move quickly and corner prey. The thorns on Pinnacold's arms are also sharper, allowing it to inflict as much pain as possible to both its attackers and victims.
>
>Pinnacold is essentially the opposite archetype of the defensive, slow Toxapex. It's more offense-oriented, and much, much faster. It goes from being brutal and merciless to outright vicious and malicious.
>
>Plus, we need more fast attacker Ice-types. Pokemon like Weavile show how viable and even lethal the Ice-type can be when given the proper tools to exploit the strengths of their typing, instead of being designed as ungodly slow tanks who can't even serve their intended role right because they get shafted by the frailty of their own typing.
