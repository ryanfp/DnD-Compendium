# Regional Variants

## Good Inspo


## Design Philosophy

*choose some, not all:*

- should change at least ½ types
- should alter stats or how the pokemon plays
- should alter role
- should alter color scheme
- should alter habitat

## Unused Type Combos

*normal + [type] will not be counted*

- fire/fairy (Galarian Ponyta line in ROMhacks)
 - ice/poison (Etrian Sneasal in *Pokemon Odyssey*)
- ground/fairy (Seviian Feebas in *Radical Red*)
- bug/dragon (Popular Flygon ROMhack typing)
- rock/ghost (Seviian Noibat in *Radical Red*)
