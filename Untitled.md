# Campaign Primer
(the pre-preseason stuff)

## What I'm Planning

### Meta Details

**Frequency:** weekly, 3-hour sessions. *If you expect to be too busy or unable to commit to, this is something to consider.*
**Length:** *Ideally*, levels 1-20. But, I'm flexible and will always accommodate someone leaving for any reason, and potentially filling the spot with new players if one opens up. Shooing for the high levels tho. *If you prefer only low levels, or only high levels, or generally don’t have interest in long-term progression, this is something to consider.*
**Players:** 3-4, if **exactly** 5 players are interested, I will do a 5-person party. 5 max though. I wish I could do 3 versions of the same campaign but I'm only one person. I'll be running and helping set up other games hopefully so we can all get a chance to play. No FOMO allowed!! *If you prefer games with low or high playercount (2-3, or 6-7, respectively), this is something to consider.*

### Story/World

**Fantasy:** Teeters between **Noblebright/Nobledark**, think of a world that combines 1920s post-WWI era America, with Cold War era politics, Arcanepunk and FlintlockFantasy (but *Magitech*, oooooh). The world is as it is, because it is *desperately* in need of heroes. Who will answer the call? *If you prefer Grimdark or Heroic/Epic Fantasy worlds, this is something to consider.*

Trust and who deserves it. Institutions that may not be what they claim. What it means to sacrifice something you can't get back. The campaign asks whether fixing a broken world is worth what it takes from the people doing the fixing.
**Tone:** Complex. Some aspects will be very morally-gray and require hard choices with no clear good or bad. Some will be very "Heroic-Fantasy"-esque, with a clear villain and clear heroes. The story and world will react with the events of the story/influence from the party. There may be lighthearted moments, periods of peace and grandeur, or gritty, hopeless expeditions into the unknown for the greater good. Will explore maybe some touchy topics and ideologies, depending on how the story goes. *If you prefer cut-and-dry themes, or little nuance, this is something to consider.
*
 Investigation and mystery first, action second. The world reacts to what you do and what you fail to do. It starts street-level and escalates to continent-scale. Expect things to get darker as the stakes rise — not grimdark, but consequences land and victories cost something.


## What I Expect
**Reasonable Commitment & Consideration:** shit comes up, people have lives, this is a hobby, I get it. I'm aiming to make this something that people *make* time for. Not a "sorry we got something going on this week I can't make session", but a "sorry I can't do that thing this week we have session that night", ya know? Obvi there's going to be more important things that come up, always. But please let us know. I want to try and treat this more seriously to make this a much better experience in more ways than one. If you might be unable to commit to a weekly session, and consistently make it, that would be something to think about.
