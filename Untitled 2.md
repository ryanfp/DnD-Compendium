## __**Game/Campaign Questions**__

### What ruleset are you running for your games?

I will be running a *heavily* modified version of the 5e ruleset, drawing from numerous sources of inspiration. The core chassis is definitely still 5e, but many features have been added or changed to add more depth, immersion and grit. Some sources I've considered (no matter how big or small) are:

- 5.5e or 2024 edition
- Pf2e (Pathfinder 2nd Edition)
- A5e (Advanced 5th Edition)
- Draw Steel
- Daggerheart
- Popular homebrews from
  - LaserLlama's Alternate Classes
  - KibblesTasty
  - MCDM
  - Sterling Vermin Adventure Co.
  - Loot Tavern
  - Mage Hand Press (previously Middle Finger of Vecna)
  - somanyrobots

### What sources are allowed/not allowed in games?

Luckily, all content that I use/allow will be on the [5e.tools website](5etools.overthemoondnd.com). If it's on the website, it's allowed 100%. If you think you've discovered an interaction that wasn't considered and it's too powerful, game-breaking, etc. please go to <#1475334869805432975> or <#1474856221717565613>. If you see something that you would like to be added, please go to <#1475334869805432975> and make a post. 

### How do I get into an active campaign? (How can I play?)

Currently, as of 3/5/2026, I am not running a campaign. I hope to soon be able to run 1-2 full campaigns and 1 Westmarches style game regularly. I also plan to host one-shots to hopefully get those not included in some other games some table time. I would *love* if everyone was able to play, and strongly encourage anybody who is considering DMing, to try it. I will do anything I can to help and support you in that journey, and plan to host some GM workshops to facilitate that in the future. 

### What time zone is primarily used?

Most of the server is in EST timezone, with a few in PST. Any time referenced by me will be in EST.

### What are the extra roles for?/Are players assigned roles?

Yes! They are. The extra roles will be designated per campaign/game and their roles are as follows:

💰 Treasurer: Keeps track of the party inventory and money
📚 Scribe: Writes session recaps
🌎 Cartographer: Keeps track of the parties quests and locations or map-handouts
📅 Planner: (Depending on the game) Schedules sessions and reminds players of upcoming sessions + confirms attendance

One will be given to each player, per campaign/game. This will help me by taking some bookkeeping weight off my shoulders and also keep every player engaged with the process at some level.

### Something came up before the session but after I RSVP'ed, what do I do?

Please let me know as soon as possible. If it’s the Campaign, I generally try to run if only 1 person can’t attend, but if half or more than half of the party will be absent sessions will be cancelled/rescheduled.
