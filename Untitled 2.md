## “Running The Game” & Goals

I’m generally aiming to run a campaign with 3-4 people. The only times I would run a 5-person campaign now is if 5 or 10 total people wanted to play. Otherwise, I’m doing my best to split to the 3-4. Unfortunately, I’m sure this means there will still be some people who are unable/unwilling to play, whether by availability, time slots, responsibilities or just what I will detail below. 

If I could do this as a full time job I would, but there’s only so much time. I would **LOVE** to encourage other people to DM and I would gladly spend a considerable amount of time explaining things and walking them through. In short, it’s not nearly as complicated as you may think. The goal is fun with your friends in a silly fantasy world, and anyone and everyone of us will appreciate those who take the time and effort to do so. 

That being said, to set some expectations, here’s a rundown of some stuff I can think of. It’ll be broken into parts for brevity but also bc of length. My goal is **NOT** to tell people they shouldn’t be interested in my games or that they would not fit in, moreso to give a better idea to those window-shopping. In the end, I’m sure I can organize some one-shots that are different to give everyone a chance to have some fun. Anyway:



### Mechanics
- I have been for the better part of 9 months or so, working on a massive project of basically:
  - finding and incorporating any homebrew I like that I feel is a benefit to the game without creating issues
  - taking what works from different systems and translating that into 5e
  - creating my own personal homebrew rules or slightly tweaking existing rules to better suit the goals of my table
  - "building" a website (forking an existing one, and making lots of changes) with all of this incorporated
  - editing the website and adding features for better UX

culminating in what is basically a 5.1e or 5.75e (or whatever you wanna call it). A heavily modified version of the game that still uses base D&D as its core. 

Some of the errata includes:

- a total of ~30 NEW Classes, such as the:
  - Alchemist
  - Bender (yes, like Avatar)
  - Psion
  - Jaeger
  - Summoner
  - Warlord and the Gunslinger
- Anywhere from 20-35 new subclasses for each of the base 13 classes
- New spells, weapons, feats, backgrounds, races, monsters/enemies, and such
- Large scale rebalance of the game like
  - Reworking certain spells that were outliers in either direction or outright removing some egregious examples
  - Reworking the base classes ranging from a small QOL change or buff to a heavy framework shift in some cases
  - Rebalancing certain conditions and effects, how equipment interacts with the player, resting mechanics
  - Adding new skills and buffing weaker ones, changing the Saving Throw system
  - Adding exploits/maneuvers for martials to rival the versatility and experience of spells for spellcasters
  - Adding more social/exploration options to character progression

And some more that I’m sure I’m forgetting. If this makes absolutely no sense to you, great! You don’t have to worry about anything, as the rest of the game will seem like mumbo jumbo too lmao. Once I get everything finished, some features may be seamless or as if they were apart of the base game, whereas others will take a very minor explanation once you have context. That being said:

**IF YOU HAVE NO DESIRE TO PLAY WITH HOMEBREW, AND FAVOR A HEAVY RULES AS WRITTEN (RAW) APPROACH, YOU WILL NOT ENJOY MY GAMES**. If your concern is balance, I can assure you that’s a top priority, and whatever can’t be ironed out will be fixed on the other end with stronger monsters and better mechanics. 

### Style

*GENERALLY*, I run games that focus more on the **Social** and **Combat** pillars of D&D (I’m trying to incorporate **Exploration** better, but we’ll see how that goes). I enjoy the grittiness and crunch of combat and tactics, so I will be doing my best to design encounters that reflect that. I’m hoping to be able to create meaningful choices both in and out of combat, where you’ll have to manage your resources and work together to overcome the obstacle. Obviously, this won’t be all the time or even *most* of the time, but that is what' I’ll be striving for. Which is to say:

**IF YOU DO NOT ENJOY COMBAT OR GRITTINESS OR MANAGING HEALING/COMBAT RESOURCES AT ALL, OR BUILDCRAFTING, YOU WILL NOT ENJOY MY GAMES**. If you are looking for a low-stakes, social-only game with little to no combat encounters, **you will not have a good time.** 

### Tone/Theme

The campaign setting I plan to use as the basis for the campaign and any game I foresee myself running in the future is *Eberron* (if you’re familiar with it). It’s a high-magic setting that meshes a ton of genres such as:

- High-Fantasy
- Neo-Noir Thriller
- Pulp Adventure
- Swashbuckling Action
- Political Intrigue
- Post-War Society
- Sword and Sorcery
- Eldritch Horror
- Magitech/Arcanepunk/Spellpunk/Dungeonpunk (however you wanna flavor it, you get the idea)

Some popular media that were heavy influences:

- Indiana Jones
- Lord of the Rings
- Blade Runner
- Full Metal Alchemist: Brotherhood
- Cowboy Bebop
- Pirates of the Caribbean
- Van Helsing
- Tomb Raider
- Game of Thrones
- The Maltese Falcon
- Casablanca
- Howl’s Moving Castle
- Treasure Planet

Basically, think Legend of Korra *New Republic City* level tech with ancient Draconic Prophesies, huge corporate conglomerate crime families, a fractured world following a devastating war, with creationist moral dilemmas and the like. 

**IF YOU ARE NOT INTERESTED IN ANY OF THE THEMES OR MEDIA INFLUENCES LISTED ABOVE, YOU WILL NOT ENJOY MY GAMES.** That’s not to say that other genres won’t be explored, or that it will *always* be the above, but that is the general idea going in. 

### Medium

Lastly, I run these games online, using the Foundry VTT. Unfortunately, we don’t live close enough to everyone to let us play in person, but I think Foundry is the next best thing. I run the games in Foundry, we join a Discord call and I stream the sessions (unlisted) for playback if desired. It’s able to run on some potatoes, so I wouldn’t worry about performance. 

## Going Forward
