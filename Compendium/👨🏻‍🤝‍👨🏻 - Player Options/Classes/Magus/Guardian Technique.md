## Class Features

### Guardian Technique (Level 3)

Specializing in defending their allies, spell blades following this technique make broad use of abjuration and self-enhancing magics, often specializing more toward their physical skills. They place themselves at the front of combat, but are a highly mobile line of defense.

### Guardian Spells (Level 3)

You learn the following spells at the following levels. The following are Spellblade spells for you, but do not count against your spells known.

### Guardian Proficiency (Level 3)

Starting at 3rd level when you select this archetype, you gain proficiency in heavy armor and shields.

Additionally, you can use a melee weapon as your arcane focus, and when you do so you can perform somatic components with the hand holding your arcane focus (even for spells without a material component).

### Projected Barrier (Level 3)

Starting at 3rd level, you gain a new option for your [Arcane Surge](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/spellblade-kt-cll.md#Arcane%20Surge%20(Level%201)) feature:

- As a reaction to an allied creature within 30 feet being hit by an attack or targeted by the magic missile spell, you expend a use of Arcane Surge to cast shield without expending a spell slot, granting that creature the AC bonus instead of yourself.  

### Fortifying Surge (Level 7)

At 7th level, when you expend a use of [Arcane Surge](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/spellblade-kt-cll.md#Arcane%20Surge%20(Level%201)), you gain temporary hit points equal to `d8` + your Intelligence modifier.

### Swapping Surge (Level 15)

Starting at 15th level, when you use your [Arcane Surge](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/spellblade-kt-cll.md#Arcane%20Surge%20(Level%201)) to teleport, you can instead swap positions with a willing creature within range, teleporting to their space and teleporting them to your space. When you do so, both you and them gain the temporary hit points from [Fortifying Surge](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/spellblade-guardian-technique-kt-cll.md#Fortifying%20Surge%20(Level%207)).

### Resilient Aegis (Level 20)

At 20th level, you can fortify yourself with magic for 1 minute (no action required). For the duration, you have resistance to all damage. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a long rest.
