## Class Features

### Swiftblade Technique (Level 3)

The quickest and most deadly of the Spellblades, Swiftblades specialize in mobility, striking from the shadows and withdrawing as quickly as they appeared, favoring lighter weapons and flurries of blows that leave their target dead before it can retaliate.

### Swiftblade Spells (Level 3)

You learn the following spells at the following levels. The following are Spellblade spells for you, but do not count against your spells known.

**Swiftblade Spells**

| Spellblade Level | Spells Learned |
|------------------|----------------|
| 3rd | inflict wounds |
| 5th | [poison dart](/3-Content/Compendiums/spells/poison-dart-kt-cll.md) |
| 9th | [spider bite](/3-Content/Compendiums/spells/spider-bite-kt-cll.md) |
| 13th | blight |

### Fighting Style (Level 3)

Starting at 3rd level when you gain this archetype, you gain the Two Weapon Fighting Style.

#### Two-Weapon Fighting

When you engage in two-weapon fighting, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of the second attack.

### Swift Casting (Level 3)

Starting at 3rd level you can use [Spellstrike](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/spellblade-kt-cll.md#Spellstrike%20(Level%202)) as part of the Attack action before making a melee weapon attack. You cannot Spellstrike more than once per turn.

Additionally, you can use a melee weapon as your arcane focus, and when you do so you can perform somatic components with the hand holding your arcane focus (even for spells without a material component).

### Rapid Surge (Level 3)

Starting at 3rd level, you gain a new option for your [Arcane Surge](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/spellblade-kt-cll.md#Arcane%20Surge%20(Level%201)) feature:

- You can expend a use of Arcane Surge to make a single weapon attack (no action required).  

### Relentless Spellstrike (Level 7)

At 7th level, if you miss an attack empowered by a [Spellstrike](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/spellblade-kt-cll.md#Spellstrike%20(Level%202)), you can choose to not trigger the effect. When you do so, your next attack before the end of your turn will trigger it instead.

### Vanishing Surge (Level 15)

Starting at 15th level, when you expend an [Arcane Surge](/3-Content/Compendiums/classes/spellblade-kt-cll.md#Arcane%20Surge%20(Level%201)), you become invisible until the start of your next turn.

### Infinite Strikes (Level 20)

At 20th level, you draw on your magic to hasten yourself to blinding speeds (no action required). For one minute, any time you make a weapon attack with an action or bonus action, you can immediately make another attack with the same weapon.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a long rest.
